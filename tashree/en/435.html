An author and researcher specialized in Islamic philosophy, theory of knowledge, and political legitimacy, he is concerned with rooting Islamic thought on a rigorous critical methodology that combines the proof of reason and the guidance of revelation. He works on reshaping Islamic concepts in the face of contemporary deviations, whether resulting from the infiltration of Western thought or related to the disintegration of Islamic consciousness. He has authored works that focus on establishing belief on rational and scientific foundations, rooting the methodology of reasoning, exploring divine laws, and both cosmic and historical, and clarifying the aspects of miraculousness in Islamic legislation.
