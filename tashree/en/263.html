﴾And fear Allah and know that Allah is with the righteous﴿ ﴾البقرة 194﴿, and a door to open the gates of the heavens and the earth with blessings ﴾And if the people of the towns had believed and been conscious of Allah, We would have opened to them blessings from the heavens and the earth﴿ ﴾الأعراف 96﴿, and other glad tidings.(399)

And since human nature may drive a person to succumb to desires or to fall short in fulfilling acts of obedience at times, the Wise Legislator has aided him by reminding him of vigilance and companionship so that he may return to the straight path, or so that it may help him perfect his work. This is surrounded by doctrinal ties (the awareness of Allah's companionship and vigilance), and it is accompanied by worldly and otherworldly incentives (the opening of blessings, knowledge, sustenance, and relief from distress), until the concept of piety (taqwa) becomes ingrained in souls, and it becomes a companion to every action performed by the Muslim. The Muslim surrounds his life in every moment with the awareness of Allah's companionship and vigilance, within Islam's good application of the rulings of its Sharia in both secrecy and openly, driven by an internal motivation!
