The details of this, God willing, will come shortly in a separate chapter that emphasizes it and connects it to the system that encompasses all these systems and rulings under its wings, namely the system of the caliphate, which succeeded prophethood in its methodology, responsibilities, and rulings. The caliphate is the extension and continuation of the prophetic system in governance. The verses and hadiths, which are too numerous to count, provide us with the definitive obligation of the system that applies and implements them, which is the Islamic state.
Just as Allah, Exalted is He, commanded His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) to rule by what was revealed, He has commanded us, until the Day of Resurrection, with the same directive. Thus, Allah's address to His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) is an address to his nation to rule by what Allah has revealed. The rulings that Allah has revealed, such as those related to the economic system, social system, system of governance, judiciary, and punishments, were revealed so that people may refer to them. Allah, Exalted is He, has mandated that people refer to them until the Day of Resurrection. Ruling by what Allah has revealed can only be done in accordance with what has been legislated through the organizations of the Islamic caliphate, and He has not left people aimlessly without guidance to show them how to rule by what Allah has revealed!
