Fifth: The necessity of obligation and the higher reference
There must be a higher authority with the power to impose legislation and enforce it (either through personal conviction or executive authority).
Divine legislation imparts a sense of self-obligation to the legislation: because the individual believes that he is tasked with a divine command that is not open to debate.
Modern societies, even if they claim freedom, impose strict laws on people regarding taxes, trade, banking, identity, and security... which shows that obligation is a societal necessity.
The second axis: Legislation must be correct, as errors in it lead to grave evils and widespread harms:
Legislation, in its essence, and by virtue of its nature and function, is not a circumstantial legal activity, nor a bundle of social directives or viewpoints that can be experimented with or modified according to political, cultural, or historical circumstances; rather, it is a comprehensive, integrated system intended to regulate human behavior, define one's stance towards his actions, organize his relationships with other individuals, and with state and societal institutions, and to organize the various public and private systems of life. Based on this, affairs will be managed, public interests will be defined, and societal problems will be resolved. Therefore, legislation transcends being temporary or customary laws to become a governing discourse on actions, relationships, and systems, establishing obligations, defining rights, regulating duties, organizing the management of affairs, and establishing order, justice, security, and mercy.
Legislation is not a technical matter whose errors can be compensated for later; it should not be a field for human experimentation, nor should it be entrusted to a relative and variable source like human reason. Rather, it must emanate from an absolute, knowledgeable, and just authority, which is the Creator, Exalted is He, as it is a vital process that touches the lives and destinies of people, and errors in it lead to grave evils and widespread harms:
