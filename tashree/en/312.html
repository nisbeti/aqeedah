It means: the supreme sovereignty belongs to Allah in managing this entire universe, establishing His laws and overseeing its affairs, and executing His command; for Allah rules, and there is no one to overturn His judgment.

It means: that Allah, the Owner of the Day of Judgment, is the one who will separate between people in what they used to differ about and will judge among them, and your Lord does not do injustice to anyone.

It means: that Allah is the one who legislates for His servants, and He is the one who clarifies to them - Exalted is He - the right from the wrong, the truth from falsehood, good from evil, beauty from ugliness, and faith from disbelief; there is no judgment except for Him, Exalted is He (461).

He revealed the legislation in the Book and the Sunnah, which He inspired to His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him),

and He considered anything else from the rulings as tyranny (taghut) and ignorance (jahiliyyah), ﴾Do they seek the judgment of ignorance? And who is better in judgment than Allah for a people who are certain?﴿ [al-Ma'idah 50], ﴾They want to refer to the taghut while they were commanded to disbelieve in it﴿ [an-Nisa 60].

And seeking judgment based on desires is something He has forbidden and strongly warned against! ﴾Then We placed you upon a clear legislation, so follow it and do not follow the desires of those who do not know﴿ [al-Jathiya 18]. ﴾And judge between them by what Allah has revealed, and do not follow their desires, and beware lest they turn you away from some of what Allah has revealed to you; and if they turn away, then know that Allah only intends to afflict them with some of their sins, and indeed, many of the people are defiantly disobedient﴿ [al-Ma'idah 49], ﴾O David, indeed We have made you a successor upon the earth, so judge between the people in truth and do not follow desire, lest it lead you astray from the way of Allah; indeed, those who go astray from the way of Allah will have a severe punishment for having forgotten the Day of Reckoning﴿ [Sad 26].

Say, "Allah guides to the truth. So is he who guides to the truth more worthy to be followed or he who does not guide unless he is guided? Then what is wrong with you? How do you judge?"﴿ [Yunus 35], ﴾O David, indeed We have made you a successor upon the earth, so judge between the people in truth and do not follow desire, lest it lead you astray from the way of Allah; indeed, those who go astray from the way of Allah will have a severe punishment for having forgotten the Day of Reckoning﴿ [Sad 26], ﴾So judge between them by what Allah has revealed, and do not follow their desires away from what has come to you of the truth﴿ [al-Ma'idah 48]. ﴾And Allah judges in truth, and those whom they invoke besides Him do not judge at all; indeed, Allah is the Hearing, the Seeing﴿ [Ghafir 20], and in the last verse, it becomes clear that establishing justice and truth can only be achieved by implementing the Sharia of Allah; justice and fairness cannot be established with rulings that the Qur'an has described as ignorance and tyranny!

And it rectifies the livelihood of people, what is good for them in their worldly life and their Hereafter ﴾Indeed, mankind is in loss, except for those who have believed and done righteous deeds and advised one another to truth and advised one another to patience﴿ [al-Asr 2-3].

And it rectifies their deeds, ﴾He will amend for you your deeds and forgive you your sins; and whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has certainly attained a great attainment﴿ [al-Ahzab 71].
