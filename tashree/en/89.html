Ijtihad and Taqlid:

"Allah, Exalted is He, addressed all people with the message of our master Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him). Allah, Exalted is He, said: ﴾And We have not sent you except as a giver of good tidings and a warner to all mankind, but most of the people do not know﴿ [34 Saba]. And He said: ﴾Say, O mankind, indeed I am the Messenger of Allah to you all, to whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. There is no deity except Him. He gives life and causes death. So believe in Allah and His Messenger, the unlettered Prophet who believes in Allah and His words, and follow him that you may be guided﴿ [7 Al-A'raf]. And He said: ﴾O mankind, there has come to you the Messenger with the truth from your Lord﴿ [4 An-Nisa]. He addressed His rulings to all people in general and to the believers, saying, Exalted is He: ﴾O mankind, fear your Lord. Indeed, the quaking of the Hour is a tremendous thing﴿. And Allah, Exalted is He, said in the Noble Revelation: ﴾And woe to the polytheists, who do not give zakah and of the Hereafter they are disbelievers﴿ [41 Fussilat]. ﴾O you who have believed, eat from the good things We have provided for you and be grateful to Allah if it is Him that you worship﴿ [2 Al-Baqarah]. And He said: ﴾O you who have believed, be persistently standing firm in justice, witnesses for Allah, even if it be against yourselves or parents and relatives. Whether one is rich or poor, Allah is more worthy of both. So follow not [personal] inclination, lest you not be just. And if you distort [your testimony] or refuse [to give it], then indeed Allah is Acquainted with what you do﴿ [5 Al-Ma'idah]. Thus, it is upon those who hear the address to understand it and believe in it, and upon those who believe in it to understand it and act upon it, for it is the legal ruling [that is, the obligation that will regulate human behavior and provide a ruling for their actions and the rules of their relationships that organize their lives and the life of society. It has been established for us with conclusive evidence that reason is not suitable as a judge to legislate rulings, laws, and regulations, and that it is necessary for man and society to have commands and prohibitions that clarify the rulings of their actions and relationships. It has also been established for us that Allah, Exalted is He, has revealed these commands and prohibitions as obligations to be acted upon, to govern the lives of people, and to prohibit taking rulings except through one exclusive and confirmed path, which is revelation: Allah, Exalted is He, said in the Noble Qur'an on the tongue of His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him): ﴾I only follow what is revealed to me, and I am only a clear warner﴿ [46 Al-Ahqaf]. And He said: ﴾And follow what is revealed to you from your Lord﴿ [33 Al-Ahzab]. And Allah, Exalted is He, restricted warning to revelation and emphasized this by saying: ﴾Say, I only warn you by revelation﴿ [21 Al-Anbiya]. This means that warning and following in speech, action, and relationships can only be based on revelation—exclusively and emphatically. Moreover, it has been established for us that the obligation to obey Allah and His Messenger in what He has legislated for us from the religion, to follow revelation, and to emulate the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), achieves the servitude of people to their Lord by taking Him as a legislator and a ruler whom they obey in what He commands and refrain from what He prohibits. Allah, Exalted is He, said: ﴾Indeed, the judgment belongs to none but Allah﴿ [6 Al-An'am, 12 Yusuf, 67 Yusuf]. And He said: ﴾And whatever you disagree over, its ruling is to Allah﴿ [42 Ash-Shura]. And the ruling in language means prohibition, and hence it is said that a judgment is a ruling because it prevents from what is not ruled upon, meaning that the existence of legislation in the form of a ruling necessitates preventing people from acting except according to what is legislated. Thus, only Allah has the right to prevent the ruled from acting except in accordance with His Sharia(186). Therefore, legislation establishes the meaning of servitude to Allah!

And it has been established for us that Allah, Exalted is He, has made obedience to His command—blessed and exalted is He—and obedience to His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) in what He has legislated from rulings obligatory. ﴾So let those beware who oppose the Messenger's order, lest calamity strike them or a painful punishment﴿ [24 An-Nur]. This indicates the obligation of obeying the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) in what he commands from the Qur'an and the Sunnah, and obedience means following, and it has established punishments for not adhering to it. The Qur'an has emphasized this framework to the extent that it has declared the invalidation of a person's actions if they are not within the framework of obedience to Allah and the Messenger; because obedience is a sign of submission (to the command) in language and reality, and the realization of submission and obedience, and their embodiment in reality can only be through the commanded following."
