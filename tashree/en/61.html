The reality of obligation, the pillars upon which the Islamic legislative system stands, which ensure the comprehensiveness and completeness of the religion and the vastness of the Sharia, and restrict the warning to revelation, and preserve the prophetic Sunnah, and affirm the certainty of the consensus of the Companions:

Islamic legislation is based on the concept of: legal ruling (hukm shar'i), relying on the concept of obligation (taklif), for a person is obligated and will be held accountable for adhering to the obligation in every aspect of his affairs. "Obligation is derived from the burden in the sense of activation, and its meaning is the imposition of what entails hardship in its performance, and it includes both obligation and prohibition, not according to what the nature desires, and what it recoils from"(117).

Before we ask: where does obligation begin and where does it end? We will present an important introduction that clarifies the reality of obligation, and the position of obligation in organizing the life of the individual, the community, and the state. We will focus the discussion around the idea of:

First: The reality of legislation (obligation),

Second: The preservation of legislation,

Third: The vastness of legislation, its stability, and its ability to solve human problems until the Day of Judgment,

Fourth: The methodology of dealing with its sources in a way that ensures the connection between reality and the legal ruling that regulates behavior or descends upon reality to judge it.

Here are the details:

Allah, Exalted is He, established the system of existence on justice and prohibited oppression, as in the sacred hadith narrated by Abu Dharr Jundub ibn Junadah, may Allah be pleased with him, from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) as he narrates from Allah, Blessed and Exalted, the Lord of Glory, that He said: "O My servants, I have forbidden oppression upon Myself and made it forbidden among you, so do not oppress one another." Allah has prohibited oppression upon Himself and prohibited it upon His servants and revealed the Sharia, and the methodology that ensures that injustice does not enter the dominion of the heavens and the earth. He made man a vicegerent to establish a system that upholds justice and prevents oppression, ﴾Indeed, We sent Our messengers with clear signs and sent down with them the Scripture and the balance that people may maintain [justice]﴿ [25 Al-Hadid], and He made man a vicegerent, and He approved for him to reform the earth and not to cause corruption therein, and He did not leave the organization of rights, the establishment of justice, and the prevention of oppression, and the regulation of behavior that leads to reform and not corruption, and the resolution of disputes and conflicts, without legislation and organization that regulates and organizes it, and establishes proof against creation, and holds them accountable based on it. He did not leave it to humans and their whims and the domination of the strong over the weak, and their confusion in knowing the correct organization that rectifies their conditions, establishes their system, and prevents their corruption!

The angels questioned Allah, Exalted is He, about His vicegerency of man on earth: ﴾Indeed, I am making upon the earth a vicegerent﴿, ﴾They said, "Will You place therein one who causes corruption therein and sheds blood?"﴿ as a vicegerent? The Truth, Exalted is He, replied: ﴾He said, "Indeed, I know that which you do not know."﴿ There is no meaning for the vicegerent to be a metaphor for man doing as he pleases; if that were the case, their objection would be realized: ﴾They said, "Will You place therein one who causes corruption therein and sheds blood?"﴿ However, since the Lord of Glory answered them saying: ﴾Indeed, I know that which you do not know.﴿ it implies that He did not make him a vicegerent for shedding blood and causing corruption.
