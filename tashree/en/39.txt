Thus, awareness of these truths is one of the most critical matters for the Islamic Ummah as it seeks its path to revival, and it has made significant strides without being able to stand firmly upon it!
Allah, Exalted is He, says: ﴾And [remember] when Allah promised you one of the two groups that it would be yours, and you wished that the one without shrapnel would be yours. But Allah intended to establish the truth by His words and to eliminate the disbelievers. To establish the truth and abolish falsehood, even if the criminals disliked it.﴿ [al-Anfal 7-8]. As you can see clearly, there is a complete distinction between truth and falsehood. How can the truth be established and falsehood be abolished if it were not for its distinction from it and its complete separation from it? ﴾Rather, We dash the truth against falsehood, and it destroys it; and thereupon it departs. And woe to you for what you describe.﴿ [al-Anbiya 18]. The victory of truth over falsehood can only be due to the immense inherent strength within the truth. When it collides with falsehood, it obliterates it, and thus it departs. For this reason, it must be pure, crystallized, emanating from revelation, and firmly rooted in its divine origin!
