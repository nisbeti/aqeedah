And may He forgive, by His grace, our masters the scholars, and those from whom we have taken this great religion, and may He not place in our hearts any malice towards those who have believed. Indeed, we love the Muslims, we wish them well, and we desire for them to unite upon that which Allah has united the hearts of those upon. If the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) had spent all that is on earth, he would not have united their hearts, but Allah united them. O Allah, unite the hearts of the Muslims and gather them upon Your love, obedience, and the good faith in You, and upon righteous deeds solely for Your noble face.

After this, this is what we have strived for in this difficult and intricate matter, and it is the effort of one who is lacking. A Muslim has the right to advise another Muslim, so whoever wishes to address me and fulfill the duty of advising me regarding an unintentional mistake, or a weak opinion, or to inquire about something of that nature, should write to me.

Written by: the one in need of the mercy of his Lord, Exalted is He

Abu Malik, Thaer Ahmad Salama
