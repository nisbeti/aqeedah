Sixth: It did not address problems as economic or social issues, but rather as issues related to the human being:
It did not address problems as economic or social issues, but rather as issues related to this human being, as the perspective on problems from these angles is subject to many influences that can cause them to shift from one extreme to another. For example, capitalism found itself every so often (like in the 1930s) caught in a bitter cycle: rising unemployment leads to decreased demand, decreased demand leads to a collapse in commercial investments, the collapse of investments leads to rising unemployment, and so on. This is an economic problem, and various theories have emerged among economic thinkers to solve the problem of unemployment; should the state intervene by injecting funds and spending on the private sector, or should it allow the invisible hand of the market to dominate? Thus, Keynesian theory was tried and failed, and the economic recession continued until the onset of World War II, while other theories also failed to prevent the recurring cycles of recession from ravaging the economy. However, Islam addressed poverty related to the individual, that is, each individual in society specifically, to enrich him, and did not view the problem of national poverty as other systems like capitalism did. The remedy in capitalism was through increasing production, while Islam addressed the problem by legislating how wealth is owned and how wealth is distributed among all individuals one by one, and by precisely defining the responsibilities of the individual and the state, so that every individual can fully satisfy all of his basic needs and be assisted in fulfilling his luxury needs. Therefore, in the West, the indicator that signifies the realization of happiness or misery, prosperity or hardship, the resolution of economic problems or their strangulation is what is called GDP: Gross Domestic Product. For example, the GDP of America is 22 trillion, and regardless of the fact that those who own this wealth are a minority of no more than 1% of the population owning 90% of the wealth, and that the rates of poverty, unemployment, and homelessness in society reach astronomical figures, when the GDP rate declines, they say we have entered a slowdown, and when the Dow Jones index rises to increase the wealth of speculators in the financial markets, it is a sign of economic recovery, while the unemployment rate remains unchanged, or they create temporary jobs that barely make ends meet. On April 14, 2020, when the coronavirus crisis was ravaging the American economy, a picture appeared on the business television channel "BNN" with a green background saying: "This week marks the best performance of the Dow Jones index since 1938," while in front of it, another red background said: "Breaking news: 16 million Americans lost their jobs in the past three weeks!"
In contrast, Islam addresses the poverty of the individual and the poverty of the collective, "There is no community that goes to bed with a hungry person among them except that Allah's protection is lifted from them!" Among the examples of solving problems is the social solidarity system in Islam, which we will discuss in detail in a separate chapter, God willing, and so on.
