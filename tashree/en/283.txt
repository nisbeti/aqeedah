A professor, Wisam Fouad, said: "Indeed, Allah has formulated these obligations in their legal form to ensure their legislative adherence, but He has addressed their psychological acceptance among the Muslim community in a way that made their implementation more important to them than the acts of worship themselves. Allah has made the shortcoming in the rights of people over one another more difficult to forgive than the shortcomings of the servants in their rights to Allah, Exalted is He, except for associating partners with Him. The hadiths indicating this meaning are numerous; from Abdullah ibn Abu Qatada, from his father, who heard him narrate from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), that he stood among them and mentioned to them, 'That jihad in the way of Allah and faith in Allah are the best of deeds.' A man stood up and said: 'O Messenger of Allah, what if I am killed in the way of Allah, will my sins be forgiven?' The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) replied: 'Yes, if you are killed in the way of Allah, while you are patient, seeking reward, advancing and not retreating.' Then the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: 'What did you say?' He said: 'What if I am killed in the way of Allah, will my sins be forgiven?' The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: 'Yes, while you are patient, seeking reward, advancing and not retreating, except for debt; for Gabriel (peace be upon him) told me that.' And from Abu Huraira (may Allah be pleased with him), he said: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: 'Whoever has a grievance against his brother regarding his honor or anything else, let him seek to resolve it today before there will be no dinar or dirham. If he has good deeds, they will be taken from him in proportion to his grievance, and if he has no good deeds, he will be taken from the sins of his companion...' (Narrated by al-Bukhari)"(423).
While Islam has established the idea of rights based on the premise that the source from which legal rights emerge is Islamic legislation, with its details and rulings, which is legislation that is not biased towards one group over another, nor governed by whims or narrow interests, nor by hasty perspectives that may prove erroneous over time, its source is the commands and prohibitions of Allah, Exalted is He, and His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him), which came through revelation and were established by legal evidence, to be adhered to out of faith and obedience and to attain Allah's pleasure, love, and closeness, even if they conflict with immediate desires or individual interests that a person values. They find that prioritizing the Sharia is the true interest and the benefit that has no corruption in it. The state safeguards these rights with its authority to establish justice and prevent oppression; thus, they are rights that are not surrounded by ambiguity, nor is there any confusion in them. Islam did not establish the concept of rights based on benefit or freedom, as Western civilization did, resulting in the state's role being to protect freedoms rather than manage affairs, which made rights mostly theoretical with no impact in reality, due to the impossibility of agreement on interest or benefit, or what achieves freedom when its realization conflicts among individuals in society, ultimately leading to the dominance of the strong and capable, and the tailoring of laws to accommodate their interests. Furthermore, the concept of benefit is a relative idea that changes with time and circumstances, and the idea of benefit changes according to the perspective of the dominant group in decision-making circles or those close to influencing them: such as pressure groups, social classes, or the dominant political elites... etc. This has made the idea of rights in the Western system a relative idea by extension; therefore, we should not be surprised by the atrocities committed by Western countries in the world during the colonial period and in wars that killed millions and devastated crops and progeny.
