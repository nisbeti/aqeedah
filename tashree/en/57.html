The Reality of Obligation, Its Limits, and Sources

When we study the characteristics of Islamic legislation and its components, we analyze and examine the legislation in terms of its subject, rulings, treatments, evidence, customs, and objectives, a scrutiny that penetrates the depths of the philosophy upon which it is based, including its principles, standards, foundations, evidence, and references, passing through its concepts and terminology and the systems that established its edifice, and touching upon the precise operational procedures(112) involved in ijtihad (independent reasoning), and taqlid (imitation), concluding with its wisdom, values, and customs that it seeks.

(112) The methodology can be defined as: "the correct arrangement of the mental processes we undertake in the pursuit of uncovering the truth and proving it." Islam has placed more emphasis on the methodology of research than on the results, as controlling the methodology ensures the sound progression of the thinking process and keeps it free from being subjected to whims and randomness, regulating it with the necessary standards and criteria for sound research. "If the perception of the truth as it is in reality is knowledge, then the methodology adopted to achieve that perception should undoubtedly also be knowledge." (The Great Universal Certainties, al-Bouti, p. 31.)

Ijtihad is a set of disciplined operational procedures that ensure that the mujtahid (the one exercising ijtihad) is not influenced by his whims and desires when studying the legal texts to extract the legal ruling from them, and it ensures that he proceeds according to regulated rules of thought. From this, we can say that the different methodologies of usul (principles of jurisprudence) among mujtahids in the various jurisprudential schools, such as the Hanafis, Shafi'is, Malikis, Hanbalis, and others, are organized methodologies for the disciplined operational procedures through which they study the various legal evidences related to the issue they are engaging in ijtihad, according to the fixed principles necessary for the process of deduction, the foundations of derivation, and according to the regulated rules for balancing and preference, employing the evidence, and defining terms and concepts. If you follow the methodology of ijtihad, your opinion is legally acceptable whether you arrive at the truth or miss it.

Taqlid is also a set of disciplined operational procedures that ensure that the muqallid (the one imitating) follows a recognized ijtihad according to rules of taqlid that prevent him from switching between schools and opinions in pursuit of whims, and it ensures that the opinion he imitates is consistent with the principles upon which it is based. It is not permissible for the muqallid to imitate mujtahids who differ in their principles on a specific issue, such as taking rulings related to prayer from al-Shafi'i and others related to prayer from Abu Hanifa! He cannot take from one certain nullifiers of wudu (ablution) and from the other different nullifiers, and this is not permissible. If the issues differ (for example, if he imitates a mujtahid in the rulings of prayer and another mujtahid from a different school in the rulings of zakat), it is permissible to take from a non-mujtahid. If you follow the methodology of taqlid, you will absolve yourself before Allah; otherwise, you will not! Taqlid is a set of disciplined operational procedures.

The judiciary involves disciplined operational procedures, and the study of beliefs is based on regulated methodologies in thinking and reasoning, detailing the types of evidence (rational and textual), their intellectual value in terms of certainty and conjecture, the aspect of evidence and its decisiveness, the necessity of their agreement with sensory perception and their foundation upon it, the prohibition of their contradiction with reason, their connection to proofs and evidence, their agreement with sound human nature, and their impact on hearts. Reasoning also follows disciplined operational procedures, whether it is reasoning on beliefs that involves adherence to regulated methodologies in thinking to issue a mental judgment arising from acquiring a conclusive conviction that leaves no room for doubt and is not subject to uncertainty, possessing a quality that necessitates a distinction that does not allow for contradiction, leading to certain, necessary, or impossible-to-dispute knowledge, through establishing evidence that matches reality with the rational judgment (thought) related to that reality. Its essence is establishing evidentiary proofs to demonstrate a specific issue, and in achieving a method of acquiring the evidence with the quality of certainty, the evidence establishes the connection between this understanding of reality and specific operations that build the evidence and connect it to strict rational rules that make it conclusive. As for the branches, reasoning involves investigating the indications and evidence that guide and direct towards the existence of the ruling on the specific issue therein. The methodology of interpretation also consists of disciplined operational procedures aimed at understanding the text in terms of language and meaning, and its harmony with its counterparts from texts and rulings. The methodology of hadith scholars in accepting and studying hadith involves disciplined operational procedures, while the study of language, its eloquence, and its sciences are regulated methodologies. Indeed, Muslims have invented a disciplined science for the principles of research and debate. Thus, the entirety of the Sharia is based on disciplined methodological operational procedures, and the rulings that emerge from it, such as judiciary, enjoining good, forbidding evil, buying and selling, marriage, allegiance, pilgrimage, and others, are operational procedures carried out according to a methodology to regulate transactions, relationships, and practical legal rulings. In summary, Islam has emphasized discipline in operational procedures to regulate the methodology of research and thinking. If you adhere to it, your result is acceptable whether you arrive at the truth (you will receive two rewards) or miss it (you are excused and will receive a reward) in matters of speculative disagreement, all of this in accordance with the tools and standards suitable for the researched issue, with the aim of shaping a Muslim character with sound perception, sincere thought, and disciplined in its engagement with the truth seriously!
