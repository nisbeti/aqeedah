And donations(318) such as: contracts of gift, bequest, endowment, lending, and guarantee without recourse.

Waivers(319), such as preemption or those that have the meaning of donation like debt forgiveness, bankruptcy, and settlement.

Restrictions(320), among the restrictions are the dismissal of the agent or the annulment of the agency and the dismissal of the guardian, and the restriction of someone who has encountered a temporary impediment to their legal capacity.

Documentation(321): It is divided into tangible guarantees like pledges and personal guarantees like guarantees, assignments, and agency.

And trusts(322) and safekeeping, and types of these transactions include deposits, found property, and custody (judicial custodian).

In the social aspect (the social system - the relationship between men and women):

Family relations and personal status, including the rulings on marriage, dowry, breastfeeding, custody, divorce, and khula, expenses, guarantees, kinship, and lineage, and inheritance.

And the rulings related to public and private life, mixing, and seclusion.

In the judicial and penal aspect:

The systems of judiciary, evidence, penalties, violations, rulings on retribution, criminal legislation, and punishments including fixed penalties, retribution, and discretionary punishment, and resolving disputes and returning rights to their owners, and establishing a protective force for the proper application of rulings.

The Book of Crimes, which includes criminal rulings such as: murder, retribution, blood money, injuries, oaths, and others.

The Book of Judgments and Testimonies, which includes: rulings of the judiciary, conditions of the judge, the method of judgment, and others, and the rulings related to testimony and the witness.

The Book of Lawsuits and Evidence (evidence on transactions or on penalties) and what relates to the rulings of litigation.

The Book of Fixed Penalties, which includes rulings on fixed penalties such as: the penalty for adultery, slander, theft, wine, highway robbery, apostasy, and rebellion.

And discretionary punishment.

And the rulings on organizing judicial oversight of the administrative actions of the state, oversight of the judiciary of grievances, and oversight of integrity.

(318) A donation contract is a contract in which the contracting party does not receive anything in exchange for what they have given, nor does the other contracting party give anything in exchange for what they have received.

(319) Waivers aim to release the debtor from the obligation owed to them, or to absolve the right-holder from it, thus clearing their liability from what it was encumbered with, and waivers can be purely voluntary.

(320) Restrictions are the prevention of a person from acting on behalf of another, and the restriction of the agency occurs by dismissing the agent, which ends upon dismissal after the initial appointment, and restrictions aim to protect the property and safeguard it from irresponsible actions.

(321) Documentation ensures the right and its performance to its owner, and is referred to as guarantees because it secures the right, and is also called assurances because it works to mitigate the risk of loss; these transactions are not intended for themselves, but for other transactions and rights, as they are a supportive and ancillary type, since their purpose is to document dealings and create a form of assurance for the safety of their consequences and results.

(322) Trust contracts are concluded with the aim of preserving property from loss, and the custodian must return the trust to its owner upon request.
