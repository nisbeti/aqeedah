And indeed, there are those who raise the doubt that many Muslims are far from adhering to Islam and its rulings. If we ease things for them, we may draw them closer to the side of religion, rather than making it difficult for them, which could lead them to deviate. It is as if these individuals are saying, first, that Islam imposes hardship on the servants, thus contradicting the previous verses. It seems they are trying to appeal to those who are distanced from Islam by distancing them from it. By my life, what is this that permits the forbidden in order to attract someone who is far from adherence to come closer to Islam! It is as if you are telling him, do not distance yourself from Islam, and I will tailor a version of Islam just for you! And that is a grave sin!

Moreover, it is the nature of our religion that it is a religion of ease, with no hardship in it. Adhering to it is the guarantee of happiness and tranquility for people. So let no one place themselves in the position of a legislator to counter Allah's rulings and tailor a religion for people that suits their desires. Let them seek the reasons behind the distance of those who are not adhering, not in the rulings themselves, for they are easy, but rather the distance from adherence is a necessary result of the failure to implement Islam on earth. Muslims are in a state of estrangement in their own lands from their religion, living in a reality that is deeply distant from Allah. Therefore, the reform of their conditions cannot come from further distancing but rather by addressing the problem at its roots through the establishment of the Caliphate, which will immediately implement the rulings, so that Muslims adhere to them and assist the misguided or stray among them to adhere by providing a healthy environment that strengthens their psyche and mentality.
