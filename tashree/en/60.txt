The Arabs understand the religion of Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) as a system of life and submission to Allah, Exalted is He, and to His laws, a sovereignty of those laws, accountability for adhering to them, responsibilities, duties, and rights, and that it replaces their own laws and they must submit to it. Therefore, it is natural to say that the nature of religion is that it came as a system of life to regulate and organize the behavior of individuals through obligations and to govern society with a specific methodology from Allah, Exalted is He!
