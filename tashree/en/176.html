Seventh: A Unique Method for Solving Human Problems Before They Occur:

It has been established in the points discussed above that Islamic legislation is characterized by the correctness of its treatments for human problems, its ability to achieve stability (which is against change), and its comprehensiveness and breadth (which is against deficiency and legislative void). The correctness of these treatments is a guarantee that individuals can be reformed in every time and place through legislation. How could it not be so, when the legislations are free from whims, favoritism, and deficiencies, as they come from a Gentle, Knowledgeable, and Expert Source, and are necessarily not based on narrow interests of the legislators and those in power? However, for this legislation to bear fruit, guarantees must be realized in the obligated (individually and collectively) that lead to effective application and belief in the correctness of these treatments. The proper application of these treatments is a guarantee for reaping the fruits of that application, by solving problems before they occur, and by the good commitment of individuals and society to apply them, submit to them, and adopt them as a reference for reward and punishment, transforming them into guiding concepts for behavior and relationships. The purpose of legislations is to reform the individual and society, and this application is contingent upon the individual's own will and the subjugation of societal relationships to it. In order for the Legislator to achieve this, He has relied on linking legislations to the pleasure of Allah, Exalted is He, as they are His commands and prohibitions, and on the concepts of lawful (halal) and unlawful (haram), and accountability in this world and the Hereafter, alongside reinforcing this with concepts such as piety (taqwa) and benevolence (ihsan), and linking it to concepts of responsibility, loyalty, rights, and duties. Additionally, the Wise Legislator has enhanced the inclination of individuals and communities towards application, implementation, and submission to the rulings through reassurance via many mechanisms that manifest in building rulings based on the stable nature of humanity, and on its essence which is based on stability, and on a comprehensive understanding of its aspirations and desires, and its needs and the proper balanced way to fulfill them, balancing between individual rights and public interest, and the general right, and between not unleashing desires and not suppressing them. This has led to a belief in the correctness of behavioral references, and to the harmony of the obligated with the legislations, and their feeling of truly achieving their interests, and has led to their belief in the justice of the perspective towards them, and their fairness; there is no favoritism, and through the distribution of duties, rights, and responsibilities according to capability, with no obligation beyond what one can bear, and through not basing responsibilities, rights, and duties on fluctuating and changing needs, and through the absence of contradictions in legislation, and through the manifestation of ease and the removal of hardship.

Moreover, the Legislator has made the rulings that may provoke intellectual tendencies in humans or that may clash with their desires, a subject of reasoning or explanation for their wisdom, so that Muslims can perceive their realization of justice and mercy. He has taken into account the innate dispositions that determine for humans the moral references and love of good, thus achieving harmony between sound dispositions and wise legislations. These dispositions are not the ruler, nor are they the measure that determines right from wrong, but they lead to a kind of reassurance for accepting the legislations that align with them, which has created trust and credibility between the Legislator and the obligated, ensuring self-application and diligence in adherence both in private and public. Thus, this is Islam's unique method in addressing human problems, reforming them, and indeed in solving human problems before they occur!
