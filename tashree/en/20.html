This is correct in general, but it remains that revelation (wahy) and reason ('aql) are not equal; one is greater than the other and more comprehensive. There is undoubtedly harmony and agreement between them, but on this basis, not on the basis that they are equal counterparts, with one being entirely equivalent to the other.

Moreover, the intellect that is free from deficiency and desires does not exist in the world of reality; rather, it is an "ideal".
