The warning can only be through revelation, exclusively and emphatically, and it is categorically prohibited to follow what has been legislated without it. This necessitates the preservation of revelation so that the warning, which serves as evidence against creation, can be established, and to cut off the path to the laws of others, and to enable His servants not to follow any guardians other than revelation: ﴾Follow what has been revealed to you from your Lord﴿, ﴾Al-An'am 106﴿, ﴾Follow what has been sent down to you from your Lord, and do not follow guardians other than Him; little do you remember﴿ ﴾Al-A'raf 3﴿, ﴾Say: "I only warn you by revelation, and the deaf do not hear the call when they are warned"﴿ ﴾Al-Anbiya 45﴿, and revelation is the Book and the Sunnah. Revelation is a complete proof and a comprehensive warning. Now that revelation has ceased, there is no prophet or messenger after Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) to whom anything new can be revealed regarding any legislation that arises or "is lost - they claim-". Allah, Exalted is He, said: ﴾Muhammad is not the father of any of your men, but he is the Messenger of Allah and the Seal of the Prophets﴿ ﴾Al-Ahzab 40﴿, which indicates the necessity of the complete and perfect proof remaining for those addressed by the religion of Islam - in belief and legislation - until the Hour comes, and its arrival to them is necessarily clear, preserved, and unaltered, both in the Book and the Sunnah! Thus, the system of Islam, with its belief and legislation, is complete, and no issue is devoid of legislation connected to revelation so that there remains no avenue for human legislation to be a source of injustice in that matter. This necessitates that the Sharia be preserved with its Qur'an and Sunnah to complete the blessing of establishing the balance of justice and equity among creation until the Day of Judgment.

Imam Ibn Hazm al-Andalusi said: "﴾Today I have perfected for you your religion and completed My favor upon you and have chosen for you Islam as religion﴿, [al-Ma'idah 3]. He said: So we say to those who permit that what Allah commanded His Prophet regarding the clarification of the Sharia of Islam for us is not preserved, and that it is permissible for it to be altered, and that it may be mixed with fabricated lies in such a way that it can never be distinguished: Inform us about Allah's - Exalted is He - completion of our religion, and His pleasure with Islam as our religion, and His prohibition of accepting any religion other than Islam. Is all of that still valid for us until the Day of Resurrection? Or was it only for the Companions - may Allah be pleased with them? Or was it neither for the Companions nor for us? There must be one of these possibilities... If the answer is necessarily: all of that remains for us and upon us until the Day of Resurrection, it is valid that all the laws of Islam are complete, and the favor in that upon us is complete, and this is a necessary and conclusive proof that everything the Messenger of Allah - (peace and blessings be upon him) - said regarding the religion, and in clarifying what is required of us, is preserved and can never be mixed with anything that is not from it."(128)

And when Allah, Exalted is He, has specified in Surah Al-A'raf, verse three, that following is limited to what Allah has revealed, and Allah, Exalted is He, said in the Noble Qur'an on the tongue of His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him): ﴾I follow only what is revealed to me, and I am nothing but a clear warner﴿ [Al-Ahqaf 9], and He said: ﴾And follow what is revealed to you from your Lord﴿ [Al-Ahzab 2], and Allah, Exalted is He, has restricted warning to revelation and emphasized this by saying: ﴾Say, "I only warn you by revelation"﴿ [Al-Anbiya 45], meaning that warning and following in speech and action can only be based on revelation, -exclusively and emphatically-.

And he commanded to follow the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), for this is evidence that the commands and prohibitions of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) are from what Allah has revealed, meaning that they are from revelation. Allah, Exalted is He, said: ﴾Follow what has been revealed to you from your Lord, and do not follow other than Him as allies. Little do you remember﴿ ﴾Al-A'raf 3﴿. The position of His saying ﴾Follow what has been revealed to you﴿ is that of a comprehensive clause in the definition, and the position of ﴾And do not follow﴿ is that of a prohibitive clause in the definition. The command to follow the unlettered Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) is evidence of his inclusion in the definition, meaning it is evidence that his sayings, actions, commands, and prohibitions are from the revelation, that is, from divine revelation.
