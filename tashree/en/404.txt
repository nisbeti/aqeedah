The Islamic Ummah did not decline or fall except after the corruption of its collective ideas. The individual ideas of Islam have not been distorted or corrupted among the Ummah: the rulings of prayer, fasting, the rules of Tajweed, and other rulings are still preserved, and every year thousands of scholars and students graduate from religious institutes and universities, spending most of their time memorizing and nurturing them. As for the collective ideas, they are what have become corrupted among the Ummah, leading to its decline. Nationalism, patriotism, socialism, secularism, conforming to reality, the interest-based economic system, the educational system, domestic policies based on secularism, making the Sharia revolve around public interest (maslaha), lowering the caliphate from the level of a decisive issue, the idea that the caliphate is an innovation of the companions and not an obligation imposed by Allah, the idea that "there are no political parties in Islam," the idea of adornment and unveiling, the idea of gradual implementation of Islam, the idea of moderation and balance, the idea of extremism, the idea of unregulated takfir, the idea of interfaith dialogue, and the degrading media, among other vile and rotten ideas and destructive means, are what have corrupted the collective atmosphere and confused the thinking of the Ummah!
The hadith of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) describes this accurately: "The example of the one who upholds the limits of Allah and the one who violates them is like a group of people who drew lots for a ship. Some of them got the upper deck and some got the lower deck; those who were in the lower deck, when they wanted to draw water, had to pass by those above them. They said: 'If we make a hole in our share, we will not harm those above us!' If they left them to do what they wanted, all would perish, but if they took hold of their hands, they would be saved, and they all would be saved." (Narrated by al-Bukhari). This hadith summarizes everything we have said: society is not merely individuals; there is something that unites them, and without it, they would not be a society, which is the public opinion from which the system emerges. The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) likens it to a ship, and then describes to us the corruption that leads to the destruction of society, which is that the ship (public opinion or custom) is breached: the corruption of some individuals does not lead to the destruction of the entire society because the ship does not sink if some of its members are sick, for example, or drunk, or otherwise. However, if the corruption of these individuals exceeds their individuality and transforms into a corruption of public opinion and the customs prevailing in society, and a violation of the limits of Allah (i.e., breaching the ship), then everyone will drown, both the righteous and the wicked. The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "When people see a wrongdoing and do not denounce it, it is likely that Allah will encompass them all with punishment from Him." The phrase "when they see" means that the wrongdoing is apparent before public opinion, and the silence of public opinion about it means its approval. The approval of public opinion for the wrongdoing means its corruption, making the wrongdoing recognized in the eyes of society, and the corruption of public opinion and custom means the sinking of the ship.(557)
