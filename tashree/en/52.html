But the community in Islam embodies all meanings of integration in life and mutual support (takafol) that achieves the interests of this world and the Hereafter(102) and the distribution of wealth and the utilization of facilities and services.

This integration forms a guarantee for the effectiveness of those systems, in a manner that aligns their solutions with human nature and the elements of human existence, addressing its components and energies, fulfilling its genuine basic needs that arise from the vital energy of humans—represented by their instincts and organic needs—in a balanced, purposeful manner that elevates humans above the level of animalistic satisfaction of those instincts and needs, without conflicting with their spiritual aspirations, and harmonizing—at the same time—with their material societal human reality, not based on whims and narrow immediate interests, as is the case in all other prevailing human legislations and systems in the world today!

Indeed, those divine legislations have taken into account the humanity of man, his intellect, and his psychology, and recognized that he lives in a material world, fraught with problems and calamities. Thus, the miraculous feature of legislation is its ability to penetrate the depths of various problems, what affects them, and what they are affected by, and to view them from a comprehensive perspective, based on an understanding of human nature, the reality of its motives, aspirations, and longings, and an understanding of human life and the diversity of its needs and fluctuations. Then, it established legislations that guarantee the creation of a righteous society and a reformed human being, achieving interests for humanity in a correct manner that brings about good and eradicates evil! It establishes justice and eliminates oppression!

Then, the other miracle of legislation is embodied in linking legislation to religious and moral values, so that legislation serves and protects them, rather than being a tool for their destruction. The Sharia is distinguished by what is not found in other legislations; Islam has made it standards based on belief, making it a doctrinal deterrent that compels a person to adhere to it out of fear of Allah, love for Allah, and hope in Allah's favor, which makes a person independent of the need for an external authority to compel him to abide by the law. Rather, it makes him submit to the authority of Allah in private and public, so he does not see evading the punishment of the ruler in this world as a gain or profit; he knows that Allah is watching him, and its punishments are both worldly and otherworldly.

The concepts, standards, and convictions that Islam has sought to transform its rulings (its treatments, its system) into have crystallized into a set of values(103) shaped by Islamic civilization, which establish the convictions that people will live by in their individual behavior and in their societal relationships. That is, the intellectual foundation that Islam has established to shape the Islamic personality of the individual is the same intellectual foundation that will underpin societal relationships, which have been firmly rooted in the essence of the relationships upon which society is built. Thus, the preservation of the nation and society on the system of those values and their safeguarding, and preventing their opposites or what corrupts them (that is, through the obligation of enjoining good and forbidding evil in all its forms) is linked to the elevation and renaissance of the nation, its goodness, its harmony with itself, and its occupying a peak position among other nations, making it a strong and impenetrable fortress that protects and preserves.
