I have a claim,
I am a sinner, I am wrong, I am disobedient; He is Forgiving, He is Merciful, He is Sufficient.
I have accepted them three with three, and His attributes will prevail over my attributes.

Al-Muzani narrated: I visited al-Shafi'i during the illness in which he died, and I said: O Abu Abdullah, how have you woken up? He raised his head and said: I have woken up as one departing from this world, separating from my brothers, facing my evil deeds, and approaching Allah; I do not know whether my soul will go to Paradise, so I may congratulate it, or to Hell, so I may console it. Then he wept and began to say(562):
To You, O God of creation, I raise my desire,
And when my heart hardened and my ways narrowed,
My sins overwhelmed me, so when I coupled them,
You have always been the Forgiver of sins.
If it were not for You, no worshipper would stand against Satan,
So if You forgive me, You forgive a rebellious one,
And if You take revenge on me, I am not hopeless,
So may Allah bless the one who knows, indeed,
He stands when the night stretches its darkness.
He cries out when he remembers his Lord,
And he recalls the days that have passed from his youth.
He has become a companion of worry throughout his day,
Saying: My beloved, You are my request and my desire.
Are You not the One who nourished me and guided me?
Perhaps the One who has kindness will forgive my slip.
Around me is the grace of Allah from every side,
And in my heart is the illumination of the lover through His connection.
Around me is the comfort from Allah alone,
I protect my affection from being tainted by passion.
For in my wakefulness is longing, and in my slumber is hope,
And whoever relies on Allah will be safe from the people.

And if I am, O You of grace and generosity, a criminal,
I have made hope from me a ladder to Your pardon.
By Your forgiveness, my Lord, Your forgiveness is the greatest.
You are generous and forgive as a favor and a kindness.
So how, when Your chosen one, Adam, was tempted,
Is he oppressive and cruel, never leaving sin?
And if they were to cast my soul into Hell for my crime,
My eyelids would overflow with tears from excessive longing.
For my soul, from the intensity of fear, would be a mourning.
And in what is other than that among the people, it was mute.
And what was in it was not due to ignorance a crime.
O brother of sleeplessness and whispers when the night darkens,
You are enough for those who hope for a request and a gain.
And may You always be generous to me and bestow blessings.
And cover my burdens and what has preceded.
And a light from the Most Merciful spreads across the sky,
When the good news approaches and enters the sanctuary.
It greets me in the darkness of the grave as stars,
And I keep the covenant of love from being broken.
My steps are pursued by ecstasy and melody,
And whoever hopes for You, it is unlikely that he will regret.

Thus, we ask Him, Exalted is He, to make our deeds sincere for His noble face, correct, free from the love of fame and the love of mention, and that our share of them be nothing but good in this world and the Hereafter.

(562) Al-Muzani narrated some of these verses, and I appended the rest of the poem from other references.
