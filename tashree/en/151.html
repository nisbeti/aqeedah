Islam differs fundamentally in its approach to solving problems compared to man-made laws, as it provides a solution that is contrary to legislation that addresses people's issues based on their identities, where one seeks legislation that permits adultery and another seeks legislation that permits alcohol. Legislation is tailored to the whims of the legislators or those associated with them from industrialists and capitalists, or based on favoritism, utilitarianism, and desires, which renders the legislation incapable of enduring for days, let alone being suitable for humanity in the journey of this worldly life.

Islamic law (sharia) has addressed human problems to organize behavior, ensuring that the solution is genuinely good and that anything else is truly bad. It has taken into account the establishment of truth and justice, ensuring that behavior leads to the cultivation of the earth through righteous deeds rather than spreading corruption(260). Thus, the law avoids whims and narrow, immediate interests, elevating humanity above the level of animality in satisfying instincts and organic needs, while respecting human dignity and intellect, achieving true benefit rather than mere conjecture, as a mercy to all people, based on a correct perspective of the universe, humanity, and life, founded on a proper solution to the great dilemma based on reason and in harmony with human nature.
