To encompass all the methodologies that they included within their margins and foundations, which the usuliyyun (scholars of usul al-fiqh) found necessary for them, even if there were points of disagreement among them regarding some aspects, such as their disagreement on the nature of consensus (ijma) while agreeing on its necessity, and their differences regarding some rules and evidences. However, they placed the disputed rules within a mechanism inside their specific methodologies (the principles of their schools) that ensures a good interaction with other evidences, like those who advocate for istihsan (juridical preference), for example, as they refer to it when needed after studying the other more important evidences such as the Qur'an and the Sunnah. You may disagree or agree with them in resorting to it, but they have regulated their foundational methodologies and the way to activate them in a manner that allows the mujtahid (jurist) in that school to proceed in the same way in every process of ijtihad (independent juristic reasoning), which ensures consistency in the results and adherence to the method.
