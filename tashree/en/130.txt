Reforming it, the Sharia is based on understanding the reality of those terms and facts, which entails specific legal rulings such as enjoining good and forbidding evil, establishing the abode of Islam, and others. However, it has prohibited the use of certain terms that obscure concepts; some people have coined specific terms to express certain meanings that do not deviate from them, (like the term democracy), or these terms indicate concepts that contradict Islamic concepts regarding a specific reality, (like the term social justice), while the terms that do not fall under the previous matters may be used, (like the terms constitution and law).
In topics defining reality, such as defining reason, defining society, and defining renaissance, whether it is through intellectual elevation or economic elevation, of course, some rulings will relate to applying legal rulings to this reality after understanding it. For example: after knowing the reality of society and defining it and its composition, and how to influence it, and studying the sciences of public opinion formation, propaganda, and advertising, all these sciences are rational and universal; after studying them, we use them in implementing the legal ruling specific to changing or reforming society, and this change and reform are legal rulings governed by the Sharia.
Also, in technical matters such as specifications and standards, whether it is preferable to use a certain material in manufacturing a certain device or not, this is purely rational. As for what enters into the manufactured items in legislation, the industry takes the ruling of the manufactured item, so it is forbidden to produce the prohibited (like turning grapes into wine or making crosses).
Likewise, in the administrative aspects of the state, such as establishing departments and administrations that promote the affairs of the state and serve the interests of the people, and organizing road affairs, and channeling rivers for irrigating crops, and adopting the diwan system, and using computers and statistical programs, and others, and the method of counting voters' voices, these are universal and are taken by reason.
Similarly, in technical matters related to military strategies, whether the international position is alongside a certain state or another, whether the internal situation and the international position are conducive to carrying out political actions, or military actions alongside political actions, or not conducive? Should the change of the ruling system in a state where power is concentrated in certain tribes focus on those tribes or not? And so on.
In such matters, the opinion is taken by consulting experts and specialists in that subject, and it is referred to the truth, because whatever its type falls under the saying of the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him): "Rather, it is opinion, and war, and stratagem." And the correct opinion is referred to, just as the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) referred to the opinion of al-Hubab. Such is the technical opinion. Because the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) when he referred to the opinion of al-Hubab ibn al-Mundhir, al-Hubab was knowledgeable about that place, so he referred to him for his expertise. Therefore, the technical opinion is referred to the truth. This technical opinion is derived from reason and its understanding of reality.
