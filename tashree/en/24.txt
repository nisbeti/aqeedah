In his book "The Clash of Civilizations," Samuel Huntington discusses how "democracy in the Islamic world could lead to the rise of political Islam," warning that "this could lead to tensions between the Islamic world and the West."
Bernard Lewis spoke about how "democratic systems in the Islamic world could enhance political Islam," noting that "this could have complex effects on international relations."
