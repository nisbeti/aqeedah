As for technological and urban advancement, it does not make a person independent of knowing the ruling on eating pork compared to the ruling on eating beef, nor does it make the use of a fork and knife with pork permissible after it was forbidden!
A simple look at Islamic legislation shows that it was revealed with laws to address reality, with jurisprudential research that clarifies goodness and evil, benefit and harm, reward and punishment, and clarifies the rulings on actions and things, and outlines the systems of life, society, and the state. Thus, the reality of the Sharia is that it provides humanity with an eternal, everlasting way of life that addresses every reality with its specific rulings. Since this reality is related to humans as human beings, who have instincts and organic needs, and a vital energy that drives them to engage in the challenges of life to satisfy this vital energy and those instincts and organic needs according to a specific behavior and certain systems that organize human interaction with others to address these actions and relationships, according to a specific system related to each reality with specific rulings and systems that clarify its goodness from its evil, its beauty from its ugliness, and the reward for performing an action or adhering to the system in life from the punishment for deviating from it, the Sharia cannot be a product of its circumstances, nor can it be specific to one time over another, or one circumstance over another, or one society over another. The aspirations, instincts, and needs of humans are defined and regulated by specific laws that do not change, and humans in every time and place are the same in their instincts and organic needs, which never change. Likewise, the rulings addressing them do not change. What changes around humans are some methods and tools; for example, after riding horses, they now travel by airplane, which does not affect their perspective on life. However, the renewed demands of humans arise from those instincts and organic needs, and the Sharia has come broadly to address these renewed and varied demands, no matter how diverse and how their forms change; this has been one of the reasons for the growth of jurisprudence.
Technology does not influence the determination of right from wrong; rather, it is merely a means that facilitates human life, whether one writes on bones and papyrus, or on clay tablets, or on paper, or on a computer, or uses a car. This change in means does not alter the organic composition of humans in such a way that the method of satisfying their sexual instinct changes, for example, whether through marriage, fornication, or otherwise. Nor does it mean they can do without food and drink. However, some detailed rulings may be affected; for instance, a woman traveling by airplane will be subject to the regulations regarding the time distance that allows her to travel without a mahram (male guardian), thus shortening distances, allowing her to travel by airplane between continents in a permissible journey, which would not have been permissible if she had traveled the same distance on the backs of camels (with differences among the schools of thought in the details). Instead of traveling fifty kilometers in a day and night, she will travel from one end of the world to the other, where her mahram will meet her on the other side. Likewise, when a person travels by airplane or by animal, they will eat and drink, and they are permitted to shorten their prayers and break their fast, and the essence of the human remains the same! Thus, the rulings regarding their food and drink, fasting and breaking the fast, prayer, and other matters related to their behavior are not affected by the means of their travel. Pork does not become permissible because one eats it with a fork and knife on an airplane, nor because the ruling of its prohibition was written on bones and has now been written on paper! Forbidden foods remain forbidden for both, and permissible foods remain permissible for both. Therefore, the effect is indirect on the basis of the applied rulings.
Thus, traveling by airplane has not created a legislative vacuum in Islam! Rather, the legal rulings have been able to apply to new realities with ease and simplicity, because their language has clarified how they can be applied to reality regardless of the means.
