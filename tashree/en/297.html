Seventh: Doctrinal Concepts That Drive Understanding Life and Positive Interaction with It:

The set of previous doctrinal concepts was related to faith in terms of the truth of its correspondence to reality in matters of existence, such as belief in the existence of God, that the Qur'an is His word, that people will be resurrected for reckoning and punishment, and in paradise and hell, and in the message of the messengers. Thus, belief in these matters is a belief in an existing reality that is confirmed by evidence and proofs, which reassure the hearts of its truth. There is another type of doctrinal concepts related to issues that manifest in understanding life, including trials and tribulations, gifts and decrees, effective laws, interaction with events, whose function is to shape the Islamic personality according to doctrinal concepts that drive it to interact positively with life and to understand the vicissitudes of time, and the role of the human being in what occurs from or to him in actions and circumstances. Its function is to guide the individual in life according to doctrinal concepts that serve as a fence surrounding the actions and behaviors he undertakes in adherence to the Sharia.

These doctrinal issues are also divided into two sections: one section clarifies that the vicissitudes of time and the decree of destiny are solely in God's hands; He is the Owner of the Kingdom, granting it to whom He wills and taking it away from whom He wills. He gives life and causes death, provides sustenance, honors, and humiliates. Among these issues are: divine decree (qada) and destiny (qadar), sustenance (rizq), victory (nasr), causing death (imatah), giving life (ihya), resurrection (ba'th), and the Day of Judgment (nushur), and similar matters.

The second section pertains to the human being and his interaction with those issues, such as the concept of reliance on God (tawakkul), and the concept that sustenance is in the hands of Allah, how these doctrinal issues drive him to act in life. For when he knows that life and death are in God's hands, he fulfills his duties without fear of dying before his appointed time. And when he knows that the mosques are for Allah, He alone is worthy of servitude and fear, he fears no one but Allah. And when he knows that what has befallen him could not have missed him, and what he has missed could not have befallen him, if he asks, he asks no one but Allah, and if he seeks help, he seeks help from no one but Allah. And that the pens have been lifted and the pages have dried, he does not fear anyone among humans, saying the truth without fearing the blame of the blamer, striving for the rank of the master of martyrs by speaking a word of truth before a tyrant. And when he is certain, with no doubt, that sustenance is solely in the hands of Allah, he looks at the servants among kings, sultans, and the wealthy with the gaze of a free man who knows that none of them can withhold from him a date that Allah has decreed for him, nor can they give him a date that Allah has not decreed for him. And that sustenance is in the hands of Allah, not merely that he knows that so-and-so will be provided for, [for the provider is Allah alone, meaning that Allah is the one who actually provides sustenance, that ascribing the act of providing sustenance to Allah is a reality, not a metaphor, and no one among humans can provide for himself or others. Allah extends sustenance to whom He wills and decrees for him], so he does not fear for the sustenance of tomorrow, for no one other than Allah has the ability to provide sustenance, ﴾What Allah opens for people of mercy, none can withhold it; and what He withholds, none can release it after Him. And He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise. O mankind, remember the favor of Allah upon you. Is there any creator other than Allah who provides for you from the heaven and the earth? There is no deity except Him; so how are you deluded?﴿ [Fatir 2-3]. Reflect: is there any creator other than Allah who provides for you! For anyone to be able to provide for you, they must possess the ability to create! And they must be worthy of worship by virtue of their ability to provide sustenance, and only the One who created the heavens and the earth can provide sustenance! ﴾So how are you deluded?﴿! How do you turn away from His oneness and seek sustenance from others, and therein lies a sign of the belief that you need not fear for your sustenance if you proclaim the truth, ﴾So how are you deluded?﴿! Do not turn away from seeking sustenance from Him to others who do not possess even the weight of an atom in the heavens or the earth! So do not fear for your sustenance from those who cannot provide for themselves, nor even a date! And proceed in carrying the message openly without fear, for this will not prevent anyone from receiving a date that Allah has decreed for him!
