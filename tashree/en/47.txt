Good and evil, or beauty and ugliness, or truth and falsehood, or reward and punishment, we can recognize the seriousness and importance of possessing the correct doctrinal perception (tasawwur 'aqidi) and the system that emerges from it on the life of the individual, society, and state! As for those who choose to live life aimlessly without any standards, concepts, or convictions, or with mixed standards and convictions, where goodness is good one day and evil another day, they do not concern us, and we do not accept to make them an example to rise according to!
