The seventh reason is that their schools of thought have been applied in judicial matters and in practical life, and Islamic life has proceeded in harmony with the legislation. This is one of the most important reasons, as their schools of thought are practical and capable of solving the problems and disputes of Muslims in a legitimate manner that is rooted in the reality of the practical legal rulings derived from the comprehensive evidence, with a coherent and holistic reasoning. They organize the affairs of life and are capable of being applied to new issues just as they were applied to the matters that those schools addressed in the history of Muslims. The final reason is that the community has accepted their schools of thought. (223)
