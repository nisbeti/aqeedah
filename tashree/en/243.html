Concepts, Criteria, and Convictions:

"Ideas are the meanings of words, and concepts are the meanings of ideas, not the meanings of words. When a person believes in an idea, it transforms from merely being an idea into a concept that influences behavior. Therefore, every thought that is believed in is called a concept, regardless of the narrowness or breadth of the meaning contained in the concept. Some concepts relate to a single subsidiary matter, such as the prohibition of drinking alcohol; it contains a single meaning, which is that the Sharia has prohibited drinking alcohol, meaning it contains only one subsidiary ruling... However, some concepts relate to multiple meanings because they can be used to measure several subsidiary ideas, so they are not confined to a single matter... For example, the concept of halal and haram is a concept that encompasses all human actions; it is a criterion for human actions, not limited to just one matter. In this case, this concept is a criterion by which other ideas and concepts are measured. For instance, the concept of the default rule in things being permissibility is a criterion by which many matters are measured and is not restricted to a single issue. Thus, from the perspective of breadth, the criterion is broader than the concept, while conversely, it is more specific than the concept, considering that the term concept is used for partial ideas as well as for the criterion. Hence, it becomes clear that every criterion is an idea and a concept for those who believe in it, but not every concept is a criterion because the concept may be a subsidiary idea or it may be a criterion, whereas the criterion can only be a matter upon which branches are built and by which it is measured; it cannot be a subsidiary idea.

As for convictions, they are the concepts and criteria that have taken on the role of deep-rootedness in the individual and the nation, becoming entrenched in souls and in society to the extent that it is difficult to uproot them. When a concept or criterion becomes deeply rooted in souls and in society, it rises in degree and becomes a conviction such that it cannot be easily uprooted or removed from the individual and society. There are some concepts and criteria that must reach the level of convictions in the individual and society to preserve the individual and the nation, such as the concept and criterion that the default in actions is adherence to the legal ruling, and the concept of obedience, and the concept of jihad, and the concept of reliance on Allah, and the command to enjoin what is good and forbid what is wrong... etc.
