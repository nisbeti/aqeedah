"Indeed, those who wronged among you are specific, and know that Allah is severe in punishment" ﴾Al-Anfal 25﴿. Ibn Abbas said: "Allah commanded the believers not to allow wrongdoing among them, lest they be encompassed by punishment." And Ibn Kathir said: "Allah, Exalted is He, warns His believing servants of a trial, meaning a test and affliction, that will encompass both the wrongdoer and others, and will not be limited to the people of disobedience or those who committed the sin, but will include them all." Abu Bakr (may Allah be pleased with him) said: "O people, you recite this verse ﴾O you who have believed, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones. It will be fueled by angels, harsh and severe; they do not disobey Allah in what He commands them but do what they are commanded﴿ [Al-Ma'idah 105]. And I heard the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) say: 'When people see a wrongdoer and do not take action against him, it is likely that Allah will encompass them all with punishment from Him.'" (Narrated by al-Tirmidhi, who said: It is a good and authentic hadith).
