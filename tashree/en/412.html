Liberation of Concepts: Politics, Governance, Authority, and the State...

To begin with, it is necessary to define the terms so that the discussion has clear boundaries:

Politics: The term "politics" in language indicates the management of affairs and the administration of matters. In Islamic terminology, it means managing the affairs of the nation in accordance with the Sharia. The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "The Children of Israel were governed by their prophets," meaning their prophets took charge of their affairs and managed their lives. The methodology of "Sharia-compliant politics" is the administration of people's affairs according to the rulings of Islam, applying the law of Allah, and achieving their worldly and otherworldly interests whenever the rulings pertain to that. In brief, politics is the implementation of Sharia rulings in managing society and the state to achieve the objectives of the Sharia.

Governance: In Sharia, it refers to authority, judiciary, and the enforcement of legislation. Governance by what Allah has revealed is the fundamental duty of the Islamic state: ﴾And if you judge, judge between them by what Allah has revealed...﴿ [al-Ma'idah 49]. Governance is the exercise of authority to compel people to adhere to the rulings of religion in their relationships. It also means resolving disputes (judiciary) and enforcing penalties. Therefore, the concept of governance is linked to justice; linguistically, it means prevention, as they say: "Governance linguistically is the prevention for the reform of something," meaning preventing injustice and chaos by compelling people to adhere to the Sharia to achieve righteousness.

Authority: It refers to legitimate authority, meaning the binding power that derives its legitimacy from the pledge of allegiance (bay'ah) according to the rulings of Sharia. Authority in Islam belongs to the nation, which grants it through the legal contract (bay'ah) to whomever it chooses as its leader. The term "those in authority" refers to the bearers of this authority, which is constrained by the law of Allah. Any authority without a legal basis is an illegitimate usurpation of power. Thus, authority is the right to implement rulings that the Muslim ruler carries out with the delegation of the nation and within the limits of the Sharia.

The State: It is the political entity that organizes the Muslim community under a single leadership and implements Islam fully internally and spreads it externally. It is defined as a collection of institutions and agencies overseen by the ruler (the caliph and his associates) to manage the affairs of the subjects according to the Sharia rulings. Jurists have detailed the institutions of the caliphate state to include the caliph, his assistants, governors, judges, the army, and financial administration, etc., to ensure that it fulfills its functions. The Islamic state is not merely a neutral administrative body, but an executive apparatus for implementing the rulings of Islamic doctrine and carrying its message. Hence, Islamic doctrine is the foundation of the state and the source of legitimacy for its constitution and laws.

Obedience: It refers to the obedience to those in authority (the rulers who govern by the Sharia) in what is good. It is obligatory in what they command as long as it does not contradict the law of Allah: ﴾Obey Allah and obey the Messenger and those in authority among you﴿ [an-Nisa 59]. Thus, obedience to the authority is a branch of obedience to Allah and His Messenger, which is why he (peace and blessings be upon him) restricted it by saying: "There is no obedience to a created being in disobedience to the Creator." Political obedience means adhering to the decisions of the state and its legislation derived from the Sharia, in order to achieve unity of ranks and comply with the command of Allah. It also includes submission to the Sharia judiciary and all other authoritative rulings.

Consultation: It is the process of consulting and seeking opinions between the ruler and the people of expertise and opinion from the nation.
