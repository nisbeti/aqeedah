Some features of the effects of legislative chaos and the dominance of Western civilization over the Islamic world
If we reflect on the dangerous transformations that the dominance of Western civilization and its control have imposed today on the world and humanity, "which have restructured man in this era according to a mechanical secular vision, unleashing the weaving of his life according to a purely material machine, separating him from his essence, and crushing his being, so that he manifests as a being alien to his humanity, turning away from the God of the heavens, the Creator of the universe, man, and life, and from the promised paradise of heaven as a reward for good deeds in this life, turning instead to the worship of new earthly deities, cloaked in the guise of secularism and modernity at times, and behind the veil of technological progress at other times, promising him an earthly paradise governed by raging desires and passions, and the glorification of pleasures, so this earthly perspective has bequeathed him a life of hardship, and misery of the soul"(78), and emptiness of thought, and confusion and agnosticism that he is cloaked in, and he no longer cares!
If we look deeper, we find a complete reversal of the concepts of culture and civilization by completely emptying them of all value and meaning, and of every human dimension that represents the ladder of social values, knowledge, beliefs, arts, morals, traditions, philosophy, and the other talents, capabilities, and customs that distinguish societies, transforming them into material value and immediate benefit, reducing them to mere increases in production, marketing, and profit, accumulating quantity upon quantity, of goods and rates of production and consumption, until invention became the father of necessity, and man himself transformed into a commodity, or a promotional material to market a product that costs no more than a quarter of a dinar!
And societies transformed from being cultural entities whose values are embodied in their way of life into consumer groups, which did not last long after their intellectual, cultural, and civilizational emptiness, accompanied by the kindling of the fire of racism, discrimination, conflict, and extermination, until wars and bloodshed broke out among them, with no beginning and no end, and people were dazzled by technological progress at the expense of a severe regression in the field of human values, and sciences and technology were unable to solve the problems of the world, nor did they lead to an explanation of the meaning of existence, and they only increased the agnostics in doubt, confusion, loneliness, bewilderment, and aversion, thus we find that secularism did not answer the existential questions of man, being the first principle in the history of humanity that ignores the existential issue of man, which produced a civilization of misery and anxiety, where man lives ignorant of his being and becoming.
Colin Wilson says in his book The Decline of Civilization: "I look at our civilization as I would at something cheap and trivial, considering that it represents the degradation of all rational standards."
The historian Arnold Toynbee states: "Western civilization is afflicted with spiritual emptiness that turns man into a deformed dwarf lacking the elements of human existence, living the bare minimum of his life, which is only his material existence, transforming society into a herd that runs aimlessly, and turning his life into a hell filled with anxiety, confusion, and psychological fragmentation."
T. S. Eliot said: "The greatest evidence of the decline of our culture is our confused reaction to the name of God." "Where is the wisdom that we have lost in knowledge? Where is the knowledge that we have lost in information?"
