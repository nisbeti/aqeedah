Its fourth manifestation: balance, facilitation, lifting hardship, and obligation within capacity:

Balance represents a cornerstone in achieving stability, happiness, and tranquility, and this balance must be embodied in a regulatory system that truly realizes it. For the lack of balance will inevitably lead to disturbance, confusion, and anxiety(286). The balance of satisfying vital energy without repression leads to anxiety, while unrestrained expression leads to behavioral deviation. The balance lies in obligating what is within capacity and what one can do, and prohibiting what one can leave, while also considering normal and exceptional circumstances with balanced rulings that are applicable, lifting hardship, and balancing between the causes of fear, dread, greed, hope, and reliance on Allah, and finding comfort in Him, and trusting Him, and having good thoughts about Allah, and mistakes and repentance, in the relationship of the servant with his Lord, and taking the means and societal and human laws that the servant is capable of adhering to, and positively interacting with them. All of this achieves tranquility and balance in the distribution of wealth in society, where the state bears the responsibility of fulfilling the basic needs of those who hold citizenship, and distributes the spoils to the poor rather than the rich, achieving a balance between individual rights and the public interest and the common right, which will ensure stability in society.
