Judiciary.

In the societal aspect:

The private societal relationships that result in the removal of harm and transgression against others (punishments, evidences, retribution, rights, and judiciary, and emancipation).

The societal relationships that require the establishment of societal balance, such as social solidarity, welfare, and similar matters.

In public relations, meaning the relationships between the ruler and the ruled, that is, constitutional relationships and relationships with public authority, judicial systems and evidences, and rulings on retribution and punishments.

In international relations, meaning the relationships between groups, nations, and states with one another, in the rulings of treaties, jihad, messengers, and embassies.
