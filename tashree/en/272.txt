The Fifth Column: The Concept of Guardianship Among Believers and the Concept of Fulfilling One's Role:
The concept of responsibility in Islam is closely linked to two other concepts that Islam has firmly established, and upon which it has arranged duties assigned to the accountable individuals based on the role (or position or obligation) they fulfill in life, and based on an important bond that connects believers to one another, which is the bond of general guardianship (wilaya) that makes male and female believers like a single body, a structure that does not disintegrate. We have seen that Islam has established specific and general responsibilities and arranged them based on the specific role each Muslim undertakes in life. The imam is a shepherd and is responsible for his flock, because the role he plays in life is one of general responsibility for overseeing the affairs of the flock, both Muslims and dhimmis. Thus, the limits, retribution, and disciplinary measures, the conclusion of treaties, and the establishment of internal policy to oversee affairs according to the rulings of Islam, the resolution of disputes and the restoration of rights to their owners, and the external policy for carrying the Islamic call and jihad, and so forth, all of this has been placed under the responsibility of the ruler and those he appoints to oversee it with meticulous supervision to ensure the proper fulfillment of these duties. Therefore, he has authorities through which he can carry out these duties; he has been given power to assist him in protecting the Islamic state, and he has been given the police to ensure the proper application of the system, and he has been granted authorities that enable him to promote what is good and remove what is wrong, and so on. All these responsibilities are arranged according to his role as an imam for the Muslims. If the ruler neglects the responsibility entrusted to him and fails to fulfill it, then his sin is indeed very great. For example, when calamity follows calamity upon the nation in its borders and occupied lands, and when the honor of chaste Muslim women is violated, and when the West plunders the nation's wealth, and so forth, the silence of the Muslim rulers regarding the crimes of the enemies of the nation is nothing but direct participation with them in sin and aggression. There is no Muslim who is killed except that they are partners in his killing, and there is no right that is wasted except that they are partners in the crime, because they have neglected to bear the responsibility, unless they strive with all their effort and exhaust their energy in a manner that they can be excused before Allah. As for the fact that they did not do so, they are equal to the enemies of Islam in sin and equal in punishment, and knowledge is with Allah, Exalted is He. Among the evidence for this are a number of hadiths and verses that we cited when we discussed the concept of responsibility earlier.
The army has a role that carries with it specific responsibilities beyond what the general populace bears; they are responsible for protecting the borders, spreading the call through jihad, and the jihad of defense to reclaim what has been usurped from the land of Islam, and defending Muslims and supporting them. Therefore, if they fall short in fulfilling this role, the burden that befalls them is a great burden, and their failure to support is participation in aggression against Muslims, and their failure to support the call of Islam to establish the Islamic state is participation in prolonging the life of falsehood and the rule of tyranny. They bear the burden of every right that is lost, every case judged by other than what Allah has revealed, every honor that is violated, and everything that is stolen or plundered by the enemies of Allah from the wealth of the nation.
The scholars have a very critical role that deserves our attention, and we must warn all the scholars of the nation with a warning of great importance. Indeed, Allah, Exalted is He, has informed us that the scholar becomes godly (rabani) through what he learns from the Book of Allah and what he teaches to the people. Allah, Exalted is He, said: ﴾But be rabbaniyyin for what you have been teaching of the Book and for what you have been studying﴿. This is a rank of honor and a path among the paths to Paradise. However, you may be a godly scholar and yet the wrath of the Lord, Exalted is He, intensifies against you, and He threatens you with severe warning! This is because the role and mission assigned to you as a scholar is one of the most dangerous tasks and the most important obligations. You must be a leader for the community, guiding it in the correct direction, and directing it towards the great tasks it must undertake. Among your great duties is to command the rulers to do what is good and to forbid them from wrongdoing.
