As you can see, the Western legislator has collided with the reality of the continuous development of society and its changing perspectives, and the changes in what is called public interests accordingly, and the role of media in entrenching certain values that were prohibited for a time, then those in power wished to change society's perspective on them (for example: society's view on the rights of homosexuals, and for example: society's view on drugs and the use of cannabis which was legally prohibited, and now has become legal), and this has made them pay little attention to entrenching the purposive perspective, and to providing precise definitions for each specific objective, and how to observe it. The objectives themselves for them are subject to change, (and most of their objectives have been taken from Islamic law) (510) so how about the laws that serve those objectives!
The lack of clarity regarding the fundamental purpose of enacting laws among Westerners will inevitably lead to the misuse of the law, which undermines its credibility and neutrality in many instances. Therefore, the clarity of the purpose of the law and understanding its dimensions robs those who possess influence and power of the opportunity to interpret the law according to their personal interests (511). If the objectives themselves are smooth, fluctuating, and changing, and the definitions are nebulous and unregulated, then the breach in the exploitation of laws will certainly widen!
And if society is not based on solidarity, compassion, mutual affection, and responsibility for others, then all of this will not be achieved by legislating laws, but by establishing a thought and grounding it on a principle. However, the secular principle does not concern itself with any of this! So where will you find attention to legislating such laws by the legislators if the principle itself overlooks them? Certainly, they will not pay attention to any of it! What greater and more catastrophic loss is there for humanity! And what a great gap and vast difference there is between Islam and its suitability and good remedies, and between secularism and its shortcomings, individuality, and legislative and intellectual failure!
