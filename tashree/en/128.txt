Therefore, one can choose to trade in oil or wheat wherever reason sees benefit, (unlike the sale of wine and the prohibited, for when the legislator forbids engaging in it, he forbids its sale and purchase). Whereas when it comes to paying wages to workers in companies, the law intervenes by mandating the payment of the agreed-upon wage. Thus, there are matters regulated by the law and others left to reason and knowledge.
