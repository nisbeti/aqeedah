Love, fear, and hope elevate the servant to the sublime proximity of Allah. "The essence of this is that He, Exalted is He, makes Himself known to the servant through His divine attributes at times, and through His lordly attributes at other times. This necessitates for him the witnessing of the divine attributes: a pure, special love, longing for His meeting, comfort and joy in Him, happiness in serving Him, striving for closeness to Him, showing affection to Him through obedience, constantly remembering Him, and fleeing from creation to Him, so that He becomes his sole concern, above all else. It also necessitates for him the witnessing of the lordly attributes: reliance upon Him, neediness towards Him, seeking help from Him, humility, submission, and brokenness before Him. The perfection of this is to witness His lordship in His divinity, and His divinity in His lordship, His praise in His dominion, His might in His forgiveness, His wisdom in His decree and predestination, His grace in His trials, His giving in His withholding, His kindness, gentleness, benevolence, and mercy in His sustaining, and His justice in His vengeance, His generosity and nobility in His forgiveness, concealment, and overlooking, and to witness His wisdom and grace in His commands and prohibitions, His might in His pleasure and anger, His forbearance in His delay, and His generosity in His acceptance, and His richness in His turning away"(431).

Secondly: A method of conduct and a positive system of life, with clear features and goals:

These verses clarify to humanity its purpose of existence, and explain to individuals their position, responsibilities, and role in life, linking this to what precedes worldly life and what follows it, in a manner that ensures an impact on the social relationships arising from or based on that perception, "a self-emerging, unforced emergence"(432), an emergence that produces a method of conduct and a system of life, which manifests in a way of living, and is reflected in adopting an intellectual leadership through which a person navigates the challenges of life, advancing or retreating, according to what is determined for him as right and wrong, good and evil, or truth and falsehood, and responsibilities and duties, walking together on a straight path. Thus, obedience, closeness, piety, and vigilance serve as a fence that brings the believer closer to his Lord and distances him from sins. He knows that his Lord is All-Hearing, All-Seeing, All-Knowing, and Expert regarding the innermost thoughts. Therefore, the servant does not suffice with mere compliance in word and deed, but also in the fleeting thoughts of the soul, and in the depths of intentions and secrets, weighing all of that with the scale of the Sharia, leaving nothing in the depths of his soul or in his outward affairs that he would be ashamed of before Allah, or that contradicts His command!

And excellence elevates him to the heights of advancement towards the highest ranks in refining his character and understanding the meaning of his humanity.

Thirdly: The truthfulness of applicability to reality:

These ideas are characterized by their truthful applicability to reality, and their easy, unforced harmony and agreement with human nature. They penetrate the human soul through various avenues, from the senses through imagination and rhythm, conveying meanings in a manner that expresses the concepts with the utmost precision, selecting the purest phrases most suitable for the meaning conveyed, blending between the abstract mental image and then depicting it in its illustrative, concrete form. They touch the heart filled with emotions, the soul brimming with feelings, and the mind reflecting on the universe, humanity, and life. It is natural that hearts are drawn to them, that eyes overflow with tears in response to them, and that believers fall to their chins in prostration, their skins shivering, their hearts humbled by their greatness, and that souls are stirred, as tranquility, reassurance, and emotional impact arise within them, prompting them to carry His message, and that minds soar to the heights.
