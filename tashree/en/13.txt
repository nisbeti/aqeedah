Alongside the raging military war that the West is waging against the Muslim world, the features of a strategic civilizational struggle against Islam, especially political Islam, have intensified, and the West has adopted intensive strategies to attack Islam, particularly after America's victory in the Cold War and its need for an enemy.
Karen Armstrong (the nun who left the convent to become a distinguished researcher and author) wrote ten years before George W. Bush launched his campaign against Islam: "It seems that the Cold War against the Soviet Union is about to be replaced by a cold war against Islam." In her book Muhammad: A Biography of the Prophet, Armstrong wrote: "In the West there is a long history of hostility towards Islam, and this hostility still continues… It was prejudice against Islam that led the Secretary General of NATO to say in 1995 that political Islam was no less dangerous to the West than communism."
Eugene Rostow(9) said: "We must realize that the differences existing between us and the Arab peoples are not differences between states or peoples; they are differences between Islamic civilization and Christian civilization… The historical circumstances confirm that America is a complementary part of the Western world, its philosophy, its creed and its system, and that makes it stand hostile to the Islamic Eastern world with its philosophy and its creed represented by the religion of Islam. America cannot but take this stand in the ranks hostile to Islam, and alongside the Western world and the Zionist state, because if it did the opposite it would be disowning its language, its philosophy, its culture and its institutions."(10)
