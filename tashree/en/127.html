The limits of Islamic legislation; where does obligation begin and where does it end? What is the role of reason and what is the role of the sacred law?

Thus, it is established that one of the requirements of obligation is that it is preceded by a warning and a clarification of the rulings that must be followed or avoided, and that revelation is the source of these legislations and obligations. So where does obligation begin and where does it end? Where does the principle: "You are more knowledgeable about your worldly affairs"(227) begin? And where does the principle ﴾Does man think that he will be left neglected?﴿(228) [al-Qiyama 36] end?

Legislation is concerned with matters related to civilization, that is, the way of life concerning the perspective on life, and the total concepts about life, whether those related to the outlook on life, or those related to establishing a system for addressing life's problems. The problems of life that require a system are those related to:

Addressing the relationships of man with his Creator through acts of worship, beliefs, vows, expiations, and divine rights.

And the relationships of man with himself, which command or prohibit him or give him choices regarding his morals, etiquette, food, drink, clothing, and adornment.

And addressing the problems that arise from the gathering of people (i.e., the relationships of man with others among humanity (transactions and punishments)), and what results from that gathering of problems that need regulations, and the need to organize interests and rights, detailing these relationships:

Family relationships and those resulting from the gathering of man and woman, such as marriage, divorce, maintenance, guardianship, kinship, and inheritance, as well as the rulings of public and private life, and mixing and seclusion (the social system).

Private relationships, in contracts and transactions that result in material or moral rights.

In the economic aspect:

What results in the need to organize how wealth is owned, what is permissible among those means and what is prohibited, and the rights resulting from that.

(227) From Musa ibn Talha from his father Talha ibn Ubayd Allah (may Allah be pleased with him) who said: "I passed by the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) with a group of people on the tops of the date palms, and he said: What are these people doing? They said: They are pollinating, they put the male in the female, and it pollinates. The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: I do not think that will benefit anything. He said: So inform them of that, and they left it. Then the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) was informed of that, and he said: If that benefits them, then let them do it, for I only thought a thought, so do not hold me accountable for my thought, but if I tell you something from Allah, then take it, for I will not lie about Allah, the Exalted." This is the wording of Muslim, and in another narration: "They mentioned that to him, and he said: I am only a human being, if I command you with something from your religion, then take it, and if I command you with something from my opinion, then I am only a human being." This is the wording of Muslim, and in another wording of Muslim: "He said: What is with your date palms? They said: You said such and such, he said: You are more knowledgeable about your worldly affairs."

(228) It is mentioned in the book of the rulings of the Qur'an by al-Shafi'i that al-Shafi'i said: And he mentioned in what he argued with the saying of Allah, the Exalted: ﴾Does man think that he will be left neglected?﴿ [He said] that the scholars of the Qur'an did not differ, as far as I know, that ﴾the neglected﴿ is that which is neither commanded nor prohibited. And whoever issues a fatwa or rules by what he was not commanded, has chosen for himself to be among the meanings of the neglected, and Allah, the Exalted, has informed him that he was not left neglected and saw that he could say what he wished, and claimed what the Qur'an was revealed contrary to. Allah (Exalted is He) said to His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him): ﴾Follow what has been revealed to you from your Lord﴿ and He, the Exalted, said: ﴾And judge between them by what Allah has revealed and do not follow their desires, and beware that they may divert you from some of what Allah has revealed to you﴿."
