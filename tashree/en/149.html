Although there is a difference between the economic system and the science of economics. The economic system is the system that explains the way of earning money and managing it, and the way of distributing wealth and managing it, and similar matters, and in this explanation, it follows a particular worldview in life.

In contrast, the science of economics is the science that studies production and its improvement, finding its means and enhancing them, and this is universal among all nations; it is not specific to one principle over another, like other sciences. When Islam addresses economics, it distinguishes between the economic system and the science of economics, and thus it does not restrict human thought in their creativity in the science of economics and ways to improve production, for example, the invention of the assembly line by the American Ford accelerated and greatly facilitated the process of car production. This matter is universal and not derived from a particular worldview in life, and thus Islam accepts it just as it accepts the Pythagorean theorem in mathematics because it is not derived from a particular worldview in life; it is not exclusive to the Greeks.

In contrast, the economic system, for example, public ownership, dealing with usury, or transactions that result in financial rights must be regulated based on a worldview in life, that is, based on Islamic belief. From here, the Islamic economic system established rules that addressed the distribution of wealth, ownership, and management in a fixed system that cannot be approached by falsehood from before it or from behind it, from an All-Knowing, All-Wise source.

From here, the doubt that some Muslims have regarding Islam's stance on the advancement of humanity on earth scientifically and industrially is dispelled. Islam does not restrict minds nor does it prevent creativity in the fields of material, industrial, and scientific forms.

As for the corrupt reality, the Sharia came to change it radically and comprehensively. It dealt with reality not on the basis of making it a source of thought but on the basis of making it a subject of thought. The society steeped in corruption did not have the Sharia come to meet it in a middle ground, but rather the Sharia completely severed ties with ignorance. It came to uproot corruption fundamentally; not to gradually eradicate it. The logic of the Sharia in this matter is: ﴾Say, "O disbelievers, I do not worship what you worship. Nor are you worshippers of what I worship."﴿, [al-Kafirun 1-3], and its logic is: ﴾And if the truth had followed their desires, the heavens and the earth and whoever is in them would have been corrupted. But We have brought them their message, and they, from their message, are turning away.﴿, [al-Mu'minun 71], and its logic is: ﴾And indeed, they were about to tempt you away from that which We have revealed to you, to fabricate about Us something else; and then they would have taken you as a friend. And if We had not made you stand firm, you would have almost inclined to them a little.﴿, [al-Isra 73], and its logic is: ﴾And I do not intend to differ with you in that which I have forbidden you. I intend only reform as much as I am able.﴿ [Hud 88].

Here we find that Islam is not responsible for the problems that arose from the application of other ideologies, whether socialism or capitalism. Islam is not responsible for finding a temporary solution to a problem, such as imposing insurance on Muslims compulsorily to solve some financial and social issues, even though it contradicts their Sharia. Nor is it responsible for finding a solution to the problem of poverty resulting from the application of the capitalist system, which has forced some Muslims into usury. If Islam were applied, it would have prohibited insurance and usury and eradicated poverty. It does not take reality as a source of thought but rather changes reality with its radical revolutionary concepts, erasing disbelief and placing Islam in a position of immediate application.

Moreover, Islam has taken into account in its legislation the realization of servitude to Allah alone. A person who legislates (creates a law) for others (outside the Islamic framework) is essentially elevating himself to the status of a deity besides Allah. Thus, Islam is a means of freeing the servants from the worship of servants.
