How did secularism stumble in addressing human problems?
Then, secularism was based on the principle of separating religion from the state, and it later evolved to become the separation of religion, values, and ethics from life. Therefore, it is necessary for the legislator, for example, or for the thought that is intended to dominate social relations, to be based on the premise that it removes from its thought, while judging a particular issue, any influence from values derived from religion, ethics, customs, or similar sources, in order to reach what is called value-free(537), value free. This value-free state is a guarantee for secularism to be neutral, so that legislations are neutral, knowledge is neutral, and the relationships that prevail in society are based on a neutral foundation. This is because their view of religion is that it is the cause of backwardness and of restraining the mind and curbing its freedom; thus, it must be set aside when judging something, so that the mind can flourish and rise without being affected by what restrains it, as well as any value that has a source from ethics, customs, humanity, traditions, or similar.
And in reality, this in itself is something that is impossible to exist in reality. Take, for example, the issue of ruling on adultery, that is, the relationship outside the framework of family and marriage. If a legislator wanted to rule on it in order to enact a law that prohibits or permits it, he would consider it a specific problem that needs an opinion. If he sets aside the religious perspective on it, based on the fact that it is prohibited, and sets aside the moral perspective, based on the fact that this relationship is immoral, and sets aside human values, based on the fact that human values may consider this relationship a waste of the dignity of the family, or the dignity of the woman, or the dignity of the man, or from another perspective, the liberal libertarian values that see this relationship as reinforcing freedom and the right to practice what achieves freedom, ... etc., he must set aside all these values whether they contradict or share in the outcome, even if they differ in their starting points. I say, when he sets all these values aside, he will not be able to issue any judgment on the matter, because the basis for judging a matter is its realization of certain values, or its prohibition because it contradicts the realization of certain values. So how can he do so while setting all values aside?
This issue makes issuing a ruling on any matter impossible. If one prohibits values based on religion and allows values based on liberalism, then without a doubt, he will fall into contradiction, as the poet said:
Is it forbidden for the nightingales to sing, but permissible for birds of every kind?(538)
As for the subject of this ruling, that is, the thing that issues a ruling on actions and objects, it is goodness and badness; because the purpose of issuing a ruling is to determine a person's stance towards the action, whether he should do it, leave it, or be given a choice between doing it and leaving it?
