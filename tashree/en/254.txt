Liberation is not achieved by decree, but rather through a well-thought-out plan.
The first step of the plan is to change the treatment; that is, addressing the humanitarian aspect represented in the society's view of slaves:
Among this is His saying, Exalted is He: ﴾ And worship Allah and do not associate anything with Him, and to parents, do good, and to relatives, orphans, the needy, the near neighbor, the neighbor farther away, the companion at your side, and the traveler, and those whom your right hands possess. Indeed, Allah does not like those who are self-deluding and boastful.﴿ [al-Nisa: 36]. In this verse, Allah has linked the obligation of doing good to those whom your right hands possess - which refers to slaves - with the obligation of worshiping Him and not associating anything with Him, placing them on the same level, and concluding the verse with a reprimand of the arrogant and boastful.
Moreover, the masters "of" the female slave are consulted regarding her marriage: ﴾ And marry those among you who are single and those who are fit among your male and female slaves. Allah knows best your faith; you are of one another. So marry them with the permission of their people and give them their due compensation in kindness.﴿ [al-Nisa: 25]. Reflect on the beautiful expression: ﴾ you are of one another﴿ brothers, and kin, and clients, not masters and slaves! They are brothers to their masters: the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) said, "Your brothers are your dependents - that is, your slaves - whom Allah has placed under your authority, and if He had willed, He could have made you under their authority. So whoever has his brother under his hand, let him feed him from what he eats, and let him clothe him from what he wears, and do not burden them with what they cannot bear. If you burden them, then assist them."
Thus, the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) placed slaves and their clients on the same level, and made those brothers to these, and established that slaves should not be deprived of anything that their clients enjoy in food, drink, and clothing; they eat what they eat and wear what they wear!
Furthermore, to enhance the feelings of the enslaved, the noble Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) said, "Let none of you say: This is my slave and this is my maid, but let him say: My young man and my young woman." Among this is also his saying - may the best prayers and peace be upon him -: "Gabriel continued to advise me regarding the enslaved until I thought that people would no longer be used or enslaved," narrated by al-Bayhaqi from Aisha with the wording: "And he continued to advise me regarding the owned until I thought that he would set a time or deadline for them, and when they reached it, they would be freed."(394) The Sharia has elevated the status of the enslaved and made them like the free, as it has made their blood sacred, and a free person is killed for killing them, for Allah says: ﴾ O you who have believed, prescribed for you is legal retribution for those murdered.﴿ [al-Baqarah: 178], and legal retribution is equal punishment and the penalty for sin. Among this is his saying (peace and blessings be upon him): "The blood of Muslims is equal," and this is general; the free and the slave are equal in that each of them has sacred blood, and killing them is forbidden, and their killer is to be killed regardless of who they are. He came to say: "Whoever kills his slave, we will kill him, and whoever mutilates his slave, we will mutilate him, and whoever castrates his slave, we will castrate him." This was authenticated by al-Hakim and graded as good by al-Tirmidhi, and it is a clear principle indicating the human equality between the enslaved and their masters, and it explicitly states the guarantees surrounding the life of this group of people - whose status does not remove them from their original human characteristics - which are complete and comprehensive guarantees, reaching an astonishing level that no other legislation regarding slavery in all of history, neither before Islam nor after it, has ever reached, as it made mere striking of a slave without discipline
