Fourth: A foundational principle, represented in a comprehensive, ultimate intellectual belief:
A person continually asks questions that explain existence and their role within it; these questions form what is called the great dilemma, and the answers to them provide a solution to this great dilemma, which we call belief (aqeedah). This leads to faith, which enables a person to free themselves from agnosticism and degradation, allowing them to ascend the heights of progress and elevation to the noble status for which they were honored!
The answer to these questions constitutes a solution to the great dilemma, resulting in a comprehensive idea we call belief (aqeedah), as the heart will be bound to it after the mind has contemplated it. Because this idea resolves the great dilemma and answers its existential questions, it will inherently be foundational ideas, meaning that the subsidiary ideas that stem from it (the legislations) will rely on it. They will serve as a measure for ideas and actions, branching out from it, being organized by it, and will motivate individuals to adopt principled stances in life. This means that "legislations" will emerge from it or be built upon it, regulating individual behavior and societal and international relations, and determining the way of living!
From here, belief (aqeedah) is the comprehensive idea about the universe, humanity, and life, and about what precedes and follows it, and about its relationship with what precedes and follows it. Thus, belief (aqeedah) is a fundamental idea about a human living in the universe. This fundamental idea, due to its engagement with existential and essential issues concerning humanity, the universe, and life, and due to the emergence of subsidiary ideas from it, can only be comprehensive, meaning it contains standards and reference rulings for the problems of humanity and society. These are inclusive ideas (shumuli) that pertain to humans as humans, encompassing all individuals without examining one individual over another (viewing humans as humans, not as Zayd or Abid, nor as a specific nationality, nor as a time without another time or a place without another place). Therefore, they are elevated ideas that uplift humanity.
The comprehensive belief must contain the potential for a system to emerge from it that organizes societal relations based on it(433), ensuring the proper discipline of individuals within society according to a specific system that arises from a belief they have embraced. This system adjudicates among people when their interests conflict or their perspectives on this system clash, clarifying what is suitable for their community and their gatherings in terms of a system, that is, defining what is called "public interest" (maslaha), which will serve as the foundation for achieving interests and averting evils in a manner that is not influenced by the whims of one group at the expense of another, or by the narrow perspective of one group that contradicts that of another. Otherwise, a belief that does not give rise to a system of life, or if the system that emerges from it is not comprehensive of all aspects of life (beyond personal affairs and acts of worship, for example), will merely be a spiritual, priestly belief, unsuitable for governing people and guiding them towards their renaissance. It will not be comprehensive unless it contains standards and reference rulings for the problems of humanity and society. On the other hand, a society whose systems are not connected to the belief it holds will find no intrinsic motivation to adhere to those systems and will live in contradiction between what its beliefs dictate and the systems it experiences that are not connected to them.

(433) The principle (ideology) is defined as a rational belief from which a system emerges. The comprehensive belief provides a total explanation for what is perceived by the senses or its effects in the universe, humanity, and life. If a comprehensive system emerges from it to address the problems of life and society, it transforms into a principle that serves as a foundation for the revival of communities.
