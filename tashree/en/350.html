It is clear that any human attempt at legislation – no matter how advanced in technique or methodology – will remain deficient, influenced, prone to contradictions, and perpetually unjust, simply because it originates from the limited mind that is incapable of determining good and evil, or comprehending all the outcomes and consequences of laws, or resolving disputes among the various issues related to the matter that affect it or are affected by it or are intended to be achieved. In contrast, the divine legislation revealed by the Creator, Exalted is He, possesses all the qualities that guarantee its justice, permanence, and absolute suitability, making it the sole true reference for organizing human life in every time and place, as it contains complete knowledge of the state of humanity and the outcomes of actions, balances rights and duties with precision, and combines wisdom, mercy, facilitation, and the alleviation of hardship simultaneously. Therefore, legislation must truly belong solely to the Creator, Exalted is He, for the divine law, comprehensive, just, and eternal, is the only valid framework for guiding humanity. Allah, Blessed and Exalted, said: ﴾Indeed, judgment belongs to Allah﴿.
