And since belief is a comprehensive idea about the universe, humanity, and worldly life, it connects worldly life with what came before it and what comes after it.

As for the connection with what came before it: that is, the connection of the Creator with the creation: it is the connection of creation, that is, bringing into existence from non-existence, and the connection of organization, whether the organization of the universe according to certain laws and principles, and that Allah, Exalted is He, is the Creator, the Omnipotent.

As for the connection with what comes after it: it is the connection of the Creator with the creation through holding them accountable, rewarding them, and punishing them, and seeking His pleasure (and this characteristic makes Islamic belief a spiritual belief),

And as for the connection with worldly life itself, it is the connection of the Creator with the creation through sending messengers and organizing life with commands and prohibitions, (and this characteristic makes Islamic belief a political belief):

Secondly: it is a political belief:

That is, it is connected to worldly life, that is, to overseeing matters related to every action, word, deed, and intellectual, political, social, or economic behavior in the life we live, with specific ideas and certain rulings, such as marriage, divorce, accountability, punishments, jihad, and buying and leasing. It is a political belief that addresses all the problems of life with solutions legislated by Allah, the Exalted, through revelation, both the Qur'an and the Sunnah, and governs people and oversees their affairs with the system that emanates from it or is built upon it, with obligations of commands and prohibitions, regarding the rulings related to relationships with Allah, Exalted is He, from acts of worship, beliefs, vows, expiations, and divine rights, and from the relationships of a person with himself, to command him and prohibit him or give him choices regarding his morals, etiquette, food, drink, clothing, adornment, and the rulings related to morals, modesty, virtues, good conduct, and its vices, which are called: etiquette, and addressing the problems that arise from the gathering of people (that is, the relationships of a person with others from among humanity (transactions and punishments)), and what results from that gathering of problems that need regulations, such as regulating the internal order among people, or those relationships that arise from removing harm and transgression from others (punishments, evidence, recompense, rights, and judiciary), and the need to organize interests and rights, that is, to regulate people's economic activities in contracts, transactions, and punishments, and their dealings with one another in wealth and rights, and their actions through contracting and others, and resolving their disputes through the judiciary, or the rulings related to social relationships that need to create social balance, such as social solidarity, welfare, and the like, or the rulings related to family relationships or those arising from the gathering of a woman with a man, such as marriage, divorce, maintenance, guardianship, kinship, and inheritance, and the rulings of public and private life, mixing and seclusion, or the rules related to the authority of the ruler over the subjects, and the reciprocal rights and duties between them, (that is, the legitimate politics or the sultanic rulings), that is, addressing the problems that arise from the gathering of people in public relations, that is, the relationships between the ruler and the ruled, that is, constitutional relationships and relationships with the public authority, and the systems of judiciary and evidence, and the rulings of penalties, punishments, rights, security, and authority, and the legislation of public funds from taxes, revenues, and duties, and the funds of public ownership and state funds, and holding rulers accountable, and the rulings concerning the People of the Book, and the relationships that arise between the Islamic state and the rest of the world, that is, international relations, the relationships of groups, nations, and states with one another, in the rulings of treaties, jihad, messengers, and embassies.
