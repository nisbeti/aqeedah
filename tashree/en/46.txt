The civilizational identity of the Islamic Ummah is a guarantee of progress, revival, and goodness!
The distinctive civilizational identity of a nation is formed by its possession of a specific vision of life, based on a set of beliefs, convictions, perceptions, philosophy, knowledge, social values, arts, ethics, and traditions; that is, on a creed from which a comprehensive legislation emerges that shapes all systems of life (economic, political, social, etc.); a system that provides solutions for the nation's management of its material wealth for utilization and benefit, to serve the vital interests of the Ummah, and establishes the necessary programs for scientific and technical discoveries, for industrial inventions, and legislations that achieve equality and parity in dealings to ensure the realization of rights and the fulfillment of duties owed to humanity. These are legislations that are uniform wherever the nature of those addressed by them necessitates uniformity in powers and capacities, which affects the nature of rights and duties, and they take into account differences with specific rulings when their nature requires such differences. It is unjust for two parties that differ in powers and capacities to share the same obligations and responsibilities, and it is just for the legislation to address the deficiencies of anyone whose capacities or nature are lacking, by assigning others to bear burdens that ensure those who are deficient in their capacities can enjoy parity in achieving the objectives of the legislations of justice among all, and equality in their social and human value, regardless of those differences in natural capacities. Also, those who excel should utilize what they have over others to address the deficiencies of those others, so the female can compensate for the deficiencies in the nature and capacities of the male in certain matters, and the male can compensate for the deficiencies inherent in the female, thus they are equal in value and duties and complement each other, leading to a proper livelihood ﴾as Allah has favored some of them over others﴿.
