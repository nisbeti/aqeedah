Conclusion
{In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. * Praise be to Allah, the Lord of the worlds. * The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. * Master of the Day of Judgment. * You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help. * Guide us on the straight path. * The path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not of those who have evoked [Your] anger or of those who are astray.} Amen.
O Allah, send blessings and peace upon Your best creation, our Prophet Muhammad ibn Abdullah, and upon his family and companions, and grant him the prayer of the inhabitants of the heavens and the earth upon him. And bestow, O Lord, Your hidden kindness upon our affairs by virtue of Your mercy, generosity, and kindness, O Generous One.
O Allah, I ask You by You, O Lord of the worlds. I ask You by Your greatest name, which if You are called upon by it, You answer, and if I seek forgiveness by it, You forgive, and if I seek mercy by it, You have mercy, and if I seek healing by it, You heal, O Answerer of the supplications of the supplicants:
I ask You, O Lord, by You, O You who there is no deity but You, the Lord who is called upon. I ask You, O Master of the needs of those who ask, who gives when asked, and does not increase in generosity and kindness except with the abundance of requests, and does not increase in favor and goodness except with the persistence of supplication. The one burdened with worries comes to Him complaining, and He relieves their distress, and the one drowning in sins seeks forgiveness, and He forgives their missteps, and the one despairing of salvation, He extends to them the hand of salvation. When the servants do wrong, He is forbearing and gives them time, and when they do good, He bestows and accepts, and when they disobey, He conceals, and when they sin, He pardons and forgives. And when they call upon Him, He answers, and He is closer to them than their jugular vein, and more merciful to them than a parent to their child. And when they call upon Him, He hears them, and when they turn to Him, He hastens to them. Whoever draws near to Him by a handspan, He draws near to them by an arm's length, and whoever comes to Him walking, He comes to them running. And if they turn away from Him, He is generous and bestows and calls them, and He does not close His door before them. He is severe in punishment, yet He is the Most Forgiving, the Most Merciful.
For every one who seeks mercy from Him, there is mercy, and for every one who desires closeness to Him, there is nearness. His blessings and favors have continued to flow until the souls are reassured by their continuity, and His bounties have manifested until His allies have acknowledged their shortcoming in fulfilling His rights. He has bestowed His blessings upon them, both outwardly and inwardly. I ask You by whom the lessons have manifested until the silent have spoken with His proof, and everything written on the pages of the stars of the heavens and the grains of sand on the surface of the earth points to His great power, and He has shown signs until the heavens and the earth have declared His evidence, and He has subdued by His great power until everything has submitted to His might, and faces have humbled before His greatness.
I ask You, O Allah, by You to send down upon us the torrents of Your mercy, and to accept our deeds and make them sincere for Your sake, and to conceal us in this world and the Hereafter. O Allah, I ask You for us and for all Muslims for the best of what Your beloved, the chosen one (peace and blessings be upon him), and Your righteous servants have asked You, and I seek refuge with You for them and for us from the evil of what Your beloved, the chosen one (peace and blessings be upon him), and Your righteous servants sought refuge from.
I ask You for all good, both immediate and delayed, what we know of it and what we do not know, and I seek refuge with You from all evil, both immediate and delayed, what we know of it and what we do not know.
And may Allah send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, the best of Your creation, and upon his family and companions, and grant him the prayer of the inhabitants of the heavens and the earth upon him. And grant, O Lord, Your hidden kindness in our affairs.
And our last supplication is that all praise is due to Allah, Lord of the worlds.
Praise be to Allah for His blessings, and we ask Him, the Exalted, to make the truth flow from our tongues and our hands, and to protect us from error, and to overlook us by His mercy, favor, and generosity. For indeed, we love Him for His exalted essence, despite our shortcomings, our straying from the path, and our immersion in worldly matters.
