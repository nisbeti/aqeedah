Its first aspect: Distribution of duties, rights, and responsibilities according to ability:
In addition to command, prohibition, and choice, Islamic legislation distributes duties, rights, and responsibilities among the individual, family, community, and state according to capability and ability, which ensures the individual's participation in achieving the welfare and security of the community and preserving its values, and the responsibilities of the community and state in solving the individual's problems, and distributing wealth fairly to bridge the gap between the segments of society (for example: enjoining good and forbidding wrong, social solidarity, and the spoils ﴾What Allah has given as spoils to His Messenger from the people of the towns, it is for Allah and for the Messenger and for [his] near relatives and the orphans, the needy, and the traveler, so that it will not be a perpetual distribution among the rich among you﴿ [al-Hashr 7]). Al-Bukhari (may Allah have mercy on him) narrated that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say, "I heard the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) say, 'Each of you is a shepherd, and each of you is responsible for his flock. The imam is a shepherd and responsible for his flock, and a man is a shepherd in his family and is responsible for his flock, and a woman is a shepherd in her husband's house and is responsible for her flock, and a servant is a shepherd in the property of his master and is responsible for his flock.' He said, 'And I thought he said, "And a man is a shepherd in the property of his father and is responsible for his flock." And each of you is a shepherd and responsible for his flock.'" And Allah, Exalted is He, said: ﴾Men are protectors and maintainers of women because Allah has made some of them to excel others and because they spend out of their wealth﴿ [an-Nisa 34], meaning they are responsible for fulfilling their needs and taking care of their affairs. When Islam assigned the man the duty of guardianship over the woman (wife, sister, daughter, or divorced women who have rights over him), the meaning of guardianship is the emphasis on attending to their needs, striving for their interests, and the duty of caring for them, along with the responsibilities that arise from that, such as labor and effort in the world, and striving for livelihood that the man undertakes, and effort and movement to fulfill the material and psychological care or others for women, and standing up against idleness, which necessitates constant and diligent movement to ensure proper care. It is a responsibility that involves giving hardship and toil to the man, and comfort to the woman, and it is a serious and important responsibility that honors the woman, making her free to perform her societal, familial, and personal roles comfortably, given that there is someone to attend to all her needs. When Islam assigned this duty to the man, He said: ﴾Because Allah has made some of them to excel others﴿, thus considering the man's physical abilities and psychological readiness to face the challenges of life outside the home, and considering the woman's physical abilities and psychological readiness to face the challenges of life voluntarily outside the home, and her duties inside the home to build the family, so that the man can work outside the home while being assured that his family is protected thanks to the woman, and that she is a comfort for him, and he is a comfort for her. This also falls under the category of: ﴾Because Allah has made some of them to excel others﴿. Thus, the complementarity between them was the foundation for establishing a healthy life that provides them with security, tranquility, and psychological comfort. The preference of one over the other was in certain aspects for men and in other aspects for women, and each was given characteristics that qualify them to play that role, such as physical strength, decisiveness, and firmness for men(282), and emotional strength, tenderness, and compassion for women. The complementarity by benefiting from these virtues is the optimal solution for the relationship between them, not the destruction of these virtues and equality between them without considering what each excels in physically and psychologically over the other to utilize it in the best way.
