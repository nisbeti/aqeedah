The famous saying indicates that those who engage in this do not intend to nullify it; rather, the one who seeks to nullify it aims to mix truth with falsehood to mislead others into thinking he seeks the truth. Most types of misguidance that have entered Islam are of the kind that involves mixing truth with falsehood. Ibn Adel said: "The 'ba' in the phrase ﴾بِٱلْبَٰطِلِ﴿ indicates attachment, as in saying: I mixed water with milk, meaning: do not mix truth with falsehood, so that it does not become indistinguishable." This is because souls recoil from falsehood due to its nature and the apparent corruption within it, and there is no way to accept it except through ignorance of its reality by those who do not verify, or if the motivation for acceptance is desires and corrupted taste, or if it becomes mixed for the sincere and the general public with the truth, causing confusion. Therefore, there is a prohibition against mixing truth with falsehood so that falsehood does not become indistinguishable for anyone who might accept it, and this is the entry point for most types of misguidance that have entered Islam. Thus, the evil of that mixing is indeed great!

The Islamic methodology clarifies that truth does not triumph through falsehood, so that people become confused. Indeed, the reliance on falsehood often begins with a fingertip, then an inch, then an arm, and then a span. Rather, truth must be distinguished from falsehood by its strong characteristics embodied in it as truth, with its values that aim to defeat falsehood. It does not merely suffice to completely abandon falsehood, but rather it declares a comprehensive war against it until it is uprooted!

And when the truth is characterized by its attribute of being the truth, it does not approach or draw near to falsehood even by the slightest measure, "for there is nothing between truth and falsehood except falsehood, and the truth does not descend even by the width of a finger towards falsehood without departing from its attribute"(75)! Thus, our Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) taught us when he absolutely rejected every form of convergence with falsehood at the beginning of the path, in the middle of it, or at its end, and when he rejected compromises, even in the direst situations of weakness and need,

The truth is clear, bright, and shining, a pure white path with no ash or darkness in it. Although the truth is a tremendous force due to the value it represents, it requires its adherents to detach themselves from their own desires, to endure hardships and harm, to be patient and steadfast, and to remain vigilant, believing in the promise of the truth that victory will come to the truth, completely and fully submitting to Allah and His guidance. This complete submission is one of the requirements of faith,

With this submission, the Sharia (Islamic law) abolished wine, gambling, idols, and arrows for divination, as well as the customs and values of the pre-Islamic era, along with its leaders and chieftains, in the blink of an eye from the society that had been established in opposition to that before Islam. Were it not for this, nothing of it would have been abolished to this day,

Therefore, Muslims must fiercely combat any attempt to dilute Islam, and any attempt to adopt any values from other civilizations, or other religions, or other cultures,

Islam has come with a practical, realistic, comprehensive, and integrated approach to govern life, to change human perspective, and to regulate behavior, through governance, authority, empowerment, and vicegerency, and through the reform and purification of the individual, so that Islamic civilization can achieve justice and fairness, establish the truth, and nullify falsehood, so that humanity may find happiness on earth and fulfill the purpose for which it was created!
