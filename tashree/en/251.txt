It is upon the ruler when he manifests a system other than the system of Islam (clear disbelief), and the individuals' rebellion against society(389), and the rulings regarding the organization of judicial oversight over the administrative actions of the state, and the oversight of the judiciary of grievances, and the oversight of judicial integrity,(390) and the market regulation as an oversight judicial institution to maintain order, and the role of the community and political parties in holding the ruler accountable, and the rulings regarding advising rulers and commanding them to do good and forbidding them from wrongdoing, and their role in ensuring the supremacy of the value system upon which the state was established (commanding good and forbidding wrongdoing, which is a unique characteristic of the Islamic state compared to other systems in establishing responsibilities at all levels to ensure that these values remain dominant and supreme in society and the state).
Thus, there are community responsibilities represented in advising, guiding, educating, holding accountable, commanding good and forbidding wrongdoing, and enjoining and changing (by hand or by tongue or by heart), and the clarity of the role of the individual, scholars, political parties, and the state in it, to constrain the truth, and to prevent associating with the doer of wrongdoing, to raising the sword and using force to change wrongdoing, to the responsibilities of the state regarding punishments, and other frameworks that maintain the integrity of the set of values, standards, and convictions that stem from the creed and form the organizing framework of the Sharia and its rulings, which create a specific way of life, and a system through which a person can be rectified and find happiness, and a system of customs that its opposite is considered a condemned wrongdoing that is fought against and rejected!
Therefore, legislation encompasses this matter and establishes the mechanisms and guarantees, such as the right to consultation, for example, and surrounds it with various forms of accountability previously mentioned, and the obligation to establish political parties, and the role of scholars, and the rulings regarding rebellion against the ruler, and how to depose him, and what are the criteria that determine when to resort to any of them, “and that we do not dispute the matter with its people, except that you see clear disbelief with you from Allah for which there is proof.” Narrated by al-Bukhari, and the state establishes the necessary institutions to properly implement those rulings, thus taking care of the affairs of the community with the right of guardianship that Allah has granted it: “All of you are guardians and all of you are responsible for your charges; the imam is a guardian and responsible for his charges.” Narrated by al-Bukhari, all of this ensures the complete effectiveness of Islam when applied. The community is responsible for forming political parties that ensure the maintenance of the community's thought and holding its rulers accountable, and is responsible for leading the community to ensure the integrity of the set of concepts, standards, and convictions that the community has adopted and which it pledged allegiance to the ruler to govern based on them, and the Sharia has made the ruler responsible for implementing and executing the legal rulings, and has granted him powers and capabilities that ensure the proper application of that, including powers and institutions that assist him, and police to punish those who deviate from that system, and so forth. The Sharia has balanced between the strength of the community and its entity in preserving the values of the Islamic state, led by political parties, scholars, and those with authority in the community, and between the executive entity of the state with its institutions and the power it possesses (military and police to ensure the application of rulings and prevent deviation from them). Islam has prohibited power from being the authority; rather, it has distinguished between authority, which is the guardianship of affairs, and power in any clear distinction. Thus, the Islamic state preceded Western states in establishing the correct conception for the cohesion of the state and achieving the responsibility of the integrity of legislative values within it and ensuring that there is no deviation from them, and ensuring oversight to prevent.
