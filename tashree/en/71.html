The loss of something from the Sunnah necessitates the loss of its role as an explanation of something from the religion that was revealed in the Qur'an, and this means that the Qur'an is no longer an explainer (for the Qur'an has made the Sunnah an explanation), ﴾Say, "Obey Allah and obey the Messenger. And there is not upon the Messenger except the [duty of] clear notification."﴿, [54 An-Nur]. The word "clear" is a descriptive term that implies a condition for conveying the message; thus, the message cannot be clear, and the causes of guidance are not complete in the absence of explanation and by relying solely on the general or ambiguous aspects found in the Qur'an, in details that have been lost. Therefore, in those details, the proof cannot be established by relying solely on the general aspects found in the Qur'an!

This means that the religion is preserved from loss, and it is evidence that the Sunnah (traditions of the Prophet) is preserved from loss; it has all been transmitted to us, as it contains the explanation of the Qur'an, the detailing of its generalities, the restriction of its absolutes, the clarification of its terms, and the elucidation of its rulings and meanings. Through it, the religion has been completed, and our task is to sift the authentic from the weak, given that most of the legal rulings have been derived from the Sunnah, considering that the Sunnah clarifies and explains the Qur'an and connects the branches to their roots, and legislates new rulings that have no basis in the Qur'an. Through it, the religion has been established, and the blessing has been completed, and Allah has chosen Islam for us as a religion, with all that it contains and the rulings in the Qur'an. Allah, Exalted is He, said: ﴾And We have sent down to you the Reminder that you may make clear to the people what was sent down to them and perhaps they will give thought﴿, and He said: ﴾And We did not send down to you the Book except to make clear to them that in which they have differed and as guidance and mercy for a people who believe﴿.
