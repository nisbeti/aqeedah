Why all this fear of Islam?

One of the prominent figures in strategic planning for the United States, the famous contemporary Zionist orientalist "Bernard Lewis," said: "Our custom in the Western world has been that: the more the Easterners turn to us, the more we cling to the West to make ourselves an example of virtue and progress. If they imitate us, we consider that a good thing, and if they do not, we consider that bad and evil; progress is in imitating us, while if they do not follow us, that is regression and decline!! However, this is not necessarily the case, for when two civilizations collide, one dominates and the other is shattered, idealists and thinkers may eloquently and easily talk about a mingling of the best elements of both civilizations, yet the ordinary result of this encounter is coexistence between the worst elements of both."(2)

Bernard Lewis also wrote: "Today's terrorism is part of a long struggle between Islam and the West... The moral system upon which Islam is based is different from that of Western Christian/Jewish civilization, and the verses of the Qur'an validate the practice of violence against non-Muslims... This war is a war between religions"(3).

The American strategic thinker "Fukuyama" wrote: "The current conflict is not simply against terrorism, but against the Islamic fundamentalist doctrine, which stands against Western modernity and against the secular state, and this fundamentalist ideology represents a more fundamental danger than the communist threat, and what is required is a war within Islam, so that it accepts Western modernity and Western secularism and the Christian principle: 'Render unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's, and unto God the things that are God's'"(4)!
