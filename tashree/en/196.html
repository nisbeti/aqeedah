And here the flaw arose in the research and in the thinking, and as a result a flaw arose in confidence in the rulings of Islam, whose remedy differs from the remedy of Western rulings and legislation.

The role of the intellect, when it investigates remedies in the Sharia, was never to be a judge or a source, even partially, of the rulings by which reality is to be treated. It was merely a tool used to understand reality and to understand what Allah intends by the legislative texts with which the revelation came down. The mujtahid proceeds with the Sharia evidences according to their linguistic meaning and their understood and reasonable implications to wherever they guide him until they bring him to the Sharia ruling, without having a preconceived opinion about what the Sharia ruling ought to be. The text is the evidence, not the intellect. The intellect follows the evidence, and the evidence does not follow the intellect, because Allah, Exalted is He, is the Judge, not the intellect: ﴾The decision is only for Allah﴿.
