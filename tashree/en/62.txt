Any methodology that leads to corruption and the shedding of blood is the case for every methodology based on legislating for people and their whims, in accordance with His saying, Exalted is He: ﴾And if the truth had followed their desires, the heavens and the earth and whoever is in them would have certainly been corrupted﴿, ﴾Then We placed you upon a legislation from the command, so follow it and do not follow the desires of those who do not know﴿. Thus, deviating from the legislation of Allah is to follow the whims of the legislators, which leads to corruption on earth, oppression, and injustice. Therefore, the caliph referred to is the one who establishes the methodology of Allah that ensures the realization of truth and justice according to the Sharia of Allah, by applying His legislation. This is the one whom Allah, Exalted is He, has appointed: the believers establish His legislation in every aspect of their lives and pledge allegiance to a caliph who applies it among them in their societal relations. Imam al-Qurtubi (may Allah have mercy on him) said: "This verse is a foundation for appointing an imam and caliph to whom obedience is due; so that the word may unite and the rulings of the caliph may be executed. There is no disagreement among the ummah or the imams regarding the obligation of this except what was narrated about al-Asamm, who was deaf to the Sharia." This reflects the precision of Imam al-Qurtubi (may Allah be pleased with him)! Islam has made the authority belong to the ummah, which appoints a ruler to govern them by the legislation of their Lord, as has been established from the derivation of the principles of the system of governance in Islam. The mentioned caliph is the believers who follow the legislation of Allah, Exalted is He, in what He has commanded them to do in their lives, establishing His methodology among them. For the methodology to be established in individuals does not establish it in society, and if it is not established in society, their adherence to it(118) will not be realized, nor will justice be achieved. Therefore, it necessitates the establishment of the methodology in individuals, society, and the state. Since most of these rulings are applied through the state, the people pledge allegiance to a caliph who establishes it among them. Thus, making the caliph the ruler is also the meaning of His saying, Exalted is He: ﴾And when your Lord said to the angels, "Indeed, I will make upon the earth a caliph"﴿, meaning that through him the meaning of succession is realized in a way that leads to the objective of that succession and to the realization of the honor in succession, as indicated by the verse as derived by al-Qurtubi, in accordance with His saying, Exalted is He: ﴾Then We made you successors upon the earth after them to see how you would act﴿ [Yunus 14](119). This means that you apply the rulings of Allah and the methodology of Allah among yourselves in every matter, and the caliphate specifically in governance, by the ruler who rules by what Allah has revealed. For this methodology is what distinguishes whether the one appointed as a successor on earth is corrupting it, shedding blood, or whether he is a caliph who is safeguarded from such error by that methodology. And for the methodology to prevail, it must prevail through a state, not merely through individuals adhering to it in a society where another methodology prevails! And for the meaning of succession to be realized as an honor for the successor, there is no honor for one who does not establish the methodology of Allah! There is no honor for one who corrupts and sheds blood!
Thus, it is necessary that there must be revelation, miracle, message, and legislation, and that the message be the final reference for determining what must be believed regarding the unseen matters that cannot be perceived by the senses or their effects, and what must be organized in life according to it from methodology.

(118) The cornerstone of the identity of society is the manner in which relationships are managed, and the systems that regulate these relationships. For example, usurious relationships are the result of applying a capitalist system; they cannot change in society as long as the society remains capitalist. The refusal of millions of Muslims in Western societies or even in Islamic countries where the system of usury prevails to engage in usury does not change the capitalist economic system. Indeed, their money will undoubtedly enter the banks, and the banks will invest it legally in whatever the bank sees fit, mixing the money of Muslims with usury and with the trade of alcohol, and the investments that banks make in nightclubs, whether Muslims in the West like it or not. Moreover, they will be subject to state laws regarding mandatory insurance, taxes, and systems prohibited in Islam, among other things. Therefore, the essence lies in the relationships and systems, not in the beliefs of individuals.
