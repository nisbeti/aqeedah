Islam provides rulings on actions that will satisfy the manifestations of instincts and organic needs, and details the manner of conducting transactions (such as sale and marriage; that is, contracts: their pillars, subject matter, formulas, influencing factors, conditions, and annulment, etc.). For example, it addresses the manner of acquiring (possessing) these items (such as a car or onions) that will satisfy one's hunger, and how one may act with them (such as selling, consuming, or stealing, for instance). Detailed rulings are given that permit sale and prohibit usury, prohibit theft and plunder, and provide detailed rulings on types of sales and the ruling of each one (such as salam sale, sale of stolen goods, sale of usurped property, sale of unknown items, etc.). It also provides rulings related to the growth of wealth resulting from those transactions, such as trade (thus providing rulings on zakat, prohibition of hoarding, etc.), and rulings on the guarantees of rights arising from those actions (such as the right of a worker to wages). These rulings remain fixed until the Day of Judgment; the rulings on sales do not change simply because we sell airplanes instead of camels!
Since the ways to satisfy these organic needs and instincts are limited, and the actions that a person undertakes for satisfaction are limited, they fall under similar categories such as buying and selling, marriage, and the regulations governing these actions (like contracts in buying or marriage or the appointment of a ruler in the state, for example) are limited, thus it is possible to define the stance on them and regulate them, which gives the Sharia (Islamic law) the ability to provide fixed rulings and establish permanent solutions for them,
Because it addressed the treatments from a perspective that can be confined and given appropriate and established rulings for them,
and which is also characterized by its ability to find balance in these fulfillments, achieving stability, happiness, and equilibrium in the satisfaction of human needs,
So that these solutions align with human living in society, the perspective on the individual should not overshadow the perspective on the community, nor should the interests of the group be sacrificed for the benefit of an individual, party, or influential faction, and the individual should not be crushed in the name of the community, thus extending balance and stability to the relationships within society as well.
The legislation has achieved the interests of each individual in particular, such that no group in society monopolizes rights over another group. It was thus honest and just, unaffected by any "personal" interests, and did not favor one group over another to fulfill the desires of the influential in society; rather, it was completely neutral.
This ensures the validity of the Islamic system for humanity in any place or time, regardless of their nationality, ethnicity, homeland, or location.
