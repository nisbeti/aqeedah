The follower must imitate on the condition that he knows the evidence of the jurist, whereas the layperson imitates without any condition or restriction), and the conditions and attributes of the imitators.

(220) The term "issue" here refers to any action or set of actions upon which the validity of others depends, ... Examples include: ablution (wudu): actions upon which the validity of others depends because the validity of prayer depends on ablution; therefore, ablution is not considered an issue according to the definition, but it is regarded as a part of prayer that is necessary for its validity. Prayer consists of actions that do not depend on the validity of others; it is an issue, and everything that is necessary for its validity, such as the pillars and conditions of validity like purity and facing the qibla, is considered part of it ... Accordingly, if a person imitates a jurist in prayer, he must imitate him in all its components, such as ablution, washing from major impurity, dry ablution (tayammum), facing the qibla, and the pillars of prayer. (Taysir al-Wusul ila al-Usul by the scholar Ata Abu al-Rashta, part two, chapter: Definition of the issue)
