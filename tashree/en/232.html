"And the prayer of a man in the depths of the night,"(369) for every sin, no matter how great or small, apparent or hidden, is considered a social transgression, and its effects cannot be removed except through compensation for the community."(370)

Consider how Islam has made the categories of the public treasury (bayt al-mal) and the distributions of zakat for the poor, the needy, the wayfarer, and the indebted as shares, and that zakat is not a scattered charity nor does it humiliate the poor; rather, it is a social obligation, and there is a known right for the poor in the wealth of the rich, in his crops, animals, and trade, and it is a means by which the indebted can settle their debts.

Social solidarity leads to addressing conditions of incapacity and need, and to remedying all types of weakness, whatever their cause; it is a means to combat poverty and incapacity. It also leads to cooperation, the alleviation of harm, the prevention of envy and animosity, and disparity among individuals in society, and it contributes to fulfilling needs, aiding the distressed, lifting oppression, and cooperating in righteousness and piety.

This is a brief overview that does not encompass all the rulings on social solidarity in Islam, but it gives you a glimpse of this system and how the specific rulings contribute to establishing a comprehensive system, and how Islam addresses problems within the framework of achieving societal value systems that bring happiness to individuals and establish communities on foundations of solidarity, compassion, and responsibilities that lead to the advancement and revival of those communities!
