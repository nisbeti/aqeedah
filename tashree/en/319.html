The fourth detail: practical application: the comprehensiveness of legislation for the systems of the state and society:

It has clarified for us the method and mechanisms that ensure the proper implementation and progress of this application.

Detailed verses regarding legislation have been revealed:

political (system of governance)

and we have derived from them the concepts that define the constitution, that is: the totality of rules that the authority compels people to follow in their relationships, and which define the systems of governance based on legislations and laws that clarify the form and nature of the state, its rules and pillars, and the foundation upon which it stands, (that is: "the intellectual foundation, which is the set of concepts, standards, and convictions that govern affairs according to which individual rights are defined, and the political relationship between the state as an authority that oversees the affairs of people, and protects their rights and cares for them(470) and between them"), in accordance with concepts that regulate public relations, that is, constitutional relations, and regulate the relationships between the shepherd (that is, the public authority) and the flock, and define the concepts and responsibilities of the state in its role as a caretaker, and clarify the limits and jurisdiction of each authority in the state.

And the rulings on consultation, decision-making, obedience, authority, security, and sovereignty, (that is, who has the final say in disputes and discussions on various matters(471), and in the sense of who holds the upper hand in the state and outside of it?),

and it regulates the relationships among people themselves, including systems of social interaction, education, economy, foreign policy, and governance.

It elaborates on the rulings regarding rebellion against those rulings and against the state, and the rulings on protecting the state, (the criminal legislations specific to the state), and the rulings on the rebellion of the subjects against the state, and the rulings on the ruler's deviation from the value system upon which the state was established, especially the rebellion against the ruler when manifesting another system other than the system of Islam (clear disbelief), and the role of the nation and parties in holding the ruler accountable, and the rulings on advising rulers and commanding them to do good and forbidding them from wrongdoing, and their role in ensuring the supremacy of the value system upon which the state was established (commanding good and forbidding wrongdoing, which is a unique characteristic of the Islamic state compared to other systems in establishing responsibilities at all levels to ensure that those values remain dominant and supreme in society and the state)

and the rulings on organizing judicial oversight over the administrative actions of the state, and the oversight of the judiciary of grievances, and the oversight of judicial integrity,(472)

and it details the method of choosing the ruler, the method of appointing the caliph (the pledge of allegiance), the rulings on obedience, the rulings on the absence of a rightful claimant for the pledge of allegiance, the method of his dismissal, the rulings on the multiplicity of caliphs, the killing of the second caliph, and the rulings on dividing the ranks of Muslims by establishing a second entity for them, and the powers of the ruler, and the governing and administrative bodies that constitute the government and organize its work, and its authorities,
