Foreword

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

O Allah, to You belongs all praise; You are the Sustainer of the heavens and the earth and all that is in them. And to You belongs all praise; Yours is the dominion of the heavens and the earth and all that is in them. And to You belongs all praise; You are the Light of the heavens and the earth and all that is in them. And to You belongs all praise; You are the King of the heavens and the earth. And to You belongs all praise; You are the Truth, and Your promise is true, and meeting You is true, and Your word is true, and Paradise is true, and Hellfire is true, and the Prophets are true, and Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) is true, and the Hour is true. O Allah, to You I have submitted, and in You I have believed, and upon You I have relied, and to You I have turned, and with You I have disputed, and to You I have referred for judgment. So forgive me for what I have done in the past and what I have delayed, and what I have concealed and what I have disclosed; You are the Expediter and You are the Delayer; there is no deity except You.

All praise is due to Allah, the Exalted, who has opened the hearts of the people of Islam with guidance, and has sealed the hearts of the people of tyranny so they do not comprehend wisdom at all. And I bear witness that there is no deity except Allah, alone without partner, a singular God, eternal and absolute. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, how honored he is as a servant and a master, and how great his lineage and origin, and how pure his resting place and birth, and how magnificent his chest and source. May Allah send blessings upon him and upon his family and companions, like the rains of mercy and the floods of victory, with prayers and peace that endure from today until the Day the people are resurrected(1).

All praise is due to Allah, the One who has bestowed favors and bounties, and strength and power, the One of grace and generosity, the God of the earth and the heavens, who honors Islam with His victory, and humiliates polytheism with His might, and directs affairs by His command, and maintains blessings through gratitude to Him, and leads the disbelievers astray by His cunning, the One who has decreed the days as cycles with His justice, and has made the outcome for the righteous by His grace, and has granted His servants from His shade, and has made His religion prevail over all other religions. The Conqueror over His servants, who cannot be resisted, and the Manifest over His creation, who cannot be contested, and the Commander of what He wills, who cannot be questioned, and the Ruler of what He desires, who cannot be opposed. The Rich, to whom all are in need, the Strong, upon whom all depend, the Self-Sufficient, who needs no partner, companion, or child, the One who raises the heavens without pillars that you can see. Glory be to Him, He is exalted above rivals, opposites, equals, and partners, and He is exalted above examples, manifestations, and comparisons. He is the First without beginning, and the Last without end; there is nothing comparable to Him, nothing resembles Him. All praise is due to Allah, who has made from our hearts insights that lead us to His knowledge, and understandings that guide us to acknowledge His Lordship, to bring us out of darkness into light by His mercy. I praise Him for His victory, manifestation, and support for His allies, and His assistance for His supporters, and for purifying our hearts from the filth of polytheism and its impurities, and for filling them with sincere devotion and its lights, praise be to the One who feels gratitude in the depths of his heart and openly declares it in his nights and days.

And I bear witness that there is no deity except Allah, alone without partner, a God who has made easy what was difficult. Glory be to Him, how great is His power, and how marvelous is His creation, and how astonishing is His wisdom! He has guided to the path of good deeds whom He has made happy, and has diverted from doing them whom his deeds have made miserable and distanced him. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, His chosen one from His creation and His intimate friend. He was sent as a bearer of good tidings and a warner before the Hour, and a caller to Allah by His permission and a shining lamp, whose light has illuminated the horizons, and whose radiance has not been obscured by an eclipse or a waning. The Chosen One from His creation, and the most honored of the first and the last of His creatures, dispeller of doubt and refuter of polytheism, and denouncer of falsehood, the one designated for the Praiseworthy Station on the Day of Reckoning, the owner of the banner that will be raised, and the pond that will be granted. O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, among the first and the last, and in the highest assembly until the Day of Judgment, a prayer that will be pleasing to You and a fulfillment of his right. And grant him the means of intercession and the station that You have promised him. O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad as he deserves and as is fitting for his great honor and perfection, and Your pleasure with him and what You love and are pleased for him always and forever, the best prayer and the most complete and perfect, whenever Your name is mentioned and his name is mentioned by those who remember, and whenever those who forget Your mention and his mention are heedless. And send abundant peace.

And after,
