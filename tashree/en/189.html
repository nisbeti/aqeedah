For Allah, Exalted is He, has informed us that abstaining from jihad is throwing oneself into destruction, and that fighting - which a person usually dislikes - is better for us, and that Allah knows while man does not know. This is because the inquiry does not limit itself to certain aspects of issues over others to determine good and evil, benefit and harm, and public interest (maslaha) and corruption (mafsada). Therefore, the actual attainment of benefits and the actual prevention of harms can only be achieved through correct organization, which only the wise legislator can accomplish. Wherever there is legislation, there is public interest.

If the system is of a human source that is incapable of comprehending benefits and harms, good and evil, and beauty and ugliness, it will necessarily fail to achieve public interests correctly. However, if the system is a divine legislation, it will ensure the well-being of people's lives, prevent them from causing corruption on earth, and establish justice and goodness. This detailed study "The Miracle of Islamic Legislation" provides the necessary insights to highlight how Islamic legislation has successfully achieved this.
