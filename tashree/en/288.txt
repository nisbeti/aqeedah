First: Its manifestation in instilling in the hearts the greatness of Allah:
When we contemplate the various doctrinal concepts that have transported humanity from the darkness of ignorance to the light of guidance, such as the concepts of the relationship between the Lord and His servant, we see its manifestation in instilling in the hearts the greatness of Allah, His glorification, sanctification, and exaltation. Ibn al-Qayyim (may Allah have mercy on him) said, "Knowledge of Allah, Exalted is He, is of two types: the first is the knowledge of acknowledgment, which people share; the righteous and the wicked, the obedient and the disobedient. The second is the knowledge that necessitates shyness before Him, love for Him, attachment of the heart to Him, longing for His meeting, fear of Him, turning to Him, finding comfort in Him, fleeing from creation to Him." Among the attachments of the hearts to Allah, Exalted is He, is to find comfort in Him, to feel His presence at every moment, to long for His meeting and intimate conversation, to turn to Him, to engage in praising and thanking Him, to find tranquility in His remembrance, for the tongue to constantly mention Him, and for a person to feel uneasy about anything that does not remind him of Him. Furthermore, it is to love what He loves and to hate what He dislikes. The fruit of this is to follow His guidance, obey His Messenger, implement His Sharia, and shape life by adhering to His commands and prohibitions, ﴾Say, "If you love Allah, then follow me, so Allah will love you"﴿ [Al-Imran 31], to live for His religion, to be patient and steadfast in carrying His message, along with the responsibilities and hardships that come with it, and to draw closer to Him through what He has made obligatory and through the supererogatory acts, until one reaches the station where Allah loves you while you are in need of Him, and He is free of need from you! You are with Him in accordance with how He is with you.
"And since all of existence, from the heavens and the earth and everything else, belongs to Allah, Exalted is He, He is the most deserving of being worshipped, to whom one should humble oneself, submit to Him, and obey His command, so that He is obeyed and not disobeyed. The first pinnacle of worship is to bear witness that there is no deity but Allah, and the basis of His divinity is that He owns the kingdom of the heavens and the earth(429)." "He manages the affairs of His servants; He commands and forbids, sends messengers, reveals books, is pleased and displeased, rewards and punishes, gives and withholds, honors and humiliates, lowers and raises, sees and hears, knows the secret and the public, acts as He wills, is described with all perfection, is free from all defects, not a particle moves except by His permission, not a leaf falls except by His knowledge, and no one intercedes with Him except by His permission; there is no protector or intercessor for His servants besides Him(430)." The concept of servitude encompasses the concepts of love, fear, and hope. Allah, Exalted is He, is the Majestic, the Great, and the Glorious; thus, the necks must bow to His greatness, and the souls must break before His awe, humbling themselves to His might and submitting before His grandeur. The hearts must humble themselves before Him, and the limbs must be steadfast in His command. Allah, Exalted is He, is the Absolute Perfect, the Complete Beautiful, and the Wise in His actions and legislations, and in His management of His dominion. The servant can do nothing but turn to Him with a love that is natural and not forced. Allah, Exalted is He, is the Most Merciful of the merciful, the Kind and Gentle, the Generous Provider, who has bestowed upon His servants His blessings, both apparent and hidden. Whatever blessing exists among creation is solely from Him, with no partner. ﴾And whatever blessing you have is from Allah; then when adversity touches you, to Him you cry for help﴿ [An-Nahl 53]. Thus, the servitude of the servants to their Lord and Master is manifested in the emergence of hope and aspiration in every corner of their souls, yearning for His grace and desiring His favor, ﴾Call upon your Lord in humility and privately; indeed, He does not like transgressors. And do not cause corruption upon the earth after its reformation. And invoke Him in fear and aspiration; indeed, the mercy of Allah is near to the doers of good﴿ [Al-A'raf 56], and by having good thoughts about their Lord. He is the Sufficient, the Provider, the Giver of life, the Causer of death, the Lord, the Remover of harm, in His hand is good, so the servant entrusts all his affairs to his Lord, content with His decree, trusting in His wisdom, hoping that his Lord will take care of him.
