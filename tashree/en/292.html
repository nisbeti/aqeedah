The intellectual principle, if it agrees with the human nature (fitrah), and is based on reason, is a correct principle. If it contradicts human nature, or is not based on reason, it is an invalid principle. The meaning of the agreement of the intellectual principle with human nature is that it affirms what is inherent in human nature of incapacity and need for the Creator, the Planner. In other words, it aligns with the instinct of religiosity. The meaning of it being based on reason is that it should not be based on materialism or on compromise.

The intellectual leadership of the principle of Islam is in harmony with human nature, and despite its depth, it is easy and accessible. It quickly opens up to a person’s mind and heart, and a person soon turns to it to understand it, and to delve into its intricacies with enthusiasm and appreciation, because religiosity is innate in humans, and every person by their nature is religious. No force can remove this innate disposition from them, as it is deeply rooted within them. By their nature, humans feel that they are incomplete and that there is a power greater than themselves, and that this power deserves veneration. Religiosity is the need for the Creator (al-Khaliq), the Planner (al-Mudabbir), arising from the natural incapacity in the formation of humans, and it is a fixed instinct that has a specific return, which is veneration."(435)

Sixth: Revolutionary Doctrinal Concepts:

The Islamic creed is characterized by revolutionary doctrinal concepts that have no parallel in history. It directed the necessary call to the rational forces of humanity to reflect and contemplate on the creation of the heavens and the earth, and their organization in the most precise order, and to observe, search, and think in order to derive the laws of the universe, and within the self, the camels, the mountains, and the earth, and the alternation of night and day, for insight and inference, until the decisive certainty becomes evident, resulting from the clear proof that the truths of belief disseminated in the Qur'an are firmly established in truth and applicable to reality. Thus, those rational forces recognized the existence of Allah, Exalted is He, with complete understanding, and the believer's feeling of Allah's existence and all His divine attributes, and their connection to the most intricate details of life, was coupled with certainty and a positive, contented, and pleasing relationship. In this way, the doctrinal concepts were able to possess the minds, filling them with a firm conviction that they had completely grasped the reins of truth. Soon, this inherent power of truth mingled with those minds, blending with them until it was infused into them, taking the reins of its horses, and the hearts opened to its guidance, while the warmth of those concepts mingled with the hearts after being emptied of the residues of ignorance, thus reviving them and filling them with tranquility and glad tidings. We will shed a little light on the way of Islam in achieving this to understand the impact of those revolutionary doctrinal concepts in shaping the Islamic personality with its components of mentality and psychology in a manner unmatched in history.
