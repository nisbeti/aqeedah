The legal rulings (ahkam shar'iyya) are what is established, and they are what the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) unanimously agreed upon. The evidence for this is that the Sharia has considered testimony in proving a claim, which is a report of individuals, thus the acceptance of the narration of the Sunnah and the acceptance of individual reports is analogous to the acceptance of testimony. This is because it has been established by the text of the Noble Qur'an that a judgment can be made based on the testimony of two male witnesses or one man and two women in financial matters, and based on the testimony of four men in cases of adultery, and based on the testimony of two men in cases of punishments and retribution. The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) ruled based on the testimony of one witness and the oath of the claimant, and accepted the testimony of one woman in breastfeeding, and all of this is individual reports. All the Companions acted upon this, and there is no narration from them to the contrary. The judgment is an obligation to prefer the side of truth over the side of falsehood as long as the doubts that make the report likely to be false have been removed and are not established. This obligation is nothing but acting upon individual reports. Therefore, it is necessary by analogy to act upon the individual reports narrated from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) to prefer the side of truth as long as the narrator is just, trustworthy, and reliable, and has met with the one from whom he narrated, as the suspicion of the likelihood of falsehood has been removed and this suspicion has not been established against him. Thus, accepting individual reports from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) and deriving rulings from them is like accepting testimony and ruling based on it regarding the matter adjudicated. Accordingly, individual reports are a proof based on what the Qur'an has guided us to."(177).
Fourth: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "May Allah brighten the face of a servant who hears my words, preserves them, and conveys them. Perhaps a bearer of knowledge is not knowledgeable, and perhaps a bearer of knowledge conveys it to one who is more knowledgeable than him." The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) says: "May Allah brighten the face of a servant," and he did not say "servants," and "servant" is a term that applies to one or many, meaning that he praises the individual and the individuals in conveying his hadith.
Moreover, the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) calls for preserving his words and conveying them, so it is an obligation upon everyone who hears him, whether one or many, to convey it, and there will be no effect of conveying it to others unless his words are accepted. The Prophet's call to convey his sayings is a call to accept them as long as the one to whom it is conveyed has confirmed that this is the speech of the Messenger, meaning as long as the transmitter is trustworthy, honest, pious, and reliable, knowing what he carries and what he leaves, so that the suspicion of falsehood is removed and the side of truth is preferred. This indicates that individual reports are a proof according to the explicit Sunnah and what the Sunnah indicates."(178).
Fifth: "As for the occurrence of action based on a single report, it is indicated by His saying, ﴾O you who have believed, if there comes to you a disobedient one with a report, investigate, lest you harm a people out of ignorance and become, over what you have done, regretful﴿ [49:6]. Thus, Allah made the requirement for investigation the condition of being disobedient, so in the absence of disobedience, action must be taken, which is the requirement."(179).
Sixth: And if we know that the legislator himself in the Noble Qur'an has permitted action based on individual reports and made it a proof among the proofs of judgment in testimony, then by analogy, it is accepted in establishing the narrations of hadith, "for His saying, ﴾And it is not for the believers to go forth all at once. So why not go forth in small groups from every division among them to obtain understanding in the religion and warn their people when they have returned to them that perhaps they might be cautious﴿ [9:122]. Allah has made it obligatory for the people to be cautious and to refrain from what the group has warned and frightened them about, whether the warners are few or many – the verse states that a group from every division should go forth with the aim of understanding the religion, while the rest of the division goes forth in the way of Allah for jihad, and the term "division" linguistically applies to three, so the group that comes out from this division will be fewer in number."
