The term "ruling" linguistically means prohibition; it is said: "the horse was restrained" (حَكَمَ الحصان) meaning it was prevented from running away(477). The ultimate goal of a ruling is to regulate human behavior to achieve the pleasure of Allah, the Exalted, by adhering to His commands, in accordance with a concept derived legally from actions or from things!
However, before we delve into the rulings on actions, the rulings on things, the rulings on general actions, and the rulings on innate actions, we must first examine who has the right to issue rulings on actions and things in the first place!
