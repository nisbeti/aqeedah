By the nature of the Creator ﴾Does He not know who created, while He is the Subtle, the All-Aware?﴿.

And with what is good for people and what rectifies them, ﴾And Allah knows, while you do not know.﴿.

﴾There is no blame upon those who have believed and done righteous deeds for what they have eaten, if they fear Allah and believe and do righteous deeds, and then fear Allah and believe, and then fear Allah and do good; and Allah loves the doers of good.﴿.

And with what the creation can bear and what they cannot bear, ﴾Allah does not burden a soul except with that within its capacity; it will have [the consequence of] what [good] it has gained, and it will bear [the consequence of] what [evil] it has earned.﴿.

And what alleviates hardship and difficulty from them ﴾He has chosen you and has not made upon you in the religion any difficulty.﴿ (Allah intends for you ease and does not intend for you hardship).

And we will find that Allah, Exalted is He, has commanded justice and prohibited oppression ﴾Say, "My Lord has commanded justice."﴿.

He has prohibited oppression upon Himself, ﴾And your Lord is not ever unjust to the servants.﴿.

And He established the dominion of the heavens and the earth upon the foundation of justice and equity and forbade oppression, ﴾These are the verses of Allah; We recite them to you in truth. And Allah does not intend injustice for the worlds.﴿.

And He sent down the Book and the system that ensures the realization of justice and the establishment of people in equity, ﴾Indeed, We sent Our messengers with clear proofs and sent down with them the Scripture and the balance that the people may maintain [justice].﴿,

For His legislation alone achieves justice. ﴾Indeed, Allah commands you to render trusts to whom they are due and when you judge between people to judge with justice. Excellent is that which Allah instructs you. Indeed, Allah is ever Hearing and Seeing.﴿,

And He prohibits oppression ﴾Indeed, Allah commands you to be just and to do good and to give to relatives; and He forbids immorality, wrongdoing, and transgression. He instructs you that perhaps you will be reminded.﴿.

And we will find that Allah, Exalted is He, did not leave people to legislate, ﴾And in that in which you disagree, its ruling is to Allah.﴿.

So their rulings, which are based on man-made laws, follow desires, ﴾So judge between them by what Allah has revealed and do not follow their desires, away from what has come to you of the truth.﴿.

And corruption on earth, ﴾And if the truth had followed their desires, the heavens and the earth and whoever is in them would have been corrupted. But We brought them their message, and they, from their message, are turning away.﴿, ﴾Or should we treat those who have believed and done righteous deeds like the corrupters in the earth? Or should we treat the righteous like the wicked?﴿.

And the entrenchment of oppression, for which the laws were revealed to eradicate it,

And the enslavement of one another, which contradicts that people were created free, but they were created as servants of Allah! ﴾They took their scholars and monks as lords besides Allah.﴿.

And an infringement on the right of Allah, Exalted is He, in legislation and judgment ﴾And to Him belongs the judgment, and to Him you will be returned.﴿.
