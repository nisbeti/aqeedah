It is worth mentioning that the method of Imam al-Haramayn al-Juwayni in al-Burhan, and al-Ghazali in al-Mustasfa, and the abridgment of Ibn al-Hajib is based on examining the conditions of the evidences and what they depend upon. They established the principles of their methodology along this line, supported by insights accompanied by the statements of opponents and their objections, which is the method of the people of theology.

As for the method of jurists in the principles of jurisprudence (usul al-fiqh), it is characterized by not addressing the proofs in most issues, but rather by increasing examples and instances derived from those principles. Some of them did not elaborate on this, but rather limited themselves to mentioning the issues of the principles and the disagreements within them, stripped of evidences and examples; like the author of "Jami al-Jawami," Imam Taj al-Din Abdul Wahab, known as Ibn al-Subki, who passed away in the year 771(163).

We will observe in this study that Islam is keen on organizing the methodologies of thinking, deduction, reasoning, and knowledge in such a way that the process of linking reality with the legislation that descends upon that reality is regulated, so that it is judged by commands and prohibitions that direct behavior (by obligation), such that following the methodology (or approach) becomes a protective fence against falling into error, and a sign of the "legitimacy" of the research, and the acceptance of its results, and a practical application of its sciences derived from the evidences that indicate them, which connect the investor (i.e., the mujtahid) between the fruits (i.e., rulings) and the fruitful (i.e., evidences) in a manner of investment that reveals the aspects of the evidences' indication on the rulings.

Thus, the science of usul al-fiqh is a science that encompasses a collection of methodologies (i.e., the different principles among the usuliyun(164)) that are subjected in their operation to a single methodology that regulates them (i.e., the rules that establish the principles of jurisprudence, such as the definitiveness of the evidences, and that the ruler is Allah, Exalted is He... etc., from the general principles, some of which we have outlined in our book: Theory of Knowledge and Methodologies of Thinking and Reasoning, under: The Science of Usul al-Fiqh), or it is a methodology that diverges in methodologies, as it flows within them to ensure that they remain limited by boundaries, and controlling edges for the course of research that they are not permitted to transgress, and established by rules, and grounded in principles and criteria of great precision that accurately define the methods and principles of reasoning, deduction, proof, and generalization, and the aspects of obligation and certainty (certainty or otherwise) or conjecture in the results of reasoning, and what are its most important sources, tools, and methods. It is the essential tool of ijtihad, and it is the foundational programming (principles) upon which the research process is built that will establish the connection between human problems and revelation, i.e., the legislations that command and prohibit. Any tampering with this foundational programming that takes it outside the methodological limits that must flow in its veins to ensure the integrity of its operation will lead to major calamities. This programming does not require development, nor change, nor renewal that takes it outside these limits and foundations, as tens of thousands of minds have studied it, utilized it, and brought it to perfection, with the required breadth.
