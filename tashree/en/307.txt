(And these verses were revealed about Jews who lived outside the authority of the Islamic state, who came to the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) to judge between them. He admonished them that they find the rulings in the Torah but do not implement them, while claiming to have faith.) Then Allah, Exalted is He, said: ﴾And judge between them by what Allah has revealed, and do not follow their desires, and beware lest they entice you away from some of what Allah has revealed to you. And if they turn away, then know that Allah intends to afflict them with some of their sins, and indeed, many of the people are transgressors. Is it the judgment of ignorance that they seek, and who is better in judgment than Allah for a people who are certain?﴿ [Al-Ma'idah 49] This definitively ends the speech of every orator, for the address is to the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him), to judge by what has been revealed to him! Moreover, the verses came in general terms, as He said: ﴾And whoever does not judge by what Allah has revealed﴿, so the term ﴾and whoever﴿ is one of the general terms, encompassing us, them, and everyone who does not judge by what Allah has revealed. It is a general term according to the structure of the Arabic language, requiring no contextual evidence for us to understand that it is inclusive! The principle in usul al-fiqh (principles of jurisprudence) states that "the consideration is for the generality of the wording, not for the specificity of the cause"!
