The soul for a soul, and the eye for an eye, and the nose for a nose, and the ear for an ear, and a tooth for a tooth, and for wounds is retribution (qisas). And whoever gives up (his right) as charity, it is an expiation for him. And whoever does not judge by what Allah has revealed, then those are the wrongdoers. And We followed in their footsteps with Jesus, son of Mary, confirming what was before him of the Torah, and We gave him the Gospel, in which is guidance and light, and confirming what was before him of the Torah, and guidance and an admonition for the righteous. And let the people of the Gospel judge by what Allah has revealed therein. And whoever does not judge by what Allah has revealed, then those are the disobedient. And We have sent down to you the Book in truth, confirming what was before it of the Book and a guardian over it. So judge between them by what Allah has revealed, and do not follow their desires away from what has come to you of the truth. For each of you We have made a law (shari'a) and a method (minhaj). And if Allah had willed, He would have made you one nation, but He intends to test you in what He has given you. So race to good deeds. To Allah is your return all together, and He will inform you of that in which you used to differ. And judge between them by what Allah has revealed, and do not follow their desires, and beware lest they entice you away from some of what Allah has revealed to you. And if they turn away, then know that Allah intends to strike them for some of their sins. And indeed, many among the people are disobedient. Do they seek the judgment of ignorance?
