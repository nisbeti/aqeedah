The state of truth was destroyed, and the state of falsehood invaded the stronghold. The earth was shrouded for years in famine, where the oppressors grazed freely, the corrupt spread corruption, and the rumor-mongers spread their rumors. Oppression rose to a difficult peak, truth was obscured, goodness was diminished, evil spread, blood flowed like rivers, and honor was violated openly in broad daylight. The heavens withheld their rain, falsehood proliferated and swelled, the lowly took over our land, hypocrisy emerged, and morals decayed.
The nations have conspired against us like hungry wolves over their prey, from every direction and corner. They have unleashed their swords, and their spiteful projectiles, and the molten flames of their fires upon the body of the Ummah. They have appointed the worst of them as rulers over the Ummah, and the wicked as ministers, and the greatest of its criminals as tax collectors, judges, and informants. They have stifled breaths, plundered resources, and the best Ummah brought forth for mankind has become like the scum of a flood, surrounded as if it were a plant that neither cut the ground nor left any back intact!
And they left nothing for the choice people except the scraps from the tables, and the hardship of living, and they waged war against the nation in the way it lives, and its beliefs, and they bombarded it with the projectiles of their falsehood to silence the breaths of truth within them.
The blessings of the nation have become plunder for its enemies, so do not ask about the looted oil, nor the stolen wealth, nor the usurped rights. How can it be otherwise when the blood of children has become fuel for the revenge of their enemies against them? Mosques have been demolished, homes have been swallowed up, the beloved children have been killed and burned, and families have been displaced to the corners of the earth, begging for a morsel and shelter, while they are the best nation brought forth for mankind!
The armies of the nation have become, by the order of their rulers, a drawn sword and arrows aimed at their necks, a peace for their enemies and a war against their allies. The sacred places have been violated, the chaste women have been violated, and vice has spread without anyone daring to denounce it, while virtue has withered away. All of this has become as clear to the Muslims as the sun, and they perceive it just as others from different nations do.
