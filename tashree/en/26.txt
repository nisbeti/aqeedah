The missionary Lawrence Brown says: "If Muslims unite in an Arab empire, they could become a curse on the world and a danger, or they could also become a blessing for it. However, if they remain divided, they will then remain without weight or influence... Arabs and Muslims must remain divided to stay without power or influence"(51).
Arnold Toynbee says in his book Islam, the West, and the Future: "It is true that Islamic unity is asleep, but we must take into account that the sleeper may awaken if he hears a call for leadership that is hostile to the West, and this call may have countless psychological effects in awakening the fighting spirit of Islam, even if it has slept the sleep of the people of the cave. This call can awaken echoes of Islam's heroic history, and there are two historical occasions when Islam was a symbol of the elevation of Eastern society in its victory over the Western intruder. During the era of the Rightly Guided Caliphs, Islam liberated Syria and Egypt from Greek domination, which had burdened them for almost a thousand years, and during the time of Nur al-Din, Saladin, and the Mamluks, Islam maintained its stronghold against the attacks of the Crusaders and the Mongols.
