When we measure the lifespan of an individual human being and consider the days they spend in this existence in relation to the age of the universe and eternity, we find that their time is but a few moments, like a blink of an eye, even though they yearn for immortality. There must, therefore, be a connection between them and the mystery of existence, binding them with ties of kinship and bonds that cannot be severed. Just as their existence has a purpose and meaning that must be grasped, it is undoubtedly an extension of the purpose of existence itself. How could it not be so, when we have witnessed with our own eyes that the general characteristics of planet Earth have been calibrated to receive life, within a precise system of ecological balance? The laws of nature and the values of constants and boundary conditions of the universe have been set to accommodate life. Thus, the universe has indeed been prepared to welcome this human being, and the human being themselves has been equipped, with their faculties and abilities, to live this life on this planet. Therefore, it is essential to recognize the bonds of kinship between the purpose of existence and the purpose of humanity. It is inconceivable to think that this grand plan of existence is merely for a moment, like a blink of an eye, after which the rational, thinking human disappears from the stage of existence. Rather, the correct conception necessitates that there must be another life in which that human is resurrected, extending their thoughts, actions, and beliefs that they engaged in during their worldly life.
This comprehensive conception "is the function of the correct religious belief, and its impact on the soul and life... No other belief can connect the mortal being with the power of eternity, and grant the weak individual that support and assistance"(561). It provides the human being with an inexhaustible source in facing the hardships of life and the trials of existence, stemming from true faith that arises from a sound doctrinal thought based on evidence and proofs, which fill the heart with faith, contentment, and submission. This is based on a genuine connection with the Creator of the universe, humanity, and life, and the One who manages its affairs, in light of a precise understanding of organizing life according to responsibilities that fall upon the human being within a system that God has made available to them to fulfill those responsibilities in the best manner. This connection enables the human to always view the vicissitudes of time, the joys of life, and its tribulations with positivity, hope, and expectation. Whoever possesses this faith confronts all causes of depression, anxiety, and frustration, dispelling them from their life and living with a sound heart!
