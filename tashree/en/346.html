The first: reliance on experimentation in legislation: laws are enacted, then their effects are monitored. The second: resorting to narrow material standards: where success and failure in legislation are measured based on economic indicators or relative community satisfaction, without considering the value and spiritual dimensions, resulting in "materially advanced" societies that are nonetheless engulfed in depression, suicide, family disintegration, and rampant addiction.
