First: Legislation is the right of Allah alone, and its relation to the obligation of the caliphate

The Concept of Sovereignty

Since the foundation of our research is a comparison between legislation and the ability or inability of humans to legislate, where the fundamental idea in democracy is placing legislation in the hands of the people or their representatives, and then comparing the laws regulating human life in the state between the Islamic state and the legal state, it is necessary to examine legislation, the enactment of laws, their objectives, and their purposes, as well as how to address human problems in life, and to compare secularism with Islam in this regard. Therefore, it is essential to immediately transition to these discussions, with the blessing of Allah!

Some argue that the governance in the state must be based on human-made laws, and on secular, democratic, liberal systems. We argue against them that there is no governance except that of Allah, and that humans are not fit to legislate; otherwise, we would be opening the doors wide to human whims, contradictions, and the interests of their factions, which would lead to injustice, tyranny, and legislative chaos, resulting in the absence of establishing truth and justice, and the people's recourse to the correct system. Therefore, this research is included in this part of the book! We will study the issue from two perspectives: a legal perspective, which is the first section of this discussion, and a rational perspective by examining the ability of humans to legislate and its catastrophic consequences for humanity, which is the second section of this discussion. May Allah grant success, so let us proceed to the first section:

Section One: The Legal Perspective in the Research:

We will study this important issue, sovereignty and the sovereign, and its relation to the state through four key points:

The first: The linguistic and legal meaning of governance

The second: Who is the sovereign, the one with the right to legislate

The third: The application of these legislations through a state to achieve the objectives of legislation

The fourth: The practical realization of this through the establishment of the Noble Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) of the state, clarifying for us the essence of the Qur'an and detailing the method of its application and implementation in reality.
