He says, "And if a servant is appointed over you who leads you by the Book of Allah, then listen to him and obey him"(473). He conditioned obedience on leading by the Book of Allah, Exalted is He.

The Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) established the Islamic state in Medina along with its institutions and system; he appointed governors, judges, and assistants, established the system of consultation (shura), and exercised governance therein. The Companions pledged allegiance to him as the head of state, and when he passed to the higher companionship, Exalted is He, the system he established continued as it was. He referred to it as the caliphate in several hadiths that we have previously mentioned, which clearly indicates that the form of the Islamic state and its system is a divine legislation, and that the rulings were revealed along with the method of their application, and were not left to the whims of people and what they have become accustomed to!

Thus, we find from the collection of verses and hadiths related to the four pillars a rich and strong material that nearly encompasses most of the Qur'an and a large portion of the practical Sunnah, all of which pertain to the state, its system, its institutions, its practices, its responsibilities, and the details of its rulings. All of this evidence collectively indicates the moral consensus on the obligation of the caliphate; it is an unequivocal obligation that only the ignorant are unaware of!
