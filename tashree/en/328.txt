The major questions, the origin from which the system of life emerges
Allah, Exalted is He, says: ﴾And Allah brought you forth from the wombs of your mothers while you knew nothing, and He made for you hearing, sight, and hearts that you might give thanks﴿.
A person lives in this world learning, using his senses to convey an image of the external world and its unknowns that need to be explored to his brain, through the means of perception such as hearing and sight, among others, to reflect and interact.
This is because a human being, any human being, is confronted with serious and important questions that urge him to seek answers, forming a complex due to the nature of their deep inquiries. Among the greatest of these complexities are the human's overarching questions about existence, that is, about the universe, humanity, and life, their source and destiny, their connection to what precedes and follows them, and what is the purpose of their existence? What is the function for which they were created? (Was the universe created to be hospitable to life? What is the role of man in this universe? What is the purpose of his creation and existence? What is his connection to existence and what is existence's connection to him?)
What is life? How did it arise in the inanimate? The first life, with all the complexity within cells? How did these cells— which were previously inanimate— pulse with feelings, emotions, affection, sadness, and joy? Then with intellect and thought! Why was man created? What is his function? What is the position of man and life in the "grand cosmic plan"?
And why is man endowed with consciousness, perception, intellect, and intelligence? What is man? What does it mean to be human? How do you realize your humanity in a way that aligns with what realizes the humanity of others? What elevates man as a noble, dignified, and thriving being, distinguishing him from other creatures?
What is the stance on freedom and servitude, security and well-being, justice and oppression, rights and duties and responsibilities?
What is the stance on the unchanging issue of humanity that one must not neglect for a moment: the issue of his existence in this universe and his destiny! What is the correct philosophy of death, life, and time?
Is man free to choose or is he guided? Is there a system that he should follow to direct his behavior? Or does he have complete freedom and choice to follow his intellect, desires, and wishes? Does he have responsibilities and duties towards others?
What is the criterion for good and evil? Right and wrong? Beauty and ugliness? Praise and blame? Reward and punishment? On what basis will it be determined whether we live life correctly or incorrectly?
Do you not see the importance of answering such questions to explain to you the reason for your existence in life, who brought you here? And what comes after? What is required of you? Why did you come to this life? Is what you do in this life related to what comes after?
These questions form what is called the great dilemma, and the answers to them provide a solution to this great dilemma, which we call belief, leading to faith, and resulting in the liberation of man from agnosticism and degradation, so that he may ascend in the ranks of elevation and rise to the lofty status for which he was honored!
