Amr: It is said that the matter of so-and-so is straight, and his affairs are straight, and he commanded him to do such-and-such, and the plural is commands (awamir). And he also commanded him, meaning he increased him, and its root is nasara. From it is the hadith "The best wealth is a mare (ma'murah) or a well-trodden path"(451), meaning a mare that produces many offspring. And "he commanded him" also means to increase him, and "he was commanded" means he became abundant, and its root is tariba, becoming similar to 'ilm (knowledge) and 'alamtu (I informed him). And "the amir" is the one with authority, and he may command with the joining (imrah) with the kasra, he became an amir, and the female is amira with the "h." And "he was commanded" also means he commands with the joining of the meem in both cases (imarah) with the kasra as well, and "he made him an amir" (ta'amir) means he dominated over them. And "he commanded him in such-and-such" (mu'amara) means he consulted him, and the general public says: "And he commanded him," and "they complied with the command" means they obeyed it, and they complied with it when they intended it and consulted about it, and "consultation" (al-ittimar) and "request for consultation" (al-isti'mar) means consultation, and likewise "mutual consultation" (al-ta'amur) is like mutual action. I said: The saying of Allah, Exalted is He: ﴾And consult among yourselves in what is good﴿ [al-Talaq: 6] means let some of you command one another with what is good, and "the command" (al-amara) and "the command" (al-amr) also mean with their opening, the time and the sign.(452)
﴾Then We placed you on a clear command, so follow it and do not follow the desires of those who do not know﴿ [al-Jathiya: 18], ﴾Obey Allah and obey the Messenger and those in authority among you﴿ [al-Nisa: 59]. The totality of commands and prohibitions that were revealed to the nation to govern their lives with them is referred to as "the command," based on the obligation to obey them and the necessity of implementing them in life; they are a law and a way of life. ﴾And We have sent down to you the Book in truth, confirming what was before it of the Book and safeguarding it. So judge between them by what Allah has revealed and do not follow their desires away from what has come to you of the truth. For every one of you We have made a law and a method﴿ [al-Maida: 48]. From here, the subjects are commanded to obey those in authority, and the command may also mean events, so those in authority derive the rulings related to events and manage affairs based on these commands and prohibitions. The connection of the term "those in authority" with politics is very strong, and more firmly than a rope, as this politics is to implement Allah's rulings on occurrences and events, so that people can govern their lives according to the system of Islam. Thus, the connection between religion, that is, the Sharia, and politics, and between governance, sovereignty, and command is an organic connection that cannot be severed except by those who are ignorant.
And when Allah, Exalted is He, says to His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him): ﴾To judge between the people by what Allah shows you﴿ [Surah al-Nisa: 105], then "what" is a term of generality, encompassing judgment among people in any matter, from adjudicating disputes to managing affairs, and it is not limited to the incident for which the verse was revealed to clarify the ruling therein. The foundational principle is: "The consideration is for the generality of the term, not for the specificity of the cause," and the term is general, including judgment in any matter. The rulings related to the internal politics of the state fall under this verse, and examples include the rulings concerning the People of the Book and the state's responsibilities towards them, based on the principle that they have what we have in fairness, and they have what we have in redress. Among their rulings is a substantial number of verses and hadiths, including the rulings that were revealed and practically applied to establish solidarity in society and achieve equality in treatment, including the establishment of markets, mosques, public facilities, and other rulings of internal politics, all of which have evidence, were indeed applied, and were among the responsibilities of the state and society, and so on.
﴾And whoever does not judge by what Allah has revealed, then those are the disbelievers﴿ [al-Maida: 44], ﴾the wrongdoers﴿ [al-Maida: 45], ﴾the transgressors﴿ [al-Maida: 47]. Are they specific to the People of the Book? These verses that were revealed in Surah al-Maida, although some were revealed concerning the People of the Book, include verses that were explicitly revealed for our Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) and for his nation, for when Allah commanded the People of the Book to judge...
