The third: it is a spiritual belief,
and in addition to being a political belief, it adds another dimension that establishes what is within it of spiritual inclinations and yearnings, and connects the individual to the afterlife, encouraging him with the delights of Paradise, warning him of the torment of Hellfire, and making his happiness realized through attaining the pleasure of Allah, Exalted is He. It links the politics of worldly affairs to the afterlife through accountability, reward, and punishment,
so that the belief does not remain confined to doctrinal issues such as sustenance and lifespan. The management of life according to what the belief has brought forth or what has emerged from it or has been built upon it is what transforms the belief from a theoretical proposition, as mentioned, into a practical project. This is what we must focus on in our discussions and emphasize in the culture of Muslims, and this is what has come in our culture, namely the connection of worldly life with what precedes and follows it, and not merely linking the relationship of the universe, humanity, and life with what precedes and follows it alone, for this alone makes it a purely spiritual belief.
