The understanding that one owes to Allah, Exalted is He, will be accounted for based on the following of what their ijtihad (independent juristic reasoning) has reached(127). We have previously pointed out the concern of the Wise Legislator with adhering to operational procedures in studying and understanding texts, in order to prevent the influence of whims and the intrusion of desires into the interpretation of texts. If the mujtahid (jurist) frees themselves from all of that and employs their intellect in understanding the texts according to the meanings and implications that the texts may hold, in accordance with the established rules of usul (principles) and language, then they issue a legal ruling. This ability contained within the texts, which encompass many meanings in few words, has led to the vastness of jurisprudence and made the texts capable of accommodating human problems until the Day of Judgment.

It is narrated from the Mother of the Believers, Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her), that she said: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said, “Whoever introduces something into this matter of ours that is not part of it, it will be rejected.” Narrated by Muslim. Al-Nawawi (may Allah have mercy on him) said in his explanation: In the second narration: “Whoever performs an action that is not in accordance with our command, it will be rejected.” The scholars of the Arabic language said: (rejected) here means that which is not accepted, and its meaning is: it is invalid and not taken into account. This hadith is a great principle among the principles of Islam, and it is one of the comprehensive sayings of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) as it explicitly rejects all innovations and inventions. In the second narration, there is an addition indicating that some doers may insist on an innovation they have previously introduced. If they are confronted with the first narration, they might say: I did not introduce anything, and they can be countered with the second narration that explicitly rejects all innovations, whether the doer introduced them or preceded others in introducing them. This hadith is something that should be memorized and used to refute wrongdoings and promote its evidence.

Thus, a ruling based on reason, which is derived from the evidences and legal signs, severed from revelation and disconnected from the principles, is a rejection because it is an introduction into our matter that is not part of it, meaning it is not derived from it. Acting based on this fatwa (legal opinion) is an action that is not in accordance with our command, and therefore it is a rejection, meaning it is invalid.

It is an established fact that Allah does not punish until He sends a Messenger, who warns the people about what they should be cautious of, as He has made clear to them. ﴾And Allah would not lead a people astray after He had guided them until He makes clear to them what they should avoid. Indeed, Allah is Knowing of all things﴿ ﴾At-Tawbah 115﴿, ﴾Whoever is guided is only guided for himself, and whoever goes astray only goes astray against it. And no bearer of burdens will bear the burden of another. And We were not to punish until we had sent a messenger﴿ ﴾Al-Isra 15﴿, ﴾And it has been revealed to me that this Qur'an is to warn you thereby and whomever it reaches﴿ ﴾Al-An'am 19﴿, ﴾Say, "I only warn you by revelation." And the deaf do not hear the call when they are warned﴿ ﴾Al-Anbiya 45﴿, ﴾Messengers as bringers of good tidings and warners, so that people will have no argument against Allah after the messengers. And ever is Allah Exalted in Might and Wise﴿ ﴾An-Nisa 165﴿. These verses clarify that revelation was sent as a warning, and its rulings are a proof against those who are warned. Whoever receives the clear warning has the proof established against them and deserves punishment for disobedience. Whoever abolishes the rulings of Islam and replaces them with others, or who deems them applicable to a time other than their own, contradicts a great principle upon which the message stands. Whoever considers Islam to be deficient has contradicted the establishment of proof against creation. Whoever introduces something into legislation based on reason contradicts these verses, which limit the warning to revelation and clarify the comprehensiveness of revelation in stating what the obligated should avoid. Based on this, the proof is established, and accountability, reward, and punishment are arranged.
