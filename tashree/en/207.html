The establishment of the Islamic state, that is, the Caliphate, is the means through which the objectives for which Allah sent the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) and for which He revealed the Book are achieved!

Otherwise, Islam is not a fanciful philosophy, but rather it was revealed to be implemented and to govern. It has been established that these rulings are to be enacted in the lives of Muslims through a state that governs them,(307) which adheres to a system of governance prescribed by Sharia, which the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) called the State of Caliphate,(308) and he established it as a reality, along with its institutions and laws. Thus, a state was effectively established, and the only course of action for the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) regarding it was to continue what the Master of Creation (peace and blessings be upon him) initiated concerning it. It is a state whose foundations are based on the actions of the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) and on the rulings that represent the majority of the Qur'an and Sunnah, which were revealed to be implemented through it, and the Companions unanimously agreed on its obligation, and its obligation has been transmitted with a significant degree of certainty. Such a state cannot be ignored in terms of existence, governance, and institutions, nor can it be claimed to be a non-essential human system except by one who is ignorant!
