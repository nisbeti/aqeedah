The principle "wherever there is public interest (maslaha), there is the law of Allah": we mentioned it earlier, and we repeat the details: if the intended public interest is a legitimate one, there is no doubt that the Sharia endorses it and commands it; for example, learning beneficial sciences, organizing the affairs of life, and fulfilling the needs of people are all things that the Sharia commands, for it is indeed the law of Allah. However, if the intended "interest" is merely a fleeting worldly benefit that contradicts the command of Allah, how can they claim it to be His law?! If someone says: the interest of the state today is to form alliances with disbelieving countries, even if it leads to fighting other Muslims – for instance – because politics and interests require it! This is false because the Sharia forbids allegiance to disbelievers and fighting believers, no matter how the matter is beautified. We have seen in our time those who have issued fatwas permitting alliances with foreign occupying forces against a Muslim state under the pretext of "national interest"! There is no doubt that this is the statement of one who does not understand the legal loyalty and enmity.
In summary: public interest in the Sharia means what is a real benefit for the servants that is endorsed by revelation in its various proofs or objectives, considering the relevant influencing factors. However, unleashing what people's minds see as interest without restriction by the Book and the Sunnah is a doctrinal and methodological deviation that removes religion from being a sacred revelation to relative human perceptions. This is exactly what the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) warned against when he said: "Beware of newly invented matters, for every innovation is a misguidance, and every misguidance is in the Fire" (a sound hadith narrated by Abu Dawood and others). So, what is innovation? Is not legislating according to opinions and desires an invention in religion? And an accusation against the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) that he did not convey the religion completely from his Lord? Indeed, by Allah. Just as there are misguided innovations in beliefs and acts of worship, there are also innovations in the field of legislation and judiciary, which have appeared in our time under the name of "the jurisprudence of reality" or "modern jurisprudence" and similar terms, and they are in reality a submission to human desires under a shiny cover of slogans.

Some have gone even further, justifying homosexuality under the pretext of individual freedom and that a person's "interest" is determined by their inclinations! This purely utilitarian rational logic is indeed used to legalize immorality in the West, and it is demanded by hypocrites and secularists in Muslim countries.
