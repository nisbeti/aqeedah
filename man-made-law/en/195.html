The Sharia of Islam is God's eternal miracle, complete, comprehensive, and suitable for all times and places as it is, without change or alteration, neither in its foundations nor in its branches, and there is no "fixed and variable" in it, but all of it is fixed and connected to revelation. This global Islamic awakening in recent decades is nothing but evidence that humanity's longing for the justice of the Sharia and the guidance of revelation has not died, despite two centuries of secular dominance. We have no choice but to present our religion to people in its pure form as it was revealed by God: with its balanced methodology that achieves justice and the true, lenient Sharia without change, alteration, or distortion.

Let us beware – we Muslims – of being bitten from the same hole of manipulating religion twice. And what is Western civilization today – with its deification of reason and desire – but the dark hole of the lizard that we are meant to enter behind them.

Finally, it must be noted that the falsehood claiming that "the implementation of the Sharia is a hindrance to reason and a restriction on thought" has been proven false during our research. The opposite is true: the implementation of the Sharia provides a solid framework for the mind to deduce and innovate safely, while following whims leads to the loss of direction and makes thought a prisoner of trends and desires. The mind in Islam is respected, but it is the mind of the obedient believer that operates within the framework of {God said and the Messenger said}. As for the unrestrained mind, it has no weight because it is prone to confusion and contradiction – as we have seen among the "rationalists" themselves.
