To achieve true higher values, such as justice, the preservation of dignity, and equal opportunities, rather than serving the interests of the powerful or legalizing deviations.

The chapter also clarifies that the corruption of legislation or the absence of legislation leads not only to legal dysfunction but also to the comprehensive destruction of society: family disintegration, moral chaos, economic injustice, political paralysis, and psychological and spiritual ruin.

Therefore, it is essential to clarify the characteristics of correct legislation and to expose the shortcomings of man-made laws based solely on human reason without revelation.

Methodological Introduction:

Legislation in any society or state is not an end in itself; rather, it is a tool whose function is to organize human behavior (i.e., human actions), the relationships among members of society, and state systems, as well as to organize the pursuit of benefits ((12)) and to organize the prevention of harms ((13)) whenever evidence indicates the places of goodness and righteousness, to establish a balance of good and evil,

(12) Note our statement: organizing the pursuit of benefits, because the angle from which we will view it is not the pursuit of benefit by any means or legislation, meaning that the reason for legislation is not merely the pursuit of benefit, but the organization of relationships in a system that will achieve the fulfillment of human needs regardless of delving into the details: what is the benefit or interest sought from legislation, and what is the harm, because this is a relative conjectural inquiry that may be valid at times but often is not, making it a precarious angle if taken as a criterion for the validity of legislation. The capable person who can lend money with interest might then ask: did the prohibition of interest achieve a benefit for me? If he is deprived of material gain, he will view the legislation as having not brought him a benefit, because it prevented him from a means to increase his wealth. Therefore, Allah, Exalted is He, said: ﴾And whatever you give in usury to increase in the people's wealth will not increase with Allah, but whatever you give in zakah, desiring the countenance of Allah, those are the ones who will have their reward multiplied﴿ [al-Rūm: 39]. Thus, increasing your wealth through usury is not the true benefit, nor is it true growth; rather, increasing it through zakah is what truly increases it, but you do not know. The borrower asks the same question, thinking that his interest may not be fulfilled due to the lack of funding except through usury, or he may rejoice in the prohibition because it has protected him from entering the cycle of usury. Thus, the solution and legislation enter into a dilemma due to the varying perspectives and estimations of people regarding benefits! That is, it is not a matter of individuals looking at benefits and harms through their opinions and desires, thinking that the benefit is what they like or what provides them with immediate material gain, and the harm is what they dislike or what harms them, as these are partial measures that are insufficient to issue a correct judgment on whether a behavior, system, legislation, or relationship will truly bring a benefit or not, or whether it will truly avert a harm or not. These measures are not suitable to be the basis upon which we organize society! Allah, Exalted is He, has informed us that abstaining from jihad is throwing oneself into destruction, and that fighting - which a person usually dislikes - is better for us, and that Allah knows while man does not know. This is because the inquiry does not limit itself to aspects of issues without others to determine good and evil, benefit and harm, interest and harm. Therefore, the true pursuit of benefits and the true prevention of harms can only be achieved through correct organization, which only the wise legislator can accomplish. Wherever there is divine law, there is benefit. If the system is from a human source that is incapable of perceiving benefits and harms, good and evil, and beauty and ugliness, it will necessarily fail to achieve public interests correctly. However, if the system is divine legislation, it will achieve for people the righteousness of their livelihood, prevent them from corruption on earth, and establish justice and goodness.

(13) The pursuit of benefits and the prevention of harms are not the reasons for legal rulings in general, as there is no text indicating that the pursuit of benefits and the prevention of harms are reasons for legal rulings, nor is there any text indicating that they are reasons for a specific ruling, so they cannot be a legal reason. Especially since the true benefits and harms are unknown to humans, who may think something is beneficial when it is harmful, and vice versa. Consequently, the judgment on estimating benefits rationally varies, making the benefit itself subordinate to the legal ruling, and the legal ruling should not be made subordinate to it. Our statement does not mean that the law was brought to harm creation, but the true benefit and righteousness, which is the essence of the law, is not determined by what minds estimate as a benefit, but by the system that will address the need that some have estimated as a benefit and others as harm, to let it speak its word in organization, meaning leaving the estimation of benefit to Allah and establishing the correct legal remedy, and wherever He has legislated, there is righteousness and benefit. Meanwhile, the law alerts us to the places of goodness and righteousness for us, especially what we may overlook, such as His saying: ﴾Go forth, light and heavy, and strive with your wealth and your lives in the cause of Allah; that is best for you if you only knew﴿ [al-Tawbah: 41]. However, at the same time, it legislates rulings where the benefit is not inherent in the ruling itself. For example, where do you find the benefit in raising the finger during the testimony in prayer, or where is the benefit in His command to the Children of Israel to kill themselves? ﴾And when Moses said to his people, "O my people, you have wronged yourselves by taking the calf; so repent to your Creator and kill yourselves; that is best for you with your Creator﴿ [al-Baqarah: 54]. Thus, the true benefit lies in obeying Allah's commands, and it is not necessarily in the act that is required to be performed, nor in what "the mind estimates and the desires imagine as a benefit in accordance with their inclinations and whims and interests."
