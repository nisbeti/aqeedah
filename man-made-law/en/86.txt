He killed him, and thus all of this is a suitable understood description, but the black color does not prevent the judge from ruling, for blackness has no relation to the prohibition of judging. The color black is not considered a suitable understood description for the prohibition of judging, and similarly, the tall person does not inherit, for height has no relation to inheritance; it is neither understood nor suitable for the prohibition of inheritance, because the heir can be tall or short, so this description (height) is neither understood nor suitable for the ruling. Thus, the suitable understood description is one that has a significant relationship to the ruling that follows it, and in this case, it is valid for reasoning, such as anger in judgment and killing in inheritance. As for the unsuitable and non-understood description, it is not valid for reasoning, like color in judgment and height in inheritance, and so on.(36) This suitable and influential description here is derived from the legal evidences, such as his saying (peace and blessings be upon him): "The killer does not inherit"(37), for he excluded the killer from the heirs because he is a killer - an understood description. "In the grazing sheep, there is zakat"(38) establishes zakat on those that are not fed by their owner but graze in the pastures - an understood description.
The suitable and appropriate: what does not have a specific text on its causation, but the generalities of the Sharia testify to its suitability, such as the reasoning for the prohibition of wine due to intoxication; (as mentioned by al-Shawkani in Irshad al-Fuhul from al-Razi), for intoxication is a major corruption that the texts testify to prohibiting its kind, thus it was a suitable description. (Noting that the prohibition of wine is not justified by intoxication, otherwise its little would not be prohibited if it does not lead to intoxication, as the cause revolves with the effect in existence and non-existence).
The suitable that is unqualified: what does not have a specific text supporting it nor a definitive general principle, but its cancellation has not appeared either. This is the very essence of the unqualified interest that we are discussing.
The suitable that is canceled: what has been shown by a specific text to be canceled and not considered despite achieving an interest in our view. For example, some justified preventing a woman from holding the highest position of leadership due to her lack of intellect; this is a fictitious interest that the texts have canceled by allowing women to hold significant positions in Islam (such as leading armies at times like Umm al-Mu'minin Aisha during the Battle of Jamal, for instance, even though we do not endorse rebellion, or her being appointed to the market oversight and judiciary, as Umar ibn al-Khattab appointed al-Shifa as a market overseer (i.e., a judge), but the point is that the Sharia did not prohibit that form due to "lack of intellect").
"And they said that the suitable is the interest. They said: the unqualified interest. This is akin to considering the type of description in the type of ruling. For whatever the description is that the ruling has come for, this description is an interest. And the mind does not see anything suitable except what it sees as an interest. Thus, all the causes are encompassed by the description of interest. Moreover, the interest exists in every ruling that the legislator has sought, and corruption exists in every ruling that the legislator has prohibited, and repelling corruption is an interest, so the interest becomes the description.
