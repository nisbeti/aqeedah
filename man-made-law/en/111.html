"Donating to churches that provide social services to Muslims (such as feeding the poor) is permissible, even if they practice heretical rituals or support homosexuality."

Sixth: Voting in hijab/niqab polls

Entity: Islam Web (Sheikh Al-Alim):

The fatwa: It is permissible to vote in Western forums in support of Islamic rituals, as it strengthens "public interest" with the public good.

Methodology: Reason assesses the benefit of "Muslims feeling cared for."

Flaw: The product of "intellectual interest" is not based on a detailed legal text, but rather on mental estimation. Does this fatwa have a basis in the Book or Sunnah? A legitimate doubt is raised.

Seventh: The Sharia swimsuit/swimming

Entity: The Egyptian Fatwa House (Dr. Mahmoud Shalabi):

The fatwa: If the swimsuit is covering according to Sharia, it is permissible, because preserving activity (health interest) is greater than the harm.

Methodology: Linking "need" and "interest" to the legal ruling.

Flaw: Is there a legal text that permits the swimsuit? Its ruling is linked to fulfilling a condition of the rationale (such as protecting modesty), but the methodology here assumes "interest" without a detailed text to rely on.
