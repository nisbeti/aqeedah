Thus, the Sharia has clarified that the command of the ruler is to be obeyed in organizing the permissible (mubah), and it has clarified that traveling on the roads is among the permissible, so this example has been addressed by the Sharia and its ruling has been made clear, so it is not correct for anyone to say that the Sharia did not address it.
As for their saying regarding matters without a text, know that we are commanded to follow the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) alone, as Allah, Exalted is He, says: ﴾Say, "If you love Allah, then follow me; Allah will love you"﴿, and the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) does not bring anything except from revelation ﴾And he does not speak from [his own] inclination. It is not but a revelation revealed﴿ ﴾Say, "I only warn you by revelation"﴿, so we are commanded to follow what has come by revelation, and its implication is that we are prohibited from following anything other than what has come by revelation, that is, from following anyone other than the Messenger. Therefore, the derived ruling that they claim has no text, and for which they have established a ruling without a legal evidence, is a means of introducing a matter into the religion from a path other than revelation, which is invalid, and this is sufficient to nullify what has been derived based on this understanding from the consideration of the Sharia.
And because the absence of a legal evidence regarding a matter indicates a deficiency in the Sharia, and it means that the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) did not clarify to the people what was revealed to them, and it contradicts His saying, Exalted is He: ﴾A clarification for all things﴿, and it contradicts the completion of the religion, and His saying: ﴾The right path has become distinct from the wrong﴿, so the absence of a matter from a legal ruling means that the right path has not been made clear, and that Allah's proof over His creation is not established in certain matters, which contradicts His saying: ﴾So that whoever would perish might perish by clear proof, and whoever would live might live by clear proof﴿. This is sufficient to refute them, because the ruling intended to be taken is a ruling of the Sharia, not a ruling of reason, so it is necessary for it to be considered from the Sharia that there is evidence indicating it, and that its source is revelation, and the legal ruling is the address of the Legislator concerning the actions of the servants, and not the address of reason, desires, or interests. Therefore, what does not come through the Legislator is not considered a legal ruling, ﴾Say, "I only warn you by revelation"﴿, and this is an exclusive formulation. And Allah, Exalted is He, says: ﴾Follow what has been revealed to you from your Lord, and do not follow other than Him allies. Little do you remember﴿ [Al-A'raf 3], so the position of His saying ﴾Follow what has been revealed to you﴿ is the position of the comprehensive separation from the limit, and the position of ﴾And do not follow﴿ is the position of the prohibitive separation in the limit, thus it is prohibited to follow the opinions of men and the legislations of humans, due to the explicit prohibition against following them. Therefore, any legislation must have a connection that indicates its issuance from revelation. And He, the Most High, said: ﴾The judgment is only for Allah. He has commanded that you not worship except Him. That is the correct religion, but most of the people do not know﴿, and Allah, Exalted is He, addressed His Prophet by saying: ﴾Indeed, We have sent down to you the Book in truth to judge between the people by what Allah has shown you﴿ and by saying: ﴾And We have sent down to you the Book in truth, confirming what was before it of the Scripture and safeguarding it. So judge between them by what Allah has revealed﴿. These verses restrict judgment to what Allah has revealed. If we know that what Allah has revealed is sufficient and comprehensive, we understand that there remains no room for judgment by other than what Allah has revealed, nor for taking other than what Allah has revealed as a judge over the actions of the servants.
