About the author: Thaer Ahmed Salama

To view the complete list of the author's works, please refer to the "Bibliographic Index" section below.

An author and researcher specializing in Islamic philosophy, epistemology, and political legitimacy, he is concerned with rooting Islamic thought on a solid critical methodology that combines the proof of reason and the guidance of revelation. He works on reshaping Islamic concepts in the face of contemporary deviations, whether resulting from the infiltration of Western thought or related to the disintegration of Islamic consciousness. He has authored works that focus on establishing belief on rational and scientific foundations, rooting the methodology of reasoning, exploring divine, cosmic, and historical laws, and clarifying the aspects of miraculousness in Islamic legislation.

Bibliographic Index:

Theory of knowledge and methods of thinking and reasoning: A foundational reference in regulating the methodology of reasoning, it clarifies how knowledge originated, the differences between Islamic and Greek methodologies, and deconstructs modernist perceptions of science and reason. The origin of the universe – A rational, scientific, and sensory guide to the existence of the Creator: It presents rational and scientific evidence that the origin of the universe is not the result of chance, but of precise design. It discusses the laws of physics, the evidence of care, and the precise cosmic constants to prove the existence of a wise Creator.

The origin of life – The miracle of the cell and evidence of the Creator's existence: An analytical journey that addresses the complexity of life from the first cell to humans, proving the impossibility of life arising by chance. It refutes materialistic and Darwinian propositions and establishes the teleology of divine design.
