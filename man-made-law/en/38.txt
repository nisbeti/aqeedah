Ignorance of the true nature of man and the purposes of his existence:
Summary:
The inability of reason to comprehend the purpose of human existence
The human intellect, with its subjective limitations, cannot determine the existential purpose of man: is it physical pleasure? Or spiritual elevation? Or worship of the Creator? Or something else? It requires divine guidance to understand this matter.
The contradiction of positivist philosophies due to ignorance of the true nature of man
Human thought has presented conflicting visions:
Capitalism has made man a consumerist individual.
Communism has confiscated his innate nature and neglected his spiritual freedom.
The result in both cases: deviation in legislation, and social and moral collapse.
Man is a composite being that can only be understood by his Creator
Man is not merely a body or a mind, but a complete entity that needs a balanced divine methodology to satisfy his needs, purify his soul, and direct his instincts.
Islamic law alone comprehends the composite nature of man
It takes into account:
The balance between permissible enjoyment and spiritual purification.
The right to work and wealth, coupled with zakat (almsgiving) and mutual support.
Worship and humility, without negating the vitality of social and economic life.
Achieving happiness through love of Allah and following His law
The purpose of man is not merely worldly benefit, but closeness to Allah, and embodying the attributes He loves, which instills tranquility in this world and salvation in the Hereafter.
