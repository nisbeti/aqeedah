Methodology of Juridical Ijtihad:
We can formulate the methodology of juridical ijtihad that the mujtahid (jurist) follows in deriving legal rulings as follows, focusing on the methodological tools used by the mujtahid to extract legal rulings, which go beyond merely having a direct detailed text, to precise mechanisms based on the implications of texts, general rules, causes, and objectives.
Introduction: On the Meaning of Ijtihad and Its Function
Ijtihad is the exertion of effort in deriving the legal ruling from its detailed evidences, with a preponderance of belief, within regulated methodological conditions. It is the means that ensures the activation of legal texts in response to the developments of life, within regulated inductive, linguistic, rational, and legal frameworks.
Linguistic Tools: The Implications of Words ((27)) on Meanings and the Role of Language in Deriving Rulings
A. Types of Implication:
The implication of conformity: the word indicates its complete meaning (like "human" = rational animal).
The implication of inclusion: the word indicates part of the meaning (like "human" = animal only).
The implication of commitment: the word indicates a necessary meaning (like "lion" = courage).
"The aspects of the implications of evidences: implication by arrangement, implication by concept, implication by necessity and requirement, and implication by rational meaning (from which analogy arises)" ((28)).
B. Basic Concepts in Usul al-Fiqh:
General and specific, absolute and restricted, ambiguous and clarified, apparent and textual, implied and stated, command and prohibition.
C. Utilizing Implication: Words are utilized through four methods: in terms of form (command and prohibition, general and specific, apparent and interpreted, and textual), in terms of content and concept (the evidence of speech, and the book of the concept), in terms of necessity, and in terms of rationality, which links implication to analogy and objectives.

(27) The implications of words on meanings are limited to three types: the implication of conformity, the implication of inclusion, and the implication of commitment. The implications of conformity and inclusion relate to the forms and structural patterns of sentences, while the implication of commitment relates to the speech and its indication and inclusion of causes, and to the content of the speech or the tone of the speech, which is the concept of agreement, or the evidence of speech, which is the concept of disagreement. The implication of commitment has five types: the first is the implication of necessity, the second is the indication of the word, the third is the hint and suggestion, the fourth is understanding the non-stated from the stated through the context of the speech and its intended meaning, and the fifth is the concept, which is called the evidence of speech. The implication of conformity is the implication of the word on the complete meaning for which the word is designated, like the implication of "human" on its meaning, which is the rational animal. The implication of inclusion is the implication of the word on part of the meaning for which the word is designated, like the implication of "human" on the animal or the rational. The implication of commitment is the implication of the word on a necessary meaning for which the word is designated.
