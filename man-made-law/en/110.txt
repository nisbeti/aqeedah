Third Case Study: Equal Inheritance Between Males and Females
Issued Fatwa: It is permissible in the West to divide inheritance equally between men and women.
Source: “Minority Jurisprudence Symposium,” Paris 2019, sponsored by the Union of Islamic Organizations in France (UOIF):
“If the Islamic rule of giving the male double the female share causes family discord or damages Islam’s image, then equal division is permissible.”
Contradiction with Shar‘ī Texts:
Qur’an: “Allah instructs you concerning your children: for the male, the share of two females.” (Al-Nisā’ 4:11)
Scholarly Consensus: Changing the shares of inheritance is among the gravest major sins. (Al-Nawawī, Al-Majmūʿ, 16/34)
Mechanism of Distortion:
Treating “social interest” (avoiding criticism from the West) as a valid basis for nullifying an explicit Qur’anic ruling.
Fourth Case Study: Endorsing Same-Sex Marriage Ceremonies
Issued Fatwa: A Muslim imam may participate in same-sex wedding ceremonies in Western churches.
Source: Islamic Society of North America (ISNA), Workshop 2022:
“If a same-sex couple invites a Muslim imam to offer a duʿāʾ at their wedding, there is no Shar‘ī objection, as it supports their Islamic identity.”
Contradiction with Shar‘ī Texts:
Qur’an: “Do you approach such immoral practices no one in the worlds has ever done before you?” (Al-Aʿrāf 7:80)
Hadith: “Allah has cursed the one who imitates the people of Lūṭ.” (Sahih al-Tirmidhī)
Mechanism of Distortion:
Using “cultural proximity” as a pretext to normalize grave sins.
