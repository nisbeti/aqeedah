Fifth: Contemporary Distorted Examples

The participation of Islamic movements in democratic governance: claiming that it "prevents the encroachment of secularists."

Response: Participation here is a legislative delegation, and merely entering this system is an entrenchment of it, not a necessity.

Deconstructing the Concept of "Encroachment":

The democratic system is based on "institutional balance," which makes participation in it an acknowledgment of its legitimacy rather than a resistance to it, according to the theory of "institutional legitimacy" by David Easton: "The participant in the system becomes its guardian, not its underminer."

A new real-world example: In Tunisia (2011-2021):

The participation of the Ennahda Movement in parliament did not prevent the passage of laws that contradict the Sharia (equality in inheritance, legalization of adoption for homosexuals).

Result: It transformed from a "reformist movement" to a "supporter of the status quo," according to the report by the International Institute for Strategic Studies (IISS 2022).
