The killing of the apostate, the rejection of cutting off the hand of the thief, the permissibility of usury, and other such widespread calamities—by this they judge the revelation itself, both the definitive (qat'i) and the speculative (dhanni), to please the infidel West!

"The sincere among them lean – as described by Dr. Muhammad Muhammad Hussein (may Allah have mercy on him) – with Islamic values to the utmost extent that the texts can bear towards Western values, and thus they fall into the traps devised by the West for individuals like them. In their effort to counter the accusation of rigidity that Westerners attach to the Sharia, they deviate to the farthest opposite in demonstrating what it entails in terms of flexibility of application, until they reach a level of laxity and the absence of identity and the essential components that make it suitable to be a tail to any system, subject to whims. Consequently, they end up nullifying the function of religion, because instead of rectifying the distortions of life with the texts of the Sharia, they manipulate the texts of the Sharia to justify the distortions of contemporary life." End of quote with slight modification(44).

These individuals did not limit themselves to placing the legal rulings in the context of their trial, but they went beyond that to the principles that have never been a subject of dispute among the ummah, in order to undermine their foundations and structure, leaving only the public interest (maslaha), custom (urf), tradition, and reality, all of which are not originally part of it.

Indeed, if these individuals had limited themselves to addressing rulings related to the new problems that people face, such as cloning, in vitro fertilization, insurance contracts, joint-stock companies, the use of nuclear weapons, and the use of medical resuscitation devices, and had employed the correct method of ijtihad (independent juristic reasoning) to arrive at the ruling of Allah, Exalted is He, on these matters and their likes, by referring their disputes back to the Book and the Sunnah, in accordance with His saying: ﴾ And whatever you disagree about, its ruling is with Allah. That is Allah, my Lord; in Him I have put my trust and to Him I turn ﴿, and His saying: ﴾ And if you dispute over anything, refer it to Allah and the Messenger, if you believe in Allah and the Last Day. That is better and more suitable for resolution ﴿, and His saying: ﴾ And We have sent down to you the Book as a clarification for all things and as guidance and mercy and good tidings for the Muslims ﴿, and His saying: ﴾ The judgment is only for Allah. He has commanded that you not worship except Him. That is the correct religion, but most people do not know ﴿, and others like them, it would have been better and more suitable for resolution, and we would have said that they truly want Muslims to adhere to the revelation. However, they did not do that; rather, they addressed established practical rulings and subjected them to criticism and rejection, replacing them with rulings that please the disbelieving West.

And how can we give a man twice the share of a woman in inheritance? And say that a woman is deficient in intellect and religion? Or that the times have evolved to the point where insurance has become a necessary aspect of life, so that a merchant does not lose goods worth tens of thousands of dinars due to flooding or fire, or that a man has to pay everything he has accumulated throughout his life for a heart surgery?
