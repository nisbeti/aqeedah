Legal necessity: "That which leads to the destruction of life, intellect, or religion if not acted upon" (Encyclopedia of Jurisprudential Concepts), such that there remains no option but to commit the prohibited act under the pressure of harm, and that the harm threatens one of the five objectives (maqasid) of the Sharia, and the prohibited is only permitted to the extent that it removes the harm, and it is not permissible to expand the allowance to what is unnecessary. (Kuwaiti Jurisprudential Encyclopedia)
