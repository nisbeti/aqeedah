The limitations of the mind in perceiving consequences and outcomes (temporal deficiency):
Summary:
The limitations of the mind in perceiving the consequences of actions and the outcomes of legislations.
The mind does not possess the necessary tools to know the future results of actions, nor does it have a comprehensive understanding of the outcomes, which limits its ability to enact sound legislations.
The mind is governed by prior information and tools that it cannot surpass.
The human mind does not operate in a vacuum; rather, it requires prior data and experiences, and it is incapable of forming concepts or legislations without them.
The mind is suitable for doctrinal reasoning, not practical legislation.
The mind can reason about major doctrinal issues, such as the existence of Allah, as it possesses the necessary tools to issue those judgments; however, it is not qualified to issue legislative rulings related to actions, consequences, and interests due to the inadequacy of its tools for such issues.
The absence of an absolute reference standard in estimating and prioritizing interests.
The mind is unable to determine which interests should take precedence when in conflict, whether they are material, moral, spiritual, or immediate benefits.
The confusion between reason and desire in the absence of reference.
When the mind is left to legislate without revelation, its decisions often turn into responses to whims and personal interests, rather than objective rational judgments.
Human legislation is contextually limited and rapidly changing.
Positive laws are often immediate, temporary, and subject to continuous modification due to their inability to encompass all aspects, which undermines their stability and permanence.
The cycle of "legal patching" in positive systems.
The frequent amendments in positive laws reveal their structural failure, leading to a loss of public trust and legal authority.
The permanence and absolute perfection of divine legislation.
Islamic law is derived from the All-Knowing, the Wise, and it has come with eternal principles and supreme objectives that make it suitable for all times and places, without the need for amendment or abrogation.
