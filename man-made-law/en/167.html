“You will surely follow the ways of those before you…” (Hadith)And today, such imitation often enters through this gateway of fabricated “maslahah.” Our Final Position in This Chapter

We say—emphatically and without hesitation—no, and a thousand times no, to turning maslahah into a super-legislator placed above the Qur’an and Sunnah. The Sharīʿah is sovereign, not subject to subjugation. It is authoritative, not subordinate. Any approach that gives precedence to current realities over revelation is, in truth, an attempt to twist the texts to conform to the dominant materialist civilization. This is an unacceptable intellectual defeatism.

And experience—both past and present—has shown that whoever compromises once in the name of maslahah will inevitably be pressured into further concessions, until he loses both his religion and his worldly dignity. But whoever remains patient upon the principles of the Sharīʿah may forgo an immediate material gain, yet he earns the pleasure of Allah and preserves the identity and unique character of the Muslim Ummah. By Allah, no people ever abandoned something for the sake of Allah except that He compensated them with something better.Let this be our creed, and let us live by it, so that we are not swept away by the mirage of imagined interests, which are in truth real and ruinous dangers.
