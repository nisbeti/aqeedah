"Indeed, you will follow the ways of those who came before you..." the hadith, and following their ways today often comes through this false door of "public interest (maslaha)."

The summary of our position in this chapter is: no and a thousand noes to turning "public interest" into a legislator above the Book and the Sunnah, for the Sharia is sovereign, not subservient, and it is dominant, not dominated. Any trend that places reality above it merely seeks, in truth, to twist the texts to conform to the prevailing material civilization, and this is a rejected intellectual defeat.

Experiences - even contemporary ones - have proven that whoever concedes once in the name of public interest will be compelled to concede more under pressure, until they lose both their worldly life and their religion. As for those who remain steadfast on the principle of the Sharia, they may lose an immediate material gain, but they earn the pleasure of Allah and preserve the identity and existence of the Ummah. By Allah, no people have ever left something for the sake of Allah except that Allah compensated them with something better. Let this be our creed and let us act upon it, so that we do not drift behind the mirage of illusory interests, which are in reality certain destruction.
