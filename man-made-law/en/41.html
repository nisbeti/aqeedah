The necessity for legislation to be stable and applicable to all times and places:

The divine law (sharia) was legislated to be the ultimate framework for human life, and it can only be so if it is:

• Stable in its foundations and principles.

• Broad and comprehensive in its application through disciplined ijtihad (independent juristic reasoning).

• Suitable for all times and places because it originates from the All-Knowing, the Wise.

Thus, the stability of legislation is the foundation of justice and the stability of society.

Legislation is linked to its higher objectives (maqasid al-sharia):

Justice, truth, and mercy cannot be achieved through arbitrary laws; rather, there must be genuine knowledge of the realities of matters.

Any legislation that does not prevent injustice or establish the truth is fundamentally flawed.

The overarching objectives, such as the preservation of religion, life, intellect, wealth, and honor, cannot be safeguarded by fluctuating human laws.

The issue of conflicting goals and interests:

The human mind cannot always balance conflicting values — such as security versus privacy or freedom, or public health versus freedom of choice — without falling into contradictions and subjective judgments.

These dilemmas produce contradictory laws that do not create a coherent system for social behavior, nor do they preserve the higher objectives and interests, but rather encroach upon one another to achieve some at the expense of others.

The need for legislative integration across various areas of life:

Islamic law provides a comprehensive system that unifies belief, ethics, worship, transactions, public order, and policies.

In contrast, positive legal systems suffer from contradictions between economic laws, civil liberties, criminal law, and family law, due to the fragmented vision of their drafters.

The rooting from the philosophy of law:

In legal jurisprudence, especially in the theory of "natural law," there is an acknowledgment that true justice must be derived from a source beyond humanity.

Even positive trends suffer from a lack of moral standards and acknowledge that the validity of law does not always mean its justice.
