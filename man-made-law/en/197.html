The Book of the Rulings of the Qur'an by Imam al-Shafi'i – We referred to it in the matter of istihsan (juridical preference) where he stated his famous phrase.

The Notification of the Signatories by Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya – A reference in the principles of fatwa (legal opinion). We supported from it the principle "Any benefit that contradicts the Sharia is a harm."

The sayings of scholars and imams of the schools of thought – We gathered them from various sources, including:

The Risala (Message) by al-Shafi'i (in condemning istihsan without evidence).

The I'tisam by al-Shatibi (the chapter on public interests).

The Muwafaqat by al-Shatibi – An exposition of the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia) and the ranks of necessities.

The writings of Ibn Taymiyya (Fatwas, Dar' Ta'arud) – We quoted from him that there is no contradiction between reason and authentic transmission.

The writings of Ibn Abd al-Barr (Jami' Bayaan al-'Ilm) – His statement regarding those who see innovation as good is that they have accused the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) of betrayal.

The Biographies of the Noble Figures – We extracted from it stances such as Malik's refusal to impose his school of thought on people, and Abu Hanifa's saying "They are men and we are men," and similar remarks.

Contemporary laws and documents:

The Tunisian Personal Status Code 1956 – We derived from it the text prohibiting and criminalizing polygamy.

Reports from the Tunisian Equality Committee 2018 – We quoted from them their arguments for equality in inheritance.

The Egyptian Khula Law 2000 – We referred to its effects.

International protocols – (CEDAW and others) we hinted at in the context of pressures to Westernize laws.

Social and economic statistics:

Divorce rates, crime, debts… from sources such as international reports and reliable news – We employed them for factual evidence (for example: the rise in theft crimes when the punishment is suspended).

Reports from financial organizations – To show the impact of usury on the economies of countries (such as public debt data).
