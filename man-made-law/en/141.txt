Disciplined legal reasoning (uṣūl al-fiqh)
This demands: Unwavering commitment to Sharʿī constants—no matter the pressure.
Strict regulation of the use of necessity, need, and maslahah—within the bounds of Islamic jurisprudence. Genuine efforts to develop viable Sharʿī alternatives, such as:
Real Islamic financing solutions, not affiliated with the capitalist system. Recognized Islamic arbitration tribunals. Effective Islamic marriage initiatives.
A clear distinction between lawful facilitation of Islam’s practice in the West and compromising divine law for comfort.
A courageous internal critique of the methodological deviations within minority jurisprudence institutions. Fiqh al-Aqalliyyāt will only fulfill its religious and societal purpose if it remains anchored in the certainties of revelation, offering a coherent jurisprudential vision that merges Sharʿī authenticity with practical realism—not one where rationalist utilitarianism erases immutable rulings.
