Give reason the authority to prefer worldly interests over legal evidence in cases of conflict, under the pretext that the purpose of religion is to achieve happiness in life, forgetting that true happiness lies in what pleases Allah, even if it contradicts people's desires.
Let us provide some real examples that illustrate these differences:
In Sharia, the punishment for theft is the cutting off of the hand, which serves a great interest for society by protecting people's wealth and deterring thieves. If reason were to judge it in isolation, it might argue that it is a harm to the thief by cutting off his hand. But Allah – the Wise, the All-Aware – knows that the interest of public security outweighs this, and that this punishment, despite its severity, is more merciful to society than the spread of theft. Meanwhile, some rationalists have viewed the punishment as "harsh and contrary to contemporary human rights"! Thus, they contradicted the text under the pretext of interest. Here we ask them: Is this truly an interest or merely a human desire influenced by certain Western cultural standards? Allah, Exalted is He, stated after mentioning the punishment for adultery: ﴾And let not pity withhold you in their case in a punishment prescribed by Allah…﴿, so that no philosopher comes along and says, "We should abandon flogging out of mercy for them"! True mercy lies in enforcing the punishment to purify them and prevent their harm to society.
Another example: The prohibition of usury (riba) is a clear text from the Qur'an aimed at cutting off the economic and social corruption represented by the oppression of the needy, the exploitation of the poor, and the concentration of wealth. This ruling is undoubtedly wise for those who reflect. A group of rationalists (since Muhammad Abduh and his student Rashid Rida) claimed: Banks have become the backbone of the economy, and the interest on loans is necessary for development, so there is no harm in "light" usury to stimulate investment! They permitted usury in the name of interest. The result was what we see today: the dominance of banks, the debt crisis, and the oppression of the usurious system even against entire countries. They approved with their limited intellect what Allah has prohibited, and thus fell into a greater corruption; and this is how Allah deals with those who disobey Him and follow their desires. The scholar Sheikh Ahmad Shakir confronted that fatwa, warning sternly: "Let the believer who advises himself beware of being deceived by Muhammad Abduh's fatwa permitting usury and calling it interest." This is a warning from a trusted scholar who recognized the danger of some muftis manipulating terms to undermine the certainties of religion.
Among their slogans is: "The change of rulings with the change of time and place cannot be denied." We have seen some say: "Inheritance rulings can be modified in our era to achieve equality between the sexes," and we have seen others propose: "The modern economy cannot function without bank interests, so it suffices to prohibit usury that is excessive (multiplying many times)," and some have even gone so far as to demand the abolition of criminal punishments such as retribution and fixed penalties, claiming that they "are no longer suitable" today!
