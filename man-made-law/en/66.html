Summary:

Al-Shatibi's conditions for the public interest transform it from a "rational opinion" into:

A revelation of a legal objective through induction.

An application of the comprehensive system of revelation.

A closure of the avenues towards independent legislation.

Thus, it remains a servant of revelation, not a competitor to it.

The public interest according to the Malikis: a legal reasoning regulated by revelation, not an independent rational foundation.

A systematic analysis of jurisprudential issues in the Maliki school highlights how the rulings based on public interest were derived from the texts of revelation and applied to realities, without establishing legislation from pure reason:

In this paragraph, we clearly reveal that the reliance of the Malikis on the public interest was not a departure from revelation or the establishment of an independent rational ruling, but rather an inductive reasoning that built a conception of the new reality and then applied to it what was suitable from the universals of the Sharia and its objectives, which are regulated by the texts of revelation. Thus, "the public interest" for them is not an intellectual authority, but a branch based on a text or objectives derived from it. We will demonstrate this by analyzing a number of jurisprudential issues that were determined by the Maliki imams through this committed methodology.

Clearly, and with the aim of shedding light on the Maliki methodology in ijtihad (independent juristic reasoning), the following examples show that when the Malikis used the public interest, they did not innovate rulings from a mere intellectual void, but rather they exerted effort based on the objectives of the Sharia and its principles grounded in revelation, applying these objectives to new realities, so that the public interest is understood in light of the texts, not in isolation from them. The purpose of presenting these examples is to demonstrate that legislation in the Maliki school—even in issues where there is no specific text—remained regulated by revelation, submissive to it, not independent of it.
