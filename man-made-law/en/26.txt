Axis three: Legislation and humanity, between rigidity and flexibility, stability and change:
Some people believe that the essence of humanity is based on change, and thus it is necessary for legislation to evolve and keep pace with the changes occurring in life. However, the truth is that the nature of humanity does not change; the reality and essence of humanity have remained the same from our father Adam until today and until the Day of Resurrection. The essence of humanity consists of its mentality, psychology, inclinations, and behaviors that it engages in to satisfy its instincts and organic needs, due to possessing vital energy, which is an unchanging force. Rather, humans need to organize the way they satisfy their vital energy and regulate it in the best possible manner that achieves satisfaction with certain characteristics and traits that are suitable for addressing those satisfactions and for building sound social relationships that ensure the management of affairs and the realization of interests. Such organization is characterized by stability, not change.
It cannot be that the satisfaction of the sexual instinct through deviance is correct at one time and wrong at another, nor can the possession of wealth through usury or monopolization be good at one time and bad at another. The discovery of cannabis (marijuana) or new types of alcoholic beverages does not make their consumption permissible, as they fall under the category of intoxicants and narcotics. The legislation that gives a ruling on the actions performed by humans to satisfy their vital energies, and provides a ruling on things in terms of permissibility and prohibition, is stable. It is legislation that pertains to human behavior as a human being, whether as an individual or as part of organized social relationships. If a problem arises in life or society, it is resolved in the same manner, as it is a problem related to vital energy and human gathering, and a stable legislation is provided to address it correctly, which does not change with time or place, in harmony with or based on the foundational principles of legislation and achieving the practical applied values, and the higher objectives and goals of society that all legislation aims to realize.
