Third: Arguments of those who claim that the Sharia (Islamic law) came to bring about benefits and avert harms

Classification of benefits into ranks:

Essential: such as the preservation of religion, life, intellect, lineage, and wealth.

Non-original essential: like the prohibition of a small amount of intoxicants.

Original needs: such as marrying off a minor by the guardian.

Non-original needs: like ensuring compatibility in marriage.

Enhancements: such as depriving slaves of the capacity to bear witness.

They conclude that these benefits are the reasons for legislating all rulings.

Evidence from the Qur'an and Hadith:

﴾And We have not sent you except as a mercy to the worlds﴿.

﴾And My mercy encompasses all things﴿.

The hadith "There should be neither harming nor reciprocating harm."

Claim of consensus:

They claim that the scholars are unanimous that the rulings of Allah are not devoid of wisdom and purpose.

General induction from the Sharia:

They have deduced that the details of the Sharia revolve around bringing about benefits and averting harms, especially in considering essentials, needs, and enhancements.

The assertion that the basis of customs is to pay attention to meanings:

They believe that customs (transactions) revolve around benefits in existence and non-existence, citing ﴾And in the law of retaliation is life﴿.

Making benefit a proof in the absence of text:

If there is no text, rulings are based on benefit if indicated by a general induction or reason enlightened by the Sharia.

Fourth: Refutation by the scholar Taqi al-Din al-Nabhani of these arguments

The invalidity of the reasoning of benefits from the perspective of intellect:

The fact that Allah is Wise or Just does not suffice to establish the reasoning of benefits in legislation, because:

These are discussions related to the science of theology, not to the principles of jurisprudence.

Belief in Allah's justice does not produce legal reasons for rulings.

The legal reason must be derived from the legal text, not from the attributes of Allah.
