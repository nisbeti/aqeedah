Public Interest and Condemnation of Desires – A radio article on the Hizb al-Tahrir website (2010). We benefited from it in highlighting the danger of using public interest (maslaha) to change rulings (where it mentioned the transformation of the forbidden into permissible through public interest, such as permitting usury and peace with the Zionists... etc.), and its useful definition of the disputed sources and their status.

Islamic Personality – Volume Three (Usul al-Fiqh) – By Sheikh Taqi al-Din al-Nabhani (1953). Theory of Knowledge and Methodologies of Thinking and Reasoning – By Thaer Ahmad Salama. We benefited from his discussion of the independence of rational knowledge and its limits. Ijtihad and Taqlid – A section from a book within the Islamic Personality (Volume 1). We used his principles: defining ijtihad as exerting effort to understand the ruling from the evidence, and the rules of taqlid. We relied on it in outlining the correct methodology of reasoning.
