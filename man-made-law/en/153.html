The "public interest" that they claim to protect is the first victim of this disruption. Leaving the appropriate legal punishment for sin has led to more sins and injustices, which is certainly against the intention of the Legislator.

Methodological mechanisms for deviation in the jurisprudence of balances

The first mechanism: the uncontrolled expansion of the concept of "necessity"

The correct approach: necessity is measured by its extent (it only permits the prohibited to avert certain destruction) - (al-Qarafi, al-Furuq: 4/40), what is feared to lead to destruction or loss, under conditions:

It must be certain (not imagined), it must be temporary (not permanent), and it must be averted with the least violation.

Deviation in the jurisprudence of balances: transforming "social needs" (such as coexistence or integration) into a necessity! And transforming "worldly interests" that do not rise to the level of necessities that meet the conditions into necessities that permit prohibitions.

Example: The fatwa of the European Council (2011) permitting work in usurious banks under the pretext of "the necessity of securing a livelihood."

Critique: Working in usury concerning its contracts is only a necessity in the face of the danger of dying from hunger (which is rare in the West), while providing luxury is not a necessity.

Example: The fatwa permitting prayer without covering the 'awra in the West:

The authority: The European Council for Fatwa, (2007), document: Decision No. (15/3) in the seminar "Facilitating Worship for Muslims in the West."

Justification: "The necessity of integration and not provoking the Western society."

Critique: Covering the 'awra is a condition for the validity of prayer according to the definitive text (Sahih Muslim: 510).

"Social hardship" is not a necessity (one can pray in homes or Islamic centers).

The second mechanism: employing "objectives" to undermine texts:

The correct approach: objectives are derived from texts and do not contradict them (al-Shatibi, al-Muwafaqat: 2/286).

Deviation in the jurisprudence of balances: prioritizing a "general objective" (such as preserving the ummah) over a specific text!

Example: Yusuf al-Qaradawi's claim in "The Jurisprudence of Jihad" (2009) prohibiting martyrdom operations under the pretext of "the objective of preserving the image of Islam," despite the existence of jihad texts that support it.

Example: Narrowing the concept of jihad under the pretext of "the objective of civilization": the authority: Yusuf al-Qaradawi, "The Jurisprudence of Jihad" (2009, vol. 2, p. 1200). Justification: "Achieving global peace is more important than jihad."

Critique: Jihad is an obligation according to the text (al-Bukhari: 2787) and its objectives are defined (defense - spreading Islam). The text cannot be annulled for a rational objective!
